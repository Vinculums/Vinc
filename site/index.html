Vinc

Mine what matters.

Vinc is an offline desktop app. Bring in your notes and documents, see them as a living graph, search by meaning, and hand curated packs to anyone.

Download View source

Intake

Drop in a folder of notes. Vinc mines concepts, records, and the ties between them.

Atlas

A 3D map of everything you know: domains as constellations, ideas as glass beads, ties as hairlines.

Packs

Select a neighborhood of the graph and export it as a portable pack. Import someone else's with one click.

Yours, on this machine. Vinc runs fully offline: no account, no keys, no telemetry. Your data lives in one folder you can copy, back up, or delete.
