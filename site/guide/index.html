01 · INTAKE

Just drag and drop. Vinc handles the rest.

Drop your notes and documents onto the window. Vinc analyzes their content and finds the key concepts and hidden connections. First time? The built-in sample pack shows you a finished knowledge map in one minute.

  • All analysis happens safely on your PC. Nothing is ever sent to an outside server.
  • When analysis finishes, press Open in Atlas to see the finished map right away.

02 · RECORDS

All your knowledge, neatly in one place.

Every document you add is organized here. Find anything instantly with live search, and pin what you open often. The leading icon (● pinned, ◐ tagged, ○ plain) shows each document's state at a glance.

  • Show in Atlas reveals exactly where a document sits on your knowledge map.
  • State is drawn with icon shapes, not colors, so it reads clearly in dark and light mode alike.

03 · ATLAS

The knowledge in your head, unfolded into a map.

Scattered memos link together into one great map of knowledge. Click a node for details, follow the lines between topics, and discover unexpected insights. Move freely between 2D and 3D views.

  • Node size follows the number of linked documents. Adjust layout and spacing from the panel on the right.
  • Shapes and the point color stay consistent, so a shared pack blends into your existing map without friction.

04 · SEARCH

Beyond keywords: search by meaning.

Hybrid search reads context as well as words (≡ keyword match, ≈ semantic match), pinpointing exactly the document you had in mind, fast and precisely.

  • Use domain: tag: and "exact phrase" operators to narrow the scope precisely.
  • No luck? Open the Atlas. A visual connection often hands you the answer.

05 · PACKS

Bundle, share, and merge your knowledge.

Cut out any part of your knowledge map and bundle it into a single Pack file. Set a topic and a reach, build a share pack, and hand it to a teammate. An imported pack blends right into your map.

  • Packs carry pure document content only. Your personal tags and pins are never shared.
  • Safe updates: re-importing an existing document never duplicates data. It simply refreshes to the latest content.

+ · CONNECT TO CLAUDE (MCP)

Ask Claude, answered from your own knowledge.

Vinc speaks MCP (Model Context Protocol), so Claude Desktop, Claude Code, and Claude on the web can read your knowledge map and answer with it — while every document stays on your machine. Connect once, then just ask.

  • Local setup is one command: vinc mcp install --client desktop (or --client code). Restart Claude and Vinc's tools appear.
  • Claude only sees the answers to the tools it calls. Your files are never uploaded.
Terminal — local setup
$ vinc mcp install --client desktop
✓ Registered Vinc with Claude Desktop.
