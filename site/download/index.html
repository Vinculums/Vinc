First launch

Vinc isn't code-signed yet, so your system may warn the first time you open it. It's safe — here's how.

macOS

  1. Drag Vinc into Applications, then try to open it.
  2. If it's blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Next to the Vinc message, click Open Anyway — or right-click the app and choose Open.

Windows

  1. Run the installer. SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC".
  2. Click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
