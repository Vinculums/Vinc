# aesthetic theories deep

## Position of this document

The *theoretical foundation* for the answer layers of "why beauty" §Q1, Q2. The *depth* stage of the Art domain — not an exhaustive survey of figures, but *the essence, structure, and interrelations of the theories*. It integrates East–West aesthetics with *equal authority* (consistent with the U-21 candidate).

## §1. Analytic aesthetics in earnest

### 1-1. The definition problem

"What is art?" — the *core problem* of analytic aesthetics. 3 principal answers:

#### Mimesis theory (Mimesis, Plato, Aristotle)

- **Plato** *Republic* X — art is *an imitation of an imitation of reality* (inferior). Yet he grants a *metaphysical beauty* (the Idea)
- **Aristotle** *Poetics* — tragedy is *imitation of action* (mimesis praxeos) + catharsis. He grants moral value
- **Limit**: modern abstract art, atonal music, non-representational poetry *all violate it*

#### Expression theory

- **Tolstoy** *What is Art?* 1898 — art = the artist's *emotional transmission* (sincere, infectious)
- **Croce** *Aesthetic* 1902 — art = *intuition*
- **Collingwood** *Principles of Art* 1938 — art = *emotional expression itself* (not communication)
- **Limit**: the artist's intent is *not externally verifiable*, conceptual art (Duchamp) has *no emotion*

#### Formalism

- **Hanslick** *Vom Musikalisch-Schönen* 1854 — music = *pure form* (no emotional expression)
- **Bell** *Art* 1914 — *significant form*
- **Greenberg** modernist painting — flatness, the essence of the medium
- **Limit**: *which* form is *significant* is circular

### 1-2. Institutional theory

- **Dickie** *Art and the Aesthetic* 1974 — art = an artifact *conferred status by the artworld*
- **Becker** *Art Worlds* 1982 — sociological refinement (art = a network of cooperative activity)
- **Merit**: can explain Duchamp's *Fountain*, Warhol's *Brillo Box*
- **Limit**: *circular* — *why* the artworld recognizes it is unanswered

### 1-3. Historical definition

- **Levinson** *Defining Art Historically* 1979 — art = that which is intended *to be regarded as prior art was*
- **Merit**: integrates the historical chain
- **Limit**: by what is *the first art* defined (infinite regress)

### 1-4. cluster concept, family resemblance

- **Wittgenstein** *Philosophical Investigations* (rejects a definition of "game," family resemblance)
- **Weitz** *The Role of Theory in Aesthetics* 1956 — art is an *open concept* (undefinable)
- **Gaut** *Art as a Cluster Concept* 2000 — 10 criteria (representation, expression, creativity, skill, emotion, intellectual challenge, complexity, tradition, intention, aesthetic value) — *partially satisfied*

→ **Contemporary analytic-aesthetics consensus**: definition is *not complete — a cluster/historical/institutional integration* is reasonable.

### 1-5. Wollheim, Walton, Goodman in earnest

- **Wollheim** *Art and its Objects* 1968 — art = a *temporally enduring object* (music = type, painting = token), *seeing-in* (seeing the depiction in a painting)
- **Walton** *Mimesis as Make-Believe* 1990 — art = a *prop in games of make-believe*. A painting is a *tool for a visual game*
- **Goodman** *Languages of Art* 1968 — art = a *symbol system* (notation, autographic, allographic). Music and poetry are allographic (exact copies via score/text possible), painting is autographic (forgeable)

→ **The 3 most influential of contemporary analytic aesthetics**. Walton's make-believe is a direct foundation for *VR/game aesthetics*.

## §2. Phenomenology of aesthetics

### 2-1. The Husserl, Heidegger foundation

- **Husserl** *Phenomenology* — the *intentionality* of consciousness, *epoché* (suspension of judgment)
- **Heidegger** *Der Ursprung des Kunstwerkes* 1935 — art = *the disclosure of the truth of Being* (alētheia). Through the peasant's shoes (Van Gogh) the *conflict of earth and world* is disclosed

### 2-2. Merleau-Ponty's embodied aesthetics

- **Merleau-Ponty** *Phénoménologie de la perception* 1945 — perception is *an activity of the body*. Seeing = touching
- **Cézanne's doubt** (*L'Œil et l'Esprit* 1964) — painting is *the world seeing itself*
- → a direct foundation for neuroaesthetics (Zeki, Gallese mirror-neuron *embodied cognition*)

### 2-3. Dufrenne's aesthetic experience

- **Dufrenne** *Phénoménologie de l'expérience esthétique* 1953 — aesthetic experience = the *self-manifestation of the object* + the *reception by the subject*. Both dimensions at once
- **aesthetic object vs ordinary object** — the aesthetic object *intimates* the *world itself*

### 2-4. Sartre, Bachelard, Ingarden

- **Sartre** *L'imaginaire* 1940 — aesthetic consciousness = the *freedom of the imagination*. A painting is *presence through absence*
- **Bachelard** *La poétique de l'espace* 1958 — space is transformed by *poetic imagination* (the attic, the cellar as *archetypes*)
- **Ingarden** *Das literarische Kunstwerk* 1931 — the literary work = *4 strata*: phoneme, meaning, representation, meta (a structuralist foundation)

### 2-5. Phenomenology vs analytic aesthetics

| Dimension | Analytic aesthetics | Phenomenology |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | definition, classification | experience, perception |
| Tools | logic, linguistic analysis | intuition, description |
| Object | the artwork, *what is it* | aesthetic experience, *how is it experienced* |
| Limit | the experiential dimension is underdeveloped | objective verification is difficult |

→ **The two schools are *mutually complementary*** — the analytic = external structure, the phenomenological = internal experience. The R-11 candidate's 4 axes (cognitive, evolutionary, cultural, social) can *integrate* them.

## §3. East–West aesthetic integration

Consistent with Dohun's learning philosophy §2-2 C, *preserving diverse perspectives equally*. It rejects the Western-aesthetics-first view and integrates with *equal East–West authority*.

### 3-1. Chinese aesthetics (integration of Confucianism, Daoism, Buddhism)

#### Confucian

- **Confucius** *論語* (Analects) — the unity of poetry (詩), rites (禮), and music (樂). Art = *cultivating virtue*
- **Xunzi** *樂論* (Discourse on Music) — music is *emotional regulation and social harmony*
- **Zhang Zai, Zhu Xi** — the aesthetics of *li 理* and *qi 氣* (the accord of the ideal and the real)

#### Daoist

- **Laozi** *道德經* (Dao De Jing) — the beauty of *無爲* (non-action) and *自然* (naturalness). Art is *union with the natural Dao*
- **Zhuangzi** *莊子* — *坐忘* (sitting in forgetfulness), *心齋* (fasting of the mind), *無心* (no-mind) — artistic creation is *non-action, naturalness*
- **Chinese landscape painting** (Northern Song, Guo Xi's *林泉高致*) — *三遠* (high, deep, level distance) perspective, a *fluid perspective* different from the West's
- **Calligraphy, literati painting ↔ the Daoist spirit** — the one-stroke (一筆揮之) = the flow of the Dao

#### Buddhist

- **Zen aesthetics** — *conciseness, spontaneity, impermanence (無常)* — a direct influence on Japanese *wabi-sabi*
- **空 sunyata** — the aesthetics of empty space and lingering resonance (餘韻)
- **頓悟 (sudden enlightenment), 漸修 (gradual cultivation)** — the two modes of aesthetic awakening

### 3-2. Japanese aesthetics (4 core concepts)

#### Wabi-Sabi (侘寂)

- **Sen no Rikyū**, 16th century — the tea ceremony (茶道), *the beauty of imperfection, simplicity, impermanence*
- **Andrew Juniper** *Wabi Sabi* — 7 principles (Kanso, Fukinsei, Shibumi, Shizen, Yugen, Datsuzoku, Seijaku)
- Mathematical parallel: *asymmetry* (violating group action) + *fractal matching of nature* (a Mandelbrot parallel)

#### Mono no aware (物の哀れ)

- *物の哀* (the pathos of things) — the aesthetic sensibility of the sorrow and impermanence of things
- **Motoori Norinaga**, 18th century — analysis of *Genji Monogatari*
- Contemporary: film (Ozu, Kore-eda), anime (Studio Ghibli) directly

#### Yūgen (幽玄)

- deep, mysterious beauty — *Noh 能 theater* (Zeami, 14th century)
- Musical parallel: *empty space, stillness* — an East–West parallel to Cage's *4'33"*

#### Iki (粋)

- **Kuki Shūzō** *いきの構造* 1930 — Edo aesthetics (refinement, freedom, resignation)
- Directly to fashion, design

### 3-3. Indian aesthetics (Rasa theory)

- **Bharata** *Nāṭyaśāstra* (200 BC–200 AD) — **9 Rasa (emotions)**: Sringara (love), Hasya (laughter), Karuna (sorrow), Raudra (anger), Vira (courage), Bhayanaka (fear), Bibhatsa (disgust), Adbhuta (wonder), Shanta (serenity)
- **Abhinavagupta**, 10th century — *rasa = emotion generalized into aesthetic experience*
- Contemporary: a direct foundation for Bollywood, Bharatanatyam, Indian classical music
- Mathematical parallel: *taxonomy* — coordinatizing aesthetic experience into a *9-emotion space*

### 3-4. Islamic aesthetics

- **Tawhid (oneness)** — all beauty reflects *the unity of God*
- **Arabesque, calligraphy, tessellation** — *non-representational beauty* (prohibition of figural representation → abstraction in earnest)
- **The Alhambra** — uses all 17 wallpaper groups (*reached empirically* before Fedorov 1891)
- **Music** — maqam, a raga parallel, mysticism (Sufi whirling dance)

### 3-5. East–West comparison matrix

| Dimension | West | East Asia |
|---|---|---|
| **Starting point** | objective classification, definition | subjective experience, cultivation |
| **Goal** | elucidating the essence of beauty | union with the aesthetic Dao |
| **Spatial expression** | one-point perspective (Alberti) | fluid three-distance (Guo Xi) |
| **Time** | eternity (Plato's Idea) | impermanence (mono no aware) |
| **Empty space** | filling (Renaissance) | emptying (Zen, landscape painting) |
| **Artificial/natural** | imitation (Aristotle mimesis) | union (Daoism) |
| **Music** | harmony, polyphony (Bach) | monophony, microtones (raga, gagaku) |
| **Mathematical parallel** | golden ratio, equal temperament, group theory | yin-yang (binary), 5 phases (oscillator), 8 trigrams (combinatorics) |

→ **The two traditions are *not mutually contradictory*** — *mutually complementary, translatable*. East–West integration is the *cultural* dimension of the R-11 candidate's 4 axes in earnest.

### 3-6. Contemporary East–West fusion

- **Cage** *4'33"* (1952) — Zen *soundlessness*, Suzuki's influence
- **Pollock, Tobey** action painting — Japanese calligraphy, Pacific influence
- **Rothko color field** — Eastern meditative influence
- **Sondheim *Pacific Overtures*** (1976) — Japanese kabuki + American musical
- **Contemporary minimalism** (Reich, Glass, LaMonte Young) — Indian raga, Balinese gamelan, African polyrhythm influence
- **Contemporary film** (Kurosawa, Wong Kar-wai, Kim Ki-duk) — Eastern aesthetics + Western film grammar

→ **Cross-field influence** (Dohun's most important ③) + **cross-cultural influence**, both dimensions proceeding at once.

## Matrix of open problems (the theories themselves)

Self-identified by this note:

1. **The *complete resolution* of the analytic-aesthetics definition problem** — even the cluster/historical/institutional integration is partial
2. **The *complete integration* of phenomenology ↔ analytic aesthetics** — the two schools are complementary OK, but a *formalized integration* is incomplete
3. **The *mathematical integration* of East–West aesthetics** — yin-yang, 5 phases, 8 trigrams vs group theory, category theory, an *exact mapping* incomplete
4. **The *neuroscientific foundation* of Zen *no-mind* and Daoist *sitting in forgetfulness*** — Friston free energy, Bayesian partial parallels
5. **The *cultural invariance* of the 9 Rasa emotions** — India-specific vs universal, unresolved
6. **The influence of Islamic non-representational aesthetics on *Western abstract aesthetics*** — Alhambra → Escher, Mondrian, a partial answer
7. **The *authenticity vs appropriation* of contemporary East–West fusion** — appropriation vs respect, unresolved
8. **The *mathematical refinement* of wabi-sabi** — a quantitative measure of asymmetry + impermanence, incomplete
9. **Translatability** — the *comparability* of aesthetic concepts *untranslatable into English*, such as *Yūgen*, *mono no aware*
10. **Meta-aesthetics** (the possibility of aesthetics itself) — why *aesthetics is possible in both East and West* is unanswered
