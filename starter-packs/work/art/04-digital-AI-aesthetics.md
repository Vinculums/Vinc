# digital AI aesthetics

## Position of this document

The *21st-century full-fledged form* of "Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard" §4, Baudrillard's simulacrum. AI and digital media *transform aesthetics itself*. The *social dimension* of the R-11 4 axes (cognitive, evolutionary, cultural, social) in earnest + MM-13 (cross-field influence) automated by AI.

## §1. New media theory — Manovich, Bolter, Murray

### 1-α — Manovich *The Language of New Media* 2001

- **5 principles**: numerical representation, modularity, automation, variability, cultural transcoding
- film → digital = *database aesthetics*
- the evolution matrix *cinema → game, VR, web*

### 1-β — Bolter–Grusin *Remediation* 1999

- *remediation* — a new medium *re-mediates* prior media
- *immediacy* (immersion) vs *hypermediacy* (awareness of the medium) — two impulses
- VR, AR = the *extreme of immediacy*

### 1-c — Janet Murray *Hamlet on the Holodeck* 1997

- digital narrative = *procedural, participatory, spatial, encyclopedic*
- foundation for interactive fiction, games, VR

## §2. NFT, blockchain art

### 2-α — NFT in earnest (2017–)

- CryptoPunks 2017, Beeple's *Everydays* $69M (2021, Christie's)
- *certificate of ownership* — token = ownership, content ≠ token
- Ethereum, Solana, Polygon standards (ERC-721, ERC-1155)

### 2-β — The 21st-century revival of Walter Benjamin's aura

- the age of digital reproduction → NFT = an *artificial aura*
- blockchain = *trust without authority* — replacing museums and dealers
- Limits: environmental cost (PoW), market bubble, *ownership ≠ access*

### 2-c — Unresolved — *genuine value*

- Beeple's $69M = *speculation vs genuine aesthetic value*
- From 2022, the NFT market lost 90% of its value — the bubble verified
- Yet the *meta-mechanism of digital ownership* is here in earnest (music, games, metaverse)

## §3. VR, AR, spatial computing

### 3-α — VR in earnest — Oculus 2016, Apple Vision Pro 2024

- *immersion*, *presence*, *embodiment* — directly Merleau-Ponty's embodied aesthetics
- 360° spatial art — Marina Abramović, teamLab, Olafur Eliasson
- Limits: motion sickness, high cost, isolation, *social presence* unresolved

### 3-β — AR — Snapchat, Pokémon Go, Apple ARKit

- *layer on reality* — Bolter–Grusin remediation in earnest
- Art application: Banksy AR, Jeff Koons AR

### 3-c — Spatial computing — Apple Vision Pro 2024

- VR + AR integration + AI = the *21st-century medium*
- Aesthetic open problem: *the essence of the physical–virtual boundary*

## §4. Generative AI art in earnest (2022–)

### 4-α — Technological evolution

- 2014 GAN (Goodfellow) → 2015 DeepDream → 2017 Style Transfer → 2021 CLIP, DALL-E → **2022 Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, DALL-E 2** → 2024 Sora, Veo, Flux
- *text-to-image*, *text-to-video*, *text-to-music* (MusicLM, Suno, Udio)

### 4-β — The challenge to authorship

- Who is the author? — the prompt writer, the AI, the artists in the training data
- The US Copyright Office (2023): no protection for AI-only works — a *human authorship* requirement
- *Zarya of the Dawn* (2023) — partial protection (text OK, images no)
- Getty vs Stability AI (2023), NYT vs OpenAI (2023) — training-data disputes

### 4-c — Does AI pass Boden's 3 creativities?

Boden's classification in "why beauty" §Q3-γ:
- **Combinatorial** ✅ AI passes (combining the existing)
- **Exploratory** ✅ AI passes (exploring within a style)
- **Transformational** ⚠️ *contested* — can AI *transform the style itself*?
  - Option 1: AI only *interpolates training data* — not transformational
  - Option 2: AI emergent properties generate a *genuinely new style* — transformational is possible
  - *Empirically unresolved*

### 4-d — Cultural bias in earnest

- LAION-5B training data — English/Western dominant
- "beautiful woman" → Western appearance, white, thin
- "temple" → Western styles dominant
- *data colonialism* (Crawford, *Atlas of AI* 2021)
- the *decolonizing AI aesthetics* movement

## §5. Post-human, post-Anthropocene aesthetics

### 5-α — Donna Haraway *Cyborg Manifesto* 1985

- *dissolving the boundaries* of human, machine, animal
- *companion species* — non-human actors
- AI art = post-human aesthetics in earnest

### 5-β — Object-Oriented Ontology (OOO)

- Graham Harman, Levi Bryant, Ian Bogost
- all objects *exist equally* — human, machine, stone, image
- Can AI-generated works have *agency*?

### 5-c — Anthropocene, climate art

- the naming of the *Anthropocene* (Crutzen 2000)
- climate aesthetics, *planetary aesthetics*
- Pierre Huyghe, Olafur Eliasson, Hito Steyerl

### 5-d — Hito Steyerl *In Free Fall* 2011

- the digital image, the aesthetics of the *poor image*
- combining AI, surveillance, capital, and art

## §6. Internet aesthetics, meme, vaporwave

### 6-α — Internet-native art

- Net art (1990s), post-Internet art (2010s)
- *meme* = 21st-century folk art
- Vaporwave, Seapunk, Aesthetic — digital aesthetic movements

### 6-β — TikTok, Instagram, Reels — the 30-second aesthetic

- short-form video = *condensed aesthetic*
- algorithmic recommendation = *21st-century canonical power*
- *FYP* (For You Page) = a personalized canon

### 6-c — The power of algorithmic curation

- TikTok, YouTube, Spotify, Netflix algorithms *shape aesthetic taste*
- *filter bubble*, *echo chamber* — Pariser 2011
- music hit prediction, literary recommendation, visual trending

## §7. The full-fledged challenge to authorship

### 7-α — Barthes 1967, *the death of the author*, in earnest in the 21st century

- The AI era = the *dispersal* of the author in earnest
- Who is the author?: the prompt, the AI model, the original authors of the training data, the platform

### 7-β — Distributed authorship models

- collaboration (Wikipedia, open source)
- user-generated (TikTok, UGC)
- AI-assisted (Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude)
- AI-only (Stable Diffusion automatic)
- → a *4-dimensional spectrum*

### 7-c — Law and ethics unresolved

- copyright law (1976 US, 1957 Korea) presupposes a *human author*
- legal *revision in progress* for the AI era (EU AI Act 2024, US NO FAKES Act)
- *opt-out* (exclusion from training data) vs *opt-in* — unresolved
