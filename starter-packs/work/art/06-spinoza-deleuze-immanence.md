## Position of this document

Building on "post-internet aesthetics" §1 (new materialism) + §2 (affect). Spinoza-Deleuze in earnest — *the immanence of the plane* and *the philosophy of difference* as the *common foundation* of 21st-century affect · new materialism · post-human aesthetics.

## §1. Spinoza — Ethics · conatus · affect

### 1-α — Substance · Attribute · Mode

- **Spinoza** *Ethica Ordine Geometrico Demonstrata* 1677 (posthumous)
- **Substance** = God = Nature = *the sole substance* (Deus sive Natura)
- **Attribute** = the essence of substance (Extension · Thought, the 2 knowable by human cognition)
- **Mode** = a modification of an attribute (every individual thing)
- → *immanence* — no transcendence · immanence acting at the same time

### 1-β — Conatus

- every mode's *desire to persist in its own being*
- the human being = *the conscious expression of conatus*
- a precursor to Nietzsche's *will to power*

### 1-γ — Affect (affectus · affectio)

- *affectus* = a change in the body's power of acting (joy · sadness etc. as the *basics*)
- *affectio* = the trace of an external action
- *active affect* vs *passive affect* — self-caused vs externally caused
- → the direct foundation of Deleuze's affect ("post-internet aesthetics" §2)

### 1-δ — Geometric method

- definitions · axioms · propositions · proofs — parallel to Euclid
- a mathematical parallel: Math 01 §Act 1, formal proof
- limits: *too formal* · *stylistic difficulty*

## §2. Deleuze — Difference · Repetition · the immanence of the plane

### 2-α — *Différence et Répétition* 1968

- **Difference itself** — a rejection of the priority of identity (Plato · Hegel)
- difference *generating identity* — the *opposite* of Hegel
- *repetition without resemblance* — always repeating *differently*
- influence: art = the production of difference in earnest

### 2-β — *Logique du sens* 1969

- *sense* = event · surface · *event*
- an analysis of Lewis Carroll's *Alice* — paradox · boundary

### 2-γ — the plane of immanence

- the *plane* on which concepts function
- a rejection of transcendence — everything on the *same plane*
- influence: the direct foundation of new materialism (Bennett · Barad)

### 2-δ — *Mille Plateaux* (with Guattari 1980)

- *rhizome* — non-hierarchical · multiple entryways · decentralized
- vs *arbor* (the tree) — hierarchical · centralized
- influence: the aesthetics of the Internet · networks · social media in earnest

## §3. Deleuze-Guattari — becoming · rhizome · assemblage

### 3-α — *L'Anti-Œdipe* 1972

- a critique of psychoanalysis (Freud · Lacan)
- *desiring-machine* — desire is *productive* (a rejection of lack)
- *body without organs* (citing Artaud)

### 3-β — *Mille Plateaux* — rhizome · assemblage · becoming

- **rhizome** = a non-hierarchical network (potato · sweet potato vs the tree)
- **assemblage** = a *temporary combination* — human · non-human · matter · language
- **becoming** (devenir) — *becoming* · *transformation* (becoming-animal · becoming-woman · becoming-imperceptible)
- *deterritorialization · reterritorialization*

### 3-γ — Nomadology · minor literature

- *nomadic thought* — the sedentary (arbor) vs the nomad (rhizome)
- *minor literature* (the 1975 analysis of Kafka) — minor writers within a major language
- → directly to post-colonial · queer · trans aesthetics

### 3-δ — Rhizome ↔ mathematical parallel

- **rhizome** = *graph without root* (Math 03 · 04 · 11)
- **assemblage** = *colimit* (category theory)
- **becoming** = *flow* · *differential* (Math 02 · 06)
- **deterritorialization** = *boundary dissolution* (topology)

## §4. Deleuze's film theory — *Cinéma 1 · 2*

### 4-α — *Cinéma 1: L'image-mouvement* 1983

- film = *philosophy with images*
- built on Bergson — *movement-image*
- *perception-image* · *affection-image* · *action-image*
- traditional Hollywood · Eisenstein

### 4-β — *Cinéma 2: L'image-temps* 1985

- after WWII — the *time-image* in earnest
- Italian neorealism (Rossellini · De Sica)
- the French New Wave (Godard · Truffaut)
- Japan (Ozu · Kurosawa · Mizoguchi)
- the direct expression of *time itself*

### 4-γ — *crystal-image* · *virtual · actual*

- the crystal-image = the virtual and the real *operating at the same time*
- a parallel to Borges's labyrinth ("case applications 3" §3)

### 4-δ — influence

- contemporary film theory (Rodowick · Marrati · Pisters)
- the aesthetics of digital film · VR (the nature of time in earnest)
