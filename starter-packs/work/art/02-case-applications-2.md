## §1. Beethoven's Late String Quartets (1825–1826)

### 1-α — The works' structure

- **Op. 127·130·131·132·133 (Grosse Fuge)·135** + the final cavatina
- *late style* — the direct foundation of Adorno's *Spätstil* analysis
- *fragmentary·unresolved·boundary-pushing*

### 1-β — Mathematical analysis

- **Op. 131 in C# minor**: 7 movements *played continuously* (no pause) — violating the traditional 4 movements
- *cyclic form* — thematic repetition·transformation (a $\mathbb{Z}_7$ period)
- **Grosse Fuge Op. 133** — *grand counterpoint*, the extreme of the fugal group action (the successor to Bach's *Art of Fugue* + its limit)

### 1-γ — Adorno's *late style* theory

- *non-identity*·*broken*·*against synthesis*
- rejection of synthesis (Hegel) — the *preservation* of contradiction
- → Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard §2 Adorno directly

### 1-δ — Neuroaesthetics

- Listener response to late style: *initial rejection·aesthetic recognition after repetition*
- Friston's free energy: *surprise + compression after learning*
- Position on Berlyne's inverted-U: *the far end of complexity* (above popular preference) — a challenging aesthetic

### 1-ε — Cross-field influence (Q3)

- Beethoven → Schoenberg·Bartók·Shostakovich·Carter (music)
- → the late Adorno's *Aesthetic Theory* (theory)
- → Said's *On Late Style* 2006 (post-colonial criticism)

## §2. Cézanne's Late Mont Sainte-Victoire (1902–1906)

### 2-α — The works·technique

- 60+ works of the same mountain (1882–1906)
- Late period: *the essence of things + structure* — the starting point of cubism
- *brush stroke as building block* — passage·constructive stroke

### 2-β — Mathematical analysis

- *multiple viewpoints* — a parallel to the Eastern "three distances" 三遠 (not intended, a parallel in outcome)
- *plane construction* — painting = the plane *piece by piece* (the stylistic foundation of cubism)
- color = *space + form simultaneously* — a parallel to the Math 02 linear-algebra matrix

### 2-γ — Phenomenological aesthetics (Merleau-Ponty's *L'Œil et l'Esprit*)

- Cézanne = *the world seeing itself*
- painting = the expression of *perception itself*
- → aesthetic theories deep §2-2 directly

### 2-δ — Cross-field influence

- Cézanne → Picasso·Braque → cubism → the *whole* of modernism (painting)
- → the multiple viewpoints of Joyce's *Ulysses*·Eliot's *Waste Land* (literature)
- → the polyrhythm of Stravinsky's *Rite of Spring* (music)

### 2-ε — cross-cultural

- The influence of Japanese ukiyo-e (Japonisme 1860–1900)
- → Cézanne → cubism → the influence of African masks (Picasso's *Demoiselles*)

## §3. Joyce's *Ulysses* (1922)

### 3-α — The work's structure

- 18 episodes·750+ pages·a single Dublin day, June 16, 1904
- Each episode in a *different style*: catechism·newspaper·musical fugue·stream of consciousness, etc.

### 3-β — The style matrix

| Episode | Style | Mathematical parallel |
|---|---|---|
| 7 *Aeolus* | newspaper headline | a group of fragments + a headline function |
| 11 *Sirens* | musical fugue (fuga per canonem) | a direct parallel to the Bach fugue (Math 03 group action) |
| 14 *Oxen of the Sun* | the evolution of English prose (Old English → modern) | a time-ordered sequence |
| 17 *Ithaca* | catechism (Q&A) | formal logic (Math 01) |
| 18 *Penelope* | stream of consciousness (8 sentences, almost no punctuation) | Markov chain text·a probabilistic sequence |

→ *Each episode = a different style = a different mathematical structure*. **Modernism = the rejection of a single style, the integration of multiple styles**.

### 3-γ — The Homer *Odyssey* parallel

- Joyce's intent: each episode *parallels a chapter of the Odyssey*
- The parallel itself is *cross-cultural cross-temporal* (Greece 8th century BC → 1904 Dublin → published 1922)

### 3-δ — Cross-field influence (Q3)

- *Ulysses* = *absorbing all of* the musical fugue + painterly cubism + lyric poetry + mythic epic
- → *every successor* in modernist literature (Woolf·Faulkner·Beckett)
- → the influence of stream of consciousness on film (Bergman·Antonioni·Tarkovsky)

### 3-ε — Neuroaesthetics

- *stream of consciousness* = an attempt to represent real-time prefrontal cortex activity
- The extreme of the reader's cognitive load (the far end of Berlyne's inverted-U)
- *The value of re-reading* — not one-time reading, multiple readings in earnest

## §4. The Song Peach Blossom Spring (桃源圖) — Eastern Ideal-Land Painting

### 4-α — The works·tradition

- **Tao Yuanming** 陶淵明 *Peach Blossom Spring* 桃花源記 (late 4th century, Eastern Jin) — the tale of the peach-blossom utopia
- Painted by many artists of the Song·Yuan·Ming·Qing: Zhao Boju 趙伯駒 (Southern Song)·Zhao Yuan 趙原 (Yuan)·Qiu Ying 仇英 (Ming)·Wang Hui 王翬 (Qing)
- This note analyzes *the tradition itself* (not a survey of artists)

### 4-β — Landscape painting's "three distances" 三遠 + the ideal-land meta

- The "three distances" 三遠 (high 高遠·deep 深遠·level 平遠) — the same as Guo Xi in case applications §4
- The ideal land = *the boundary between reality ↔ unreality* — *visualizing the unreal* through painting
- The handscroll format — *a medium integrating time + space* (a parallel to film)

### 4-γ — Mathematical parallel

- The handscroll = *the sum of 1-dimensional space → 2-dimensional painting → 3-dimensional landscape → 4-dimensional time* — *the integration of space and time*
- Film parallel: 24 fps × time = the unrolling speed of the handscroll
- The Eastern manifold parallel: 3 charts (三遠) + a time atlas

### 4-δ — Daoist aesthetics

- The peach-blossom utopia = *union with the Dao* — the rejection of the artificial vs the natural
- *wu wei* 無爲 — no artificial effort · *ziran* 自然
- Landscape painting = *an act of self-cultivation* (the same Dao as calligraphy·the way of tea)

### 4-ε — Cross-field influence + cross-cultural

- Korea (An Gyeon's *Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land* 夢遊桃源圖 1447·Goryeo·Joseon)·Japan (Sesshū 雪舟 *Mountain Landscape*)
- → the 20th-century West (the influence of Eastern meditation on Pollock·Rothko)
- → modern film (Wong Kar-wai's *In the Mood for Love*·the ideal land of Tarkovsky's *Stalker*)
