# east-west-aesthetics-comparison

## 0. Declaration of Self-Critique

This note is the **meta-comparison note of the 5 civilizations** — the most dangerous self-critique:
- **Comparison itself is a product of Western comparative aesthetics** (Hegel, Comparative Religion, Comparative Literature) — the comparative frame risks Western reduction
- **5 civilizations = a simplification** — the West, India, Japan, East Asia (China, Korea), and Africa each have *internal diversity and historical change* — this note is an *attempt at present-moment integration*
- **The "East" vs. "West" dichotomy itself risks Orientalism** (Said) — this note uses it *only as a comparative tool*, in full awareness of this
- **The non-Western ≠ homogeneous** — India ≠ Japan ≠ China ≠ Korea ≠ Africa — this note's comparison covers *both commonalities and differences*

→ **Only the West started from separation** — and this is not a *historical fact* but the product of *the West's own field-classification system* (Plato's classification of poetry, music, painting; Aristotle's classification of genres). The non-Western differs in the *classification itself*.

### Dimension 2: The relation of beauty to truth, religion, and the everyday

| Civilization | Relation | Core |
|---|---|---|
| The West | *separation* (Kant's autonomy, 1790–) | aesthetic disinterestedness — separated from truth, morality, and utility. Adorno's autonomy at the apex. Bourdieu's critique of bourgeois social capital |
| **India** | integration (rasa = an instrument of dharma and liberation) | art = the fifth Veda — no separation of religion, morality, and beauty |
| **Japan** | integration (the tea ceremony and wabi-sabi = instruments of the everyday) | the tea ceremony, ikebana, the garden, calligraphy = everyday enlightenment |
| **East Asia** | integration (qiyun = cosmos + beauty; calligraphy = morality + beauty) | the unity of poetry, calligraphy, and painting; the integration of Confucian, Buddhist, and Daoist aesthetics |
| **Africa** | integration (ritual = beauty + religion + society) | separation itself is meaningless |

→ **Western autonomy vs. non-Western integration** = an essential divergence. **However**, the 21st-century digital/AI age accelerates both *the crisis of Western autonomy* + *the recovery of non-Western integration*.

### Dimension 3: The conception of time

| Civilization | Conception of time |
|---|---|

| East Asia | cyclical + dynastic + the time of the poetic realm (詩境) |
| Africa | sankofa (return) + ancestral time |

→ **The Western linear vs. the non-Western cyclical, seasonal, returning** — recognizing the *Western-reduction risk* of applying evolutionary aesthetics and evolutionism.

### Dimension 4: The individual vs. the community

| Civilization | Cognitive unit |
|---|---|
| The West | *the individual* (Kant's universal = the individual's cognitive faculties / Wollheim's seeing-in as individual) |

→ **The individual vs. the community** — Bourdieu's habitus and Becker's art world (sociology-of-art) = on the communal side — is Western sociology attempting to recover the *non-Western communal aesthetic*?

### Dimension 5: Aesthetic distance

| Civilization | Distance model |

→ **Distance vs. participation** = the West + India/Japan/East Asia (distance) vs. Africa and the diaspora (participation). Hip-hop, jazz, and candomblé at the apex of direct participation.

### Dimension 6: The attempt at classification vs. non-classification
