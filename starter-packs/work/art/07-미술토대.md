## ② Art Theory Foundations

### Color
- **3 dimensions**: hue, value, chroma — Munsell (1905)
- **Primaries**: light RGB (additive) vs. pigment CMY (subtractive)
- **Complementary, analogous, contrast**: Goethe's *Zur Farbenlehre* (1810), Itten's *Art of Color* (Bauhaus, 1961)
- **Color constancy**: Land's *Retinex theory* — a direct input to Vermeer and Impressionism (recursion to Zeki's V4, neuroaesthetics)

### Composition
- **The golden ratio** (1.618) — Vitruvius, Da Vinci
- **The rule of thirds**, symmetric and asymmetric balance
- **Eye flow, center, negative space** (the Chinese *qiyun shengdong* (氣韻生動) — self-critiqued as absent from this note)
- **Perspective**:
  - Linear perspective (Brunelleschi 1413, Alberti's *De pictura* 1435 — the Renaissance)
  - Atmospheric perspective (Da Vinci)
  - Non-Western perspective: the Chinese landscape *three distances* (high, deep, level — 高·深·平遠) — multiple viewpoints / Japanese ukiyo-e flatness → influence on Impressionism

### Medium and Technique
- **Painting**: fresco, tempera, oil (Van Eyck, 1430s), acrylic (1934), digital
- **Sculpture**: clay, bronze, marble, metal, installation
- **Printmaking**: woodcut, engraving, lithography, silkscreen
- **Photography** (1839–): Daguerre, Talbot — the crisis of painting's autonomy (Greenberg)
- **Moving image / film**: Lumière (1895), Eisenstein, Brakhage
- **Digital**: Photoshop (1990), NFT (2014–), generative AI (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, 2022)
