# aesthetics

## ① Definition and Core Concepts

**Aesthetics** is the branch of philosophy dealing with *beauty·art·sensory cognition*. The etymology is the Greek *aisthēsis* (sense·sensory perception). The discipline was named in 1750 with **Baumgarten's** *Aesthetica* — but *the problem itself* dates from Plato.

The essential question of aesthetics is **"why do humans define beauty + by what criterion do we call something beauty or art"** (exactly matching Dohun's core meta-questions 1·2). This note *preserves as equals* the answers of 6 figures (U-21 candidate — no ranking of superiority; the diversity itself is the essence).

**The key fork — objective vs subjective**:
- The objectivist camp (beauty is in the object): Plato·parts of Aristotle
- The subjective-universal camp (beauty is in cognition + universal): Kant
- The historicist camp (beauty is the expression of an age·a spirit): Hegel·Adorno
- The vitalist camp (beauty is the affirmation of life): Nietzsche

## ③ 🔴 Simultaneous 4-Axis Analysis (the core of the R-11 candidate — the first full application)

### Axis 1 — Cognition (why do humans perceive beauty?)

**Plato**: the perception of beauty = the *recollection (anamnesis)* of the Idea. The soul recalls, in sensory objects, the Idea it saw in a former life (*Phaedrus* 250d). → beauty depends on *the soul's cognitive capacity*.

**Aristotle**: the perception of beauty = the perception of *form (eidos)*. The human intellect grasps unity·balance·appropriate magnitude at a glance. *Poetics* ch. 7: "what is excessively small or large cannot be beautiful" — the cognitive limit is the criterion of beauty.

**Kant**: the *subjective universality* of aesthetic judgment — the assumption that all humans share the same cognitive faculties (the free play of understanding + imagination). *Critique of Judgment* §22 "common sense" (sensus communis). → **the universality of cognition guarantees the universality of beauty**.

**Hegel**: the perception of beauty = the event of Spirit (Geist) recognizing itself in the sensuous. Cognition is itself the self-movement of Spirit.

**Nietzsche**: the perception of beauty = physiological — *rapture (Rausch)* is the precondition of the aesthetic state (*Twilight of the Idols*). → cognition at the bodily·physiological level.

**Adorno**: the perception of beauty = negative cognition — genuine beauty makes one perceive the *non-identity of social contradiction*. Not immediate pleasure.

> **🧠 Axis decision memo (cognition)**: Kant's "subjective universality" connects directly to the *common neural circuits* hypothesis of later neuroaesthetics (Zeki·Ramachandran). Plato·Aristotle offer *metaphysical cognition*, Kant the *universality of cognitive faculties*, Nietzsche *physiological cognition*, Adorno *negative cognition* — even on the cognition dimension alone, 4 essentially different answers coexist.

### Axis 2 — Evolution (the evolutionary origin of the sense of beauty)

This note is centered on the history of aesthetics — evolutionary aesthetics gets its depth in the advanced field (Foundations 3 neuroaesthetics + subsequent evolutionary psychology). **This note is a preliminary memo**.

**Plato·Aristotle**: no concept of evolution (BC). Yet Aristotle explicitly names the mimesis instinct as *human nature* — "humans learn from childhood through imitation" (*Poetics* ch. 4). → **read in modern terms, a hypothesis on the evolutionary origin of the imitative capacity**.

**Kant**: no concept of evolution (1790). Yet the assumption of *common cognitive faculties* is compatible with later evolutionary universality hypotheses.

**Hegel**: the *historical evolution* of Spirit — but no natural evolution. The dialectical evolution of Spirit.

**Nietzsche**: *Genealogy of Morals* — a *genealogy* of morality·the sense of beauty (not biological ≠ yet naturalistic). The *psycho-physiological evolution* of slave morality vs master morality. → the first attempt at an evolutionary aesthetics after Darwin.

**Adorno**: rejection of evolution — beauty is *socio-historical*. Yet Freud's theory of the drives is partially accepted.

→ **Full evolutionary aesthetics (Darwin's sexual selection·Pinker·Dissanayake)** is treated in earnest in the Foundations 3 neuroaesthetics note.

> **🧠 Axis decision memo (evolution)**: In the history of aesthetics, the evolutionary dimension only appears in earnest with Nietzsche. Before that it is *metaphysics·cognitive faculties*. Evolutionary aesthetics (Darwin·Dissanayake·Pinker) is an *external supplement* to the history of aesthetics. **Unresolved**: is the sense of beauty the product of the evolutionary pressures of mating·group cohesion·play? — depth in Foundations 3.

### Axis 3 — Culture (the definition of beauty by cultural sphere — universal vs relative)

**Plato·Aristotle (Greece)**: emphasis on *proportion·balance·unity* — aligned with the *geometric ideal* of Greek sculpture·architecture. Yet Plato is universal (the Idea), Aristotle's analysis is confined to Greek tragedy.

**Kant (the German Enlightenment)**: claims *universal beauty* — assuming cognitive faculties common to all humans. Yet Kant's own examples of beauty are confined to 18th-century European art·natural landscapes. → the contradiction of **a universal claim vs culturally confined examples**.

**Hegel (German idealism)**: explicit *stages of cultural evolution* — ① symbolic (the Orient·Egypt·India, architecture) ② classical (Greece, sculpture) ③ romantic (Christian Europe, painting·music·poetry). → **a Eurocentric evolutionism** — treating the Orient as an immature stage. A target of later criticism.

**Nietzsche**: the contrast of Greece (Apollonian + Dionysian) ↔ Christianity (the morality of the weak). Cultural critique, but within Europe.

**Adorno**: the critique of the *culture industry* (Hollywood·jazz·radio) — developed during his American exile (1938–1949). Yet Adorno's own examples of *genuine art* are confined to European modernism — Schoenberg·Beckett, etc. → **an absence of self-critique of Eurocentrism**.

**The universal vs relative fork**:
- Universalists: Plato·Kant
- Relativists: Hegel (though a stage theory) + subsequent cultural relativism (Boas·Geertz)
- Critical theory: Adorno (Eurocentrism + the analysis of capitalism)

> **🧠 Axis decision memo (culture)**: The history of aesthetics is itself *Eurocentric* — the line of Greece·German idealism·critical theory. Eastern aesthetics (China's *qiyun shengdong*·Japan's *mono no aware*·India's *rasa*)·African·Indigenous American aesthetics are *outside*. **Unresolved**: can the universal-beauty hypothesis (Kant) be verified in non-European cultures? — a tension between neuroaesthetics (common circuits) + cultural anthropology (distinctive styles). This aesthetics note treats *the European history of aesthetics* only — its own limit stated.

---

### Axis 4 — Society (class·institution·market as determinants of beauty)

**Plato**: the expulsion of the poets from the *ideal state* (*Republic* X) — poetry corrupts the citizens' souls. → beauty is an object of *state censorship·control*. Social institutions determine the propriety of beauty.

**Aristotle**: *Politics* VIII — musical education is essential to the formation of citizens. *Poetics* — tragedy's *katharsis* purifies the citizens' emotions. → beauty is a *tool of social education*.

**Kant**: *disinterested* — aesthetic judgment must be separated from society·class·desire. → **isolating beauty from society** (as an ideal). The target of Bourdieu's later critique — Kant's disinterestedness is itself *the social capital of the bourgeois class*.

**Hegel**: the *end of art* — in modernity (Christianity·civil society), art yields its place to *philosophy·religion*. → **social development determines art's social position**. Contemporaneous with 19th-century industrialization·market formation.

**Nietzsche**: the artist = the social function of the *Übermensch*. Rejecting the morality of the weak (Christianity·democracy) and *creating noble values*. → a justification of the hierarchy of social classes.

**Adorno**: the *culture industry* made explicit — Hollywood·radio·jazz as *instruments of homogenization*. Genuine art is an autonomous domain *resisting the market*. *Aesthetic Theory* — art's autonomy is itself defined by its opposition to society. → **the aesthetic dimension of the analysis of 20th-century capitalism**. The direct predecessor of the **subsequent sociological school of Bourdieu·Becker·Dickie**.

> **🧠 Axis decision memo (society)**: The social dimension holds the richest answers. Plato censorship → Aristotle education → Kant isolation → Hegel end → Nietzsche hierarchy → Adorno autonomy·resistance — 6 positions with essentially different social models. **Unresolved**: in the digital·generative-AI age, who decides beauty? (Platform algorithms? The crowd? Critics?) — depth in Foundations 5 (Bourdieu·Becker).

## ④ 🔴 Cross-Field Interaction (the core of the MM-13 candidate — Dohun's most important)

How this history of aesthetics *applies differently/alike* to music·visual art·literature:

### Plato
- **Poetry (literature)**: an object of expulsion — the imitation of an imitation (*Republic* X)
- **Music**: a tool for forming the soul — only Dorian·Phrygian permitted (*Republic* III)
- **Visual art (painting)**: a rank lower even than poetry (*Republic* X)
- **Cross-field hierarchy**: poetry ≥ music > painting (by distance of imitation)
- **Creation**: the imitative capacity itself is dangerous — novelty = moving away from the Idea

### Aristotle
- **Literature (tragedy)**: the core of the *Poetics* — the human imitative nature at its most refined expression
- **Music**: *Politics* VIII — the integration of tragedy's musical element + its own effect on the soul
- **Performance**: tragedy = the *first integrated analysis* of literature + music + performance (opsis)
- **🔴 A case of field integration**: in tragedy, literature·music·theater form *a single undivided art* — the first explicit case of Dohun's stated "one yet separate."
- **Creation**: mimesis is human nature → novelty is the diverse expression of that nature

### Kant
- **Symmetric analysis of all fields**: the form of aesthetic judgment applies identically to poetry·music·painting — the *cross-field equality* of the universality assumption
- **Poetry (literature)**: *Critique of Judgment* §53 — the highest art (free imagination)
- **Music**: §53 — strong in pleasure but lower than poetry for lack of *sustained thought*
- **Painting**: the peak of *plastic beauty*
- **Cross-field hierarchy**: poetry > painting > music (by sustained thought)
- **Creation**: the introduction of the concept of *Genius* — nature that gives the rule (§46) — applied regardless of field

### Hegel
- **🔴 The historical evolution of each field** (the most refined attempt at cross-field integration):
  - **The symbolic stage**: *architecture* (Egyptian pyramids·Indian temples) — spirit < matter
  - **The classical stage**: *sculpture* (Greece) — spirit = matter
  - **The romantic stage**: *painting* → *music* → *poetry* (Christian Europe) — spirit > matter
- **Poetry is the most spiritual** — the peak of the cross-field evolution is poetry (literature)
- **🔴 Field integration**: the 5 fields as *stages of the evolution of Spirit* — the most ambitious first attempt at an integrating model of Dohun's stated "separate yet influencing"
- **Creation**: the dialectical self-movement of Spirit produces new styles

### Nietzsche
- **🔴 The integrated analysis of music + tragedy (literature·theater)**: *Birth of Tragedy* — the tension of the Apollonian (sculpture·narrative·dream·form) + the Dionysian (music·intoxication·life) integrated in tragedy
- **Music**: the most direct (the direct expression of the will — inherited from Schopenhauer)
- **Poetry·narrative**: Apollonian (individuation·form)
- **🔴 Field integration**: music (Dionysian) + tragedy's narrative·theater (Apollonian) — the *integration of opposites* across fields as the peak art — the direct inspiration for Wagner's *Gesamtkunstwerk*
- **Creation**: the tension of the two drives produces novelty

### Adorno
- **The sociology of music** (the most refined): *Philosophy of New Music* — Schoenberg (autonomy) vs Stravinsky (commodity)
- **Literature**: Beckett·Kafka — cases of negative aesthetics
- **Painting**: parts of *Aesthetic Theory* (Picasso·Klee)
- **🔴 Film·jazz·radio**: the *culture industry* — the critique of homogenization
- **Absence of field integration**: Adorno goes deep into *analysis by field* — emphasizing *field autonomy* over integration (though the culture-industry critique cuts across fields)
- **Creation**: the capacity to negate social contradiction is novelty — *progressive musical form* moves in step with social progress

### 🔴 Integrated insight (a first attempted answer to Dohun's meta-question 3)

**Music·visual art·literature are one yet separate, influencing each other**:
- **One** (integration): Aristotle's tragedy / Hegel's stages of Spirit / Nietzsche's Apollonian-Dionysian / Wagner's Gesamtkunstwerk (a successor to Hegel·Nietzsche)
- **Separate** (autonomy): Kant (equal analysis by field) / Adorno (defense of field autonomy — though the culture industry cuts across)
- **Influence mechanisms**: ① formal borrowing (music → poetry: lieder) ② thematic borrowing (visual art → literature: ekphrasis) ③ synesthesia (Kandinsky·Skryabin) ④ integrating media (opera·film·games)

**Creation mechanisms (the answers of these 6 figures)**:
- Plato: creation = danger (departure from the Idea)
- Aristotle: creation = the expression of nature
- Kant: creation = *genius* (nature that gives the rule)
- Hegel: creation = the dialectic of Spirit
- Nietzsche: creation = the tension of the two drives
- Adorno: creation = the negation of social contradiction

→ **6 precursor forms of Boden's modern classification of creativity (combinatorial·exploratory·transformational)**.

## ⑤ Chronological Appendix (R-03 4-act retrofit)

This section is *appendix only* — the R-11 main text is the 4 axes of ③. The chronological 4 acts are retrofitted for the coherence of the history of aesthetics:

### Act 1 — Birth (5th–4th century BC, Greece)
- Late 5th century BC — the Sophists' debates on beauty·art
- The 380s BC — **Plato** *Republic*·*Symposium*·*Phaedrus* — beauty as the imitation of the Idea
- 335 BC — **Aristotle** *Poetics*·*Politics* VIII — mimesis·katharsis·unity

**Decisive turn:** Plato's critique of imitation → Aristotle's affirmation of imitation — a value reversal of the same *mimesis* concept. The two axes of aesthetics for the next 2,000 years.

### Act 2 — Formalization (1750–1831, German idealism)
- 1750 — **Baumgarten** *Aesthetica* — the naming of the discipline
- 1757 — Burke *On the Sublime and Beautiful* — the separation of the sublime/the beautiful
- 1790 — **Kant** *Critique of Judgment* — the critical-philosophical formalization of aesthetic judgment
- 1835 — **Hegel** *Aesthetics* (published posthumously) — integrating aesthetics through the historical dialectic

**Decisive turn:** Kant's *subjective universality* → Hegel's *evolution of objective Spirit* — beauty moves from cognitive faculty to historical spirit.

### Act 3 — Critique·Expansion (1872–1944, 19th–20th centuries)
- 1872 — **Nietzsche** *Birth of Tragedy* — Apollonian/Dionysian, the reinterpretation of Greek tragedy
- 1900 — Croce *Aesthetic* — expressionist aesthetics
- 1934 — Dewey *Art as Experience* — pragmatist aesthetics
- 1944 — **Adorno·Horkheimer** *Dialectic of Enlightenment* — the emergence of the culture-industry critique

**Decisive turn:** Nietzsche's *life-philosophical aesthetics* → Adorno's *critical-theory aesthetics* — aesthetics expands from metaphysics to social·political critique.

### Act 4 — The Modern Fork (1970–present, pluralization)
- 1970 — **Adorno** *Aesthetic Theory* (posthumous) — the formalization of negative aesthetics
- 1968–84 — Goodman·Danto·Dickie — analytic aesthetics·institutional theory (Foundations 2 in earnest)
- 1979 — Bourdieu *Distinction* — the sociological dismantling of aesthetic judgment (Foundations 5 in earnest)
- 1990s– — the emergence of Zeki·Ramachandran neuroaesthetics (Foundations 3 in earnest)
- 2000s– — digital·NFT·generative-AI aesthetics (the advanced domain)

**Decisive turn:** Adorno's *synthesis of aesthetics* → the *forking pluralization* of analytic aesthetics + sociology + neuroscience + the digital — the end of a single aesthetics, the age of interdisciplinary aesthetics.

→ These 4 acts are *appendix*. The essence is the 4 axes of ③.

### Meta-question 2: by what criterion do we call something beauty·art?

**What this note has answered**:
- Plato: the degree of conformity to the Idea (an objective criterion)
- Aristotle: unity·completeness·appropriate magnitude + the effect of katharsis
- Kant: disinterested·universal·necessary·purposiveness without purpose (the 4 moments)
- Hegel: the sensuous shining of the Idea + alignment with the historical stage
- Nietzsche: the Apollonian + Dionysian balance, the capacity to affirm life
- Adorno: the capacity to negate social contradiction, resistance to the culture industry

→ **Criteria coexisting across the 5 dimensions of the objective·subjective·historical·vital·social**. No single one is the correct answer.
