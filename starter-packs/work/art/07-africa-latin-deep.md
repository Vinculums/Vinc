## §1. African aesthetics in earnest

### 1-α — Négritude, Senghor

- **Léopold Sédar Senghor** (1906–2001, Senegal) — the *Négritude* movement (1930s), poet, politician, president
- *Anthologie de la nouvelle poésie nègre et malgache* 1948
- *Liberté I–V* (1964–1993)
- **"l'émotion est nègre, la raison est hellène"** — emotion = Black, reason = Greek (contested, later revised)
- Politics: later *cultural mixing*, *civilisation de l'universel*
- Critique: Soyinka, *Myth, Literature and the African World* 1976 — *"a tiger does not declare its tigritude"*

### 1-β — Achille Mbembe — Necropolitics, Afropolitanism

- **Mbembe** (1957–, Cameroon), *De la postcolonie* 2000, *Critique de la raison nègre* 2013, *Necropolitique* 2016, *Brutalisme* 2020
- *necropolitics* — successor to Foucault's biopower (*politics of death*)
- *Afropolitanism* — a new identity, Africa + cosmopolitan
- Aesthetic application: colonial trauma, aesthetics of technology, the post-colonial in earnest

### 1-γ — àshẹ (Yoruba aesthetics)

- **àshẹ** = *vital force*, *power to make things happen* (Yoruba cosmology)
- Robert Farris Thompson, *Flash of the Spirit* 1983 — African and African-American art in earnest
- The 4 dimensions of àshẹ aesthetics:
  - *coolness* (itutu) — balance, control
  - *ephebism* — freshness, youth
  - *parallelism* — temporality, repetition
  - *vital aliveness* — vitality
- Application: jazz, blues, hip-hop, African sculpture, textile

### 1-δ — Sankofa, Adinkra symbols

- **Sankofa** (Akan, Ghana) — *go back and fetch it* (a bird figure, an egg)
- **Adinkra** = a Ghanaian symbol system — metaphysics, maxims, visual signs
- → a parallel to the East's *八卦* (bagua), India's *mandala* — every culture has a symbol system

### 1-ε — Visual anthropology, African film

- **Jean Rouch** (1917–2004, France) — *Les maîtres fous* 1955, *Chronique d'un été* 1961
- *ethnofiction*, *shared anthropology* — rejecting colonial anthropology
- → Ousmane Sembène (Senegal), Souleymane Cissé (Mali), Djibril Diop Mambéty
- Contemporary: Mati Diop, *Atlantique* 2019 (Cannes Grand Prix), Wanuri Kahiu

### 1-ζ — Music — polyrhythm, call-response, praise singing

- *polyrhythm* in earnest ("non western deep integration" §6-α)
- *call and response* — community integration
- *griot* (West Africa) — integration of oral history, poetry, and music (the Mali Mande tradition)
- Influence: blues, jazz, hip-hop, gospel (diaspora currents)

### 1-η — Contemporary — El Anatsui, Kehinde Wiley, Yinka Shonibare

- El Anatsui (Ghana) — *bottle cap tapestry* (material transformation, the art-craft boundary)
- Kehinde Wiley — Black portraiture + European classical style (the Obama portrait, 2018)
- Yinka Shonibare (Britain/Nigeria) — Dutch wax fabric, colonial critique
- This note works only at the *approach level* — the exhaustive survey of figures is handled by the Art session

## §2. Latin aesthetics in earnest

### 2-α — The foundation of Mestizaje + Magical realism

- **mestizaje** — a *mixture of 3 cultures*: indigenous, European, African ("non western deep integration" §6-c)
- **magical realism** (lo real maravilloso, realismo mágico):
  - Alejo Carpentier, preface to *El reino de este mundo* 1949 — Latin reality itself as *marvelous*
  - García Márquez, *Cien años de soledad* 1967 — canonization
  - Return of Borges ("case applications 3" §3)
  - Isabel Allende, Juan Rulfo, *Pedro Páramo* 1955

### 2-β — Néstor García Canclini — Hybrid Cultures

- **García Canclini**, *Culturas híbridas* 1990 — an analysis of Latin modernity
- *hybridity* — a Bhabha parallel ("Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard" §5)
- *culto, popular, masivo* — a *mixture of 3 dimensions*: high, folk, mass
- *deterritorialization* (directly from Deleuze) — the city, globalization

### 2-γ — Return of Borges — layered aesthetics

- *El Aleph*, *Library of Babel* ("case applications 3" §3)
- Latin = *Europe + indigenous + African* = *all libraries* (a Borges parallel)
- Direct to the post-colonial Bhabha *third space*

### 2-δ — Borges, Cortázar, Onetti — Latin modernism

- The *Boom* (1960s–1970s) — García Márquez, Vargas Llosa, Cortázar, Fuentes
- Integration of magical realism + politics + modernism
- → film (Iñárritu, Cuarón, Del Toro), music (Bossa Nova, Tropicalia)

### 2-ε — Indigenous resurgence

- the *land back* movement (North America), *buen vivir* (South America — Quechua *sumak kawsay*)
- Andean aesthetics — Pachamama, cyclical time
- Mexican mural (Rivera, Orozco, Siqueiros), Oaxaca artisans

### 2-ζ — Tropicalia, bossa nova, tango — musical hybridity

- Bossa nova (Brazil, 1958) — samba + jazz, João Gilberto
- Tropicalia (1968) — Caetano Veloso, Gilberto Gil, Os Mutantes — pop + avant-garde + politics
- Tango (Argentina, 1880–) — multicultural Buenos Aires, renewed by Astor Piazzolla
- → 21st-century reggaeton, Latin trap (Bad Bunny, J Balvin)

### 2-η — Contemporary — Ai-Da, Mexican muralism, Brazil pavilion

- Cuban art (Tania Bruguera, José Bedia)
- The Mexican mural tradition
- 21st-century Latin Biennale, art market
