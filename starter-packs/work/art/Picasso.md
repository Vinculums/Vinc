# Pablo Ruiz Picasso

## ① One-line essence

**"I paint objects as I think them, not as I see them"** — *decomposing and reconstructing* the essence of vision + lifelong stylistic transformation (Blue · Rose · African · Analytic Cubism · Synthetic Cubism · Neoclassical · Surrealist · postwar) + the decisive influence on 20th-century art.

## ② Life and historical context (4 axes)

- **Life**: 1881 Málaga (Spain) — 1973 Mougins (France). Recognized early as a genius (entering art school at 15). From 1900 Paris — residing in France for life. Two marriages · numerous relationships (Fernande Olivier · Olga Khokhlova · Marie-Thérèse · Dora Maar · Françoise Gilot · Jacqueline) — *relations with women + aesthetics* inseparable (an object of feminist critique).
- **Cognition**: visual *decomposition and reconstruction* — simultaneous expression of multiple viewpoints (rejecting the single Renaissance viewpoint; a parallel to the Chinese 三遠 "three distances", jingju)
- **Evolution**: the *decisive inflection point* of 20th-century art — founding modernism + rejecting classification itself
- **Culture**: **🔴 1907 *Les Demoiselles d'Avignon* — borrowing African masks (Fang · Dan)** = the decisive non-Western → Western influence (returning to african-aesthetics). Yet *insufficient acknowledgment of sources* → this note's duty of self-critique
- **Society**: the *very summit* of the art market — wealthy and famous throughout his life. *Boy with a Pipe* (1905), $104M at auction in 2004 · *Les Femmes d'Alger* (1955), $179M at auction in 2015. The peak of Bourdieu's field (sociology-of-art)

## ③ Major works and methodology

**Representative works**:
- *Les Demoiselles d'Avignon* (1907) — founding Cubism · borrowing African masks
- *Guernica* (1937) — a critique of the Spanish Civil War bombing · the peak of political art
- *Three Musicians* (1921) — Synthetic Cubism
- *Weeping Woman* (1937) — Dora Maar
- *The Old Guitarist* (1903–04) — the Blue Period
- About **50,000 works** in a lifetime (painting · drawing · prints · sculpture · ceramics) — a quantitative peak

**Distinctive methodology**:
- **Analytic Cubism** (1909–12 — in collaboration with Braque) — decomposing the object into geometric facets · multiple simultaneous viewpoints
- **Synthetic Cubism** (1912~) — collage (pasted newspaper · wallpaper) — challenging the very essence of the art medium
- **Lifelong stylistic transformation** — Blue · Rose · African · Analytic · Synthetic · Neoclassical · Surrealist · postwar — *the refusal of a fixed style*
- **Multiple media** — painting · drawing · prints · sculpture · ceramics · poetry · theater stage (Diaghilev's *Parade*, 1917)
- **Borrowing African and Iberian masks** — self-critique of the *insufficient acknowledgment of sources*

## ④ 🔴 Cross-field influence and creation (MM-13 accelerated)

**Influence within art**:
- Cubism → Braque · Léger · Gris · Duchamp · Mondrian → founding abstract art
- Surrealism (Dalí · Magritte · Ernst) — a successor of Cubism
- Abstract Expressionism (Pollock · de Kooning) — absorbing and transforming Cubism
- The *common foundation* of all 20th-century art

**Art → other fields**:
- **Literature**: Apollinaire's *Calligrammes* (1918) · Stein's *Tender Buttons* (1914) — Cubist texts
- **Architecture**: Le Corbusier · De Stijl · Bauhaus (gesamtkunstwerk-bauhaus) — Cubist geometry
- **Music**: Stravinsky's *Pulcinella* (1920 — stage costumes by Picasso) · *The Three-Cornered Hat* (Diaghilev)
- **Film**: Eisenstein's montage — a parallel to the Cubist decomposition of time
- **Design · fashion**: Sonia Delaunay · Coco Chanel — geometric patterns
- **Politics**: *Guernica* — the canon of political art

**🔴 Africa · Iberia → Picasso 1907**:
- Fang · Dan masks (West and Central Africa)
- The 1907 visit to the Trocadéro museum (now the Musée du quai Branly) = the direct inspiration of the *Demoiselles*
- Iberian sculpture (indigenous Spanish), the 1906 inspiration
- → **the most explicit case of decisive non-Western → Western influence** (returning to african-aesthetics)

## ⑤ Answers to the meta-questions

- **Q1**: *the pleasure of decomposing and reconstructing the essence of vision* + *the violation of style itself as beauty*. The visual answer to Nietzsche's Dionysus (aesthetics).
- **Q2**: the criterion of beauty = *refinement of decomposition-reconstruction + intensity of stylistic violation + political meaning + integration of multiple viewpoints*. Refusing any single criterion.
- **Q3**: art → influencing literature · architecture · music · film · design alike + **borrowing from Africa into art** (the principal target of critique for the *absence of non-Western influence* in Western art history).

## ⑥ Connections and successors

- Forerunners: Cézanne (the direct forerunner of Cubism) · Iberian sculpture · African masks
- Contemporary collaborations: Braque (co-founder of Cubism) · Stravinsky · Diaghilev · Cocteau · Apollinaire
- Successors: all of 20th-century art
- Rival: Matisse (stylistic difference + mutual respect)
- Critique: feminist (the power of his depiction of women) · decolonial (the ethics of the African borrowing)
