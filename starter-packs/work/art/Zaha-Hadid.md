# Zaha Hadid

## ① One-line essence

**"There are 360 degrees, so why stick to one?"** (Hadid) — translating Malevich's Suprematist *painting* into a tool of architectural design → deconstructivism → creating the parametric architecture of *fluid curved surfaces*. **First woman to win the Pritzker Prize (2004) · first woman awarded the RIBA Gold Medal in her own right (2016)** — from the double margin (an Arab woman) of a male- and Western-centered profession, reshaping the very terrain of contemporary architecture.

## ② Life and historical context (4 axes)

- **Life**: 1950 Baghdad — 2016 Miami (heart attack, age 65). A wealthy secular family — her father Mohammed Hadid a progressive Iraqi politician and industrialist. Boarding schools in Switzerland and Britain → *mathematics* at the American University of Beirut → 1972 the London Architectural Association (AA) School — studying under Rem Koolhaas · Elia Zenghelis → after graduating in 1977, joining OMA as partner (briefly) → 1980 founding Zaha Hadid Architects (ZHA) independently. 1983 winning the Hong Kong Peak international competition (unbuilt) → then a decade-plus as a *paper architect* (an architect of drawings only) → 1993 the Vitra fire station (Weil am Rhein, Germany), her first full realization → 1994 winning the Cardiff Bay opera house then its political cancellation (the peak of frustration) → the 2000s explosion of global realizations. 2004 Pritzker (first woman) · consecutive Stirling Prizes 2010 · 2011 · 2012 DBE (Dame) · 2016 RIBA Royal Gold Medal (first woman in her own right). After her death, ZHA succeeded by Patrik Schumacher.
- **Cognition**: *mathematics → form* — training in algebra and geometry as the foundation of her cognition of curved and distorted space. Designing through *paintings* instead of drafting — a visual cognition that deliberately dismantles the rules of perspective and gravity
- **Evolution**: the orthogonal box of modernism (the International Style) → postmodern eclecticism → **the 1988 MoMA Deconstructivist Architecture exhibition** (one of seven — alongside Gehry · Koolhaas · Eisenman · Libeskind · Tschumi · Coop Himmelb(l)au) → from the 1990s the maturing of digital design tools (CAD · scripting) making the curved surface realizable — *technological evolution as the condition of a style's realization*
- **Culture**: a childhood in Iraqi secular modernism (1950s–60s Baghdad — the era of importing Western modernism) + the London AA avant-garde + absorption of the Russian avant-garde (Malevich · Tatlin) — of Arab origin, yet restrained about directly borrowing Islamic traditional iconography (the affinity with the fluidity of Arabic calligraphy is a scholars' interpretation)
- **Society**: architecture = a field concentrated with capital and power — the double barrier of being a woman and a foreigner. The "diva" stigma · explicit exclusions such as the Cardiff cancellation (1994). Late career: the controversy over worker deaths at the Qatar World Cup stadium (the disputed 2014 remarks) · winning then losing the Tokyo New National Stadium (2015) — herself also a target of critique of the starchitect system as such (a two-sidedness not to be whitewashed)

## ③ Major works and methodology

**Representative works**:
- *Malevich's Tektonik* (1977, AA graduation project) — translating a Suprematist composition into a hotel on the Thames — the origin of the methodology
- *The Peak* (1983, Hong Kong, unbuilt) — drawings of a horizontal skyscraper of exploding strata — the peak of the paper-architect period
- *Vitra Fire Station* (1993) — the first full realization — sharp diagonal concrete (the deconstructivist period)
- *Rosenthal Center for Contemporary Art* (2003, Cincinnati) — the first major American museum designed by a woman
- *Phaeno Science Centre* (2005, Wolfsburg) · *BMW Central Building* (2005, Leipzig)
- *MAXXI National Museum of 21st Century Arts* (2010, Rome) — Stirling Prize
- *Guangzhou Opera House* (2010) — the peak of the fluid-surface period
- *London Aquatics Centre* (2011, for the 2012 Olympics) — the wave roof
- *Heydar Aliyev Center* (2012, Baku) — a single continuous curved surface in which the distinctions of wall, roof, and floor vanish — her signature work
- *Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)* (2014, Seoul) — among the world's largest free-form buildings — a direct intervention in Korea's contemporary urban landscape
- *Beijing Daxing International Airport* (2019, completed posthumously)
- Beyond buildings: furniture · shoes · yachts · stage design — a cross-field design practice

**Distinctive methodology**:
- **Painting = design tool**: transforming Malevich's Suprematist compositions through drawing and painting → generating architectural form — systematizing the *painting → architecture* medium translation into a personal methodology
- **Deconstructivism**: dismantling orthogonality, gravity, and closed form — fragments · diagonals · floating (the 1980s to the first half of the 1990s)
- **Parametric design (parametricism)**: algorithmic design based on parameters — a non-repetitive system in which every member varies continuously. Named as a concept in 2008 by ZHA partner Patrik Schumacher (declaring "the new global style after modernism")
- **Fluid form**: the disappearance of the boundaries of wall, floor, and ceiling — architecturalizing terrain and fluid motion. "Seamless fluidity"
- **Mathematical foundation**: an undergraduate mathematics degree — topology and surface geometry as the base of the formal language

## ④ 🔴 Cross-field influence and creation (MM-13)

#### Malevich's Suprematism → Hadid (T6 + T3)
- **What**: the *floating geometry, weightless composition, non-objectivity* of Suprematist painting → a method of architectural design
- **Path**: Malevich's *Suprematism* (1915 paintings · the 1920s *Architekton* plaster models) → the burial of the Soviet avant-garde (1930s socialist realism) → the 1970s AA School rediscovery of the Russian avant-garde → Hadid's 1977 graduation project *Malevich's Tektonik* → the 1983 *The Peak* drawings → a lifelong methodology
- **Timing**: 1915 → 1977 (the architectural translation 62 years later)
- **Transformation**: **T6 medium translation (painting → architecture) + T3 transformative inheritance** — Malevich's own *Architekton* was static and orthogonal, but Hadid transformed Suprematism's *motion and floating* into a dynamics of curved surfaces. A case of *cross-field translation* paralleling Kandinsky's audio-visual synthesis (returning to Bach ④)
- **Basis**: *Malevich's Tektonik* (1977) / numerous Hadid retrospective interviews / the discourse of the 2014 State Hermitage and Serpentine Malevich-linked exhibitions

#### Koolhaas · the AA School → Hadid (T1 direct apprenticeship)
- **What**: conceptual architecture · program-centered thinking · the autonomy of the drawing
- **Path**: 1972–77 studying under Rem Koolhaas · Elia Zenghelis at the AA → 1977 OMA partner → 1980 independence (her own line after apprenticeship)
- **Transformation**: **T1 direct apprenticeship** + partial T4 (diverging toward a line of *formal language*, unlike OMA's city- and program-centered approach) — Koolhaas's verdict: "a planet in her own orbit"
- **Basis**: the AA graduation-jury records / Koolhaas's tributes (2004 Pritzker · 2016 memorial)

#### The 1988 MoMA deconstructivism exhibition → Hadid's canonization (T8)
- **What**: *Deconstructivist Architecture*, curated by Philip Johnson · Mark Wigley — included as the only woman in the group of seven → the paper architect's entry into the international canon
- **Transformation**: T8 reinterpretation — the exhibition's own frame of reinterpreting Russian Constructivism cohered with Hadid's methodology
- **Basis**: the 1988 MoMA exhibition catalog (Johnson · Wigley)

#### Hadid → Schumacher's parametricism (T10 + T2)
- **What**: ZHA's design practice → the theorization of *Parametricism* (a style declaration)
- **Path**: Patrik Schumacher joining ZHA in 1988 → a division of roles as partner and theorist (Hadid's formal intuition ⇄ Schumacher's algorithms and theory) → Schumacher's "Parametricism as Style" (the 2008 Venice Biennale manifesto) → succeeding ZHA after her death in 2016
- **Timing**: 1988 joining · 2008 naming · 2016 succession
- **Transformation**: **T10 bidirectional collaboration + T2 stylistic inheritance** — a rare contemporary case of a personal methodology theorized into a *named style (an –ism)*. Yet Schumacher's political radicalization (his market-liberal declarations) is a separate controversy after Hadid's death
- **Basis**: Schumacher's *The Autopoiesis of Architecture* (2011 · 2012) / the 2008 manifesto

#### Hadid → the terrain of contemporary architecture (T2)
- **What**: the global normalization of free-form, curved architecture — across the building types of museum · opera house · airport · stadium
- **Path**: the 2000s global realizations of ZHA → mutual acceleration with the industry standardization of digital design and construction (BIM · 3D fabrication) → the generalization of the free-form vocabulary in the following generation (BIG · MAD Architects, Ma Yansong 马岩松 · Thomas Heatherwick, etc.) → Seoul's DDP (2014) = a direct case in the Korean urban landscape
- **Transformation**: T2 stylistic inheritance — yet the critique of "iconic architecture and the star system" (a target of the Tafuri lineage of architectural theory) is inherited along with it
- **Basis**: the 2004 Pritzker citation / the 2016 RIBA Gold Medal citation

#### Hadid → the terrain of women architects (T8 + symbolic capital)
- **What**: first woman Pritzker (2004) · first solo woman RIBA Gold Medal (2016) — making the gender barrier of the profession visible and partially cracking it
- **Transformation**: symbolic breakthrough and its limits at once — Hadid herself was wary of the "woman architect" categorization ("I am an architect"). Contemporaneous with the Denise Scott Brown Pritzker-exclusion controversy (the 2013 petition) — the tension of individual breakthrough ↔ structural problem
- **Basis**: Pritzker 2004 / the Scott Brown petition (2013) / Despina Stratigakos's *Where Are the Women Architects?* (2016)

**Architecture → other fields**:
- **Design**: furniture (B&B Italia · Sawaya & Moroni) · shoes (Lacoste · United Nude) · yachts (Blohm+Voss) — transplanting the parametric language into products
- **Fashion**: the free-form curved aesthetic converging with 3D-printed fashion (Iris van Herpen and others) (T7)
- **Digital art**: parametric algorithms → spreading as a vocabulary of generative design and game-environment design

## ⑤ Answers to the meta-questions

- **Q1 (why define beauty?)**: beauty = *the spatialization of motion and flow* — the form of currents and energy, not a static orthogonal order. The architectural answer to Malevich's non-objective painting
- **Q2 (by what criterion?)**: *continuity (seamlessness) + mathematical coherence (parametric rules) + fluid integration with terrain and context*. A head-on replacement of classical proportion (the Plato–aesthetics lineage) — a criterion with rules but without repetition
- **Q3 (cross-field influence and creation)**: creating a personal methodology through the painting → architecture medium translation (T6) → producing the *named style* of parametricism + re-crossing from architecture into products, fashion, and the digital — the contemporary individual version of the 20th-century Bauhaus integration model

## ⑥ Connections and successors

- Forerunners: Malevich · Tatlin (the Russian avant-garde) · Oscar Niemeyer (the precedent of the curve) · Frei Otto (structural form-finding)
- Teachers: Rem Koolhaas · Elia Zenghelis (AA · OMA)
- Contemporaries: Gehry · Eisenman · Libeskind · Tschumi (the seven of the 1988 deconstructivism exhibition) · SANAA · Tadao Ando (the contemporary antipode — minimal stillness)
- Successors: Patrik Schumacher (ZHA · parametricism) · BIG · MAD (Ma Yansong) · the subsequent terrain of women architects (Kazuyo Sejima — 2010 Pritzker, the second woman)
- Crossings: Iris van Herpen (fashion convergence) · generative design · game environments
- Korean connection: the DDP (2014, Seoul) — collision and coexistence with the Korean architectural discourse of the Kim Swoo-geun · Seung H-Sang lineage (linked to the Korean-architecture item, missing-checklist v12 #2)
