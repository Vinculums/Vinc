# post-internet aesthetics

## Position of this document

Follow-up to "Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard" and "digital AI aesthetics". The additional 7 tracks of the *21st-century theoretical horizon*. The *matter, body, environment, sound* dimensions of the R-11 4 axes (cognitive, evolutionary, cultural, social) in earnest.

## §1. New Materialism — Bennett, Barad, Braidotti

### 1-α — Vital materialism (Bennett 2010)

- **Jane Bennett** *Vibrant Matter: A Political Ecology of Things*
- *thing-power* — the *agency* of things
- rejection of anthropocentrism — *non-human actors* (electricity, metal, trash)
- Art application: reinterpretation of the *found object*, assemblage, Arte Povera

### 1-β — Agential realism (Barad 2007)

- **Karen Barad** *Meeting the Universe Halfway*
- physicist + feminist — Bohr's quantum mechanics + feminism
- *intra-action* — the identities of two objects arise *after their interaction* (they are *entangled*)
- measuring instrument + measured object = *co-arising*
- → the *entanglement aesthetics* of painting, VR, generative AI

### 1-γ — Nomadic subject (Braidotti 1994, 2013)

- **Rosi Braidotti** *Nomadic Subjects*, *The Posthuman*
- post-humanism in earnest — the *human + non-human continuum*
- *zoe* (life) vs *bios* (political life)
- Art application: bio art (Eduardo Kac, Stelarc), technological art

### 1-δ — Mathematical parallels

- *intra-action* (Barad) ↔ quantum entanglement (Math fa↔QM bridge §dictionary)
- *vital materialism* ↔ network, graph (Math 03)
- *nomadic subject* ↔ moduli space (Math 04 §act 3)

## §2. Affect Theory — Massumi, Sedgwick, Ahmed

### 2-α — Affect vs Emotion (Massumi 1995)

- **Brian Massumi** *The Autonomy of Affect*
- **affect** = *pre-personal, intensity, body-level* (a Spinoza–Deleuze foundation)
- **emotion** = *personal, named, culturally coded*
- Art acts directly on *affect* — before representation, before language

### 2-β — Ugly feelings (Ngai 2005)

- **Sianne Ngai** *Ugly Feelings*
- the aesthetic value of negative emotions (envy, irritation, anxiety, paranoia)
- *aesthetic categories* (2012) — cute, zany, interesting

### 2-c — Queer phenomenology (Ahmed 2006)

- **Sara Ahmed** *Queer Phenomenology*
- *orientation* — where one is oriented is identity
- *happy objects* (2010) — the objects to which happiness attaches
- Art application: queer art, the politics of orientation

### 2-d — Trauma, memory (Caruth, Felman)

- **Cathy Caruth** *Unclaimed Experience* 1996
- trauma = *an experience that returns*
- 9/11, Holocaust, post-colonial trauma art

### 2-e — Mathematical parallels

- affect = continuous flow ↔ differential equation (Math 02)
- emotion = discrete state ↔ finite automaton (Math 07)
- trauma = recursive return ↔ recursive function, strange loop

## §3. Queer / Trans Aesthetics

### 3-α — Performativity (Butler 1990, 1993)

- **Judith Butler** *Gender Trouble*, *Bodies That Matter*
- gender = *repeated performance*, *citation*
- Art application: drag (RuPaul, Vaginal Davis), performance art

### 3-β — Camp (Sontag 1964, Cleto 1999)

- **Sontag** *Notes on Camp* — *failed seriousness*
- *exaggeration, artifice, doubleness*
- queer culture history (Warhol, Almodóvar, John Waters)

### 3-γ — Disidentification (Muñoz 1999)

- **José Esteban Muñoz** *Disidentifications: Queers of Color and the Performance of Politics*
- identity *refused + transformed + used*
- a strategy of minoritarian art

### 3-d — Trans aesthetics

- **Susan Stryker**, **Jay Prosser**, **Paul Preciado**
- the aesthetics of *passing*, *becoming*, *body modification*
- bio-political + bodily autonomy

### 3-e — Crip theory, disability aesthetics

- **Robert McRuer** *Crip Theory* 2006
- rejecting *able-bodiedness* — disability = identity, aesthetics
- Art application: Christine Sun Kim, Park McArthur

## §4. Environmental, Ecological Aesthetics

### 4-α — Land art, earth art (1960s–70s)

- **Robert Smithson** *Spiral Jetty* 1970
- **Walter De Maria** *Lightning Field* 1977
- **Nancy Holt** *Sun Tunnels* 1976
- rejecting the museum — nature = the medium

### 4-β — Ecological aesthetics, ecocriticism

- **Allen Carlson** *Aesthetics and the Environment* 2000 — *scientific cognitivism*
- *natural aesthetic appreciation* vs *human-made*
- a parallel to Eastern landscape painting and Daoist aesthetics ("aesthetic theories deep" §3-1)

### 4-γ — Anthropocene, climate art

- *planetary aesthetics*, *Plantationocene* (Haraway 2015)
- **Olafur Eliasson** *Ice Watch*, *Weather Project*
- **Pierre Huyghe** *Untilled* 2012
- **Hito Steyerl** *In Free Fall*, *How Not to Be Seen*

### 4-δ — Indigenous environmental aesthetics

- the *land back* movement, indigenous knowledge
- Robin Wall Kimmerer, *Braiding Sweetgrass* 2013
- rejecting colonialism + oneness with nature

### 4-ε — Mathematical parallels

- ecological dynamics ↔ Lotka–Volterra, predator-prey ODE (Math 02, 06)
- climate model ↔ chaotic dynamical system (Lorenz)
- biodiversity ↔ entropy, diversity index

## §5. Sound Studies, Soundscape

### 5-α — Acoustic ecology (Schafer 1977)

- **R. Murray Schafer** *The Soundscape*
- *hi-fi* vs *lo-fi* soundscape
- *keynote sounds*, *signals*, *soundmarks*

### 5-β — Sound art (1970s–)

- **Max Neuhaus** *Drive-In Music* 1967, *Times Square* 1977
- **Christina Kubisch** *Electrical Walks*
- the boundary between music and sound art

### 5-γ — Sonic philosophy (Goodman, Cox)

- **Steve Goodman** *Sonic Warfare* 2010 — the politics of the sonic weapon, infrasound
- **Christoph Cox** *Sonic Flux* 2018 — a full philosophy of sound

### 5-δ — Field recording, acoustic anthropology

- **Steven Feld** *Sound and Sentiment* 1982 — Kaluli (PNG) acoustic anthropology
- *acoustemology* — knowing through sound

### 5-ε — Mathematical parallels

- soundscape = Fourier decomposition (Math 02 §act 2)
- acoustic ecology = signal-to-noise ratio (information theory)
- sonic art = a *time + space + frequency* matrix (Math 02, 06)

## §6. Post-Internet Aesthetics (2010s–)

### 6-α — Defining Post-Internet (Olson, Vierkant)

- **Marisa Olson** *post-Internet art* 2008 — art operating after the Internet
- **Artie Vierkant** *Image Object* 2010 — digital ↔ physical *mutual conversion*
- the Internet is *base infrastructure* — all art is already post-Internet

### 6-β — Vaporwave, seapunk, hauntology

- 1990s nostalgia + twisting digital/corporate aesthetics
- **Mark Fisher** *Ghosts of My Life* 2014 — *hauntology*, *lost futures*
- an aesthetics of the *loss* of the future

### 6-γ — Meme aesthetics

- *folk art of the 21st century*
- *exploitable, remixable, viral*
- → algorithmic culture (TikTok, Instagram Reels)

### 6-δ — NFT, crypto art

- → directly "digital AI aesthetics" §2

### 6-ε — Mathematical parallels

- meme propagation ↔ branching process, percolation (Math 06)
- viral cascade ↔ network theory (Math 03, 04)
- vaporwave = *frequency shifting* (Math 02 Fourier)

## §7. Media Archaeology — Huhtamo, Parikka, Kittler

### 7-α — Media archaeology (Huhtamo, Parikka 2011)

- **Erkki Huhtamo, Jussi Parikka** *Media Archaeology*
- excavating *dead media*, *lost technologies*
- rejecting linear progress — a *non-linear media history*

### 7-β — Cultural techniques (Krämer, Siegert, Vismann)

- **Friedrich Kittler** *Discourse Networks 1800/1900* 1985, *Gramophone, Film, Typewriter* 1986
- media *produce culture*
- *hardware* + *software* not separated

### 7-γ — Zombie media, obsolescence

- **Jussi Parikka** *Zombie Media* 2012
- the *revival* of discarded media
- a critique of electronic waste, planned obsolescence

### 7-δ — German media theory

- the Kittler school — mathematics + media
- more *technical and material* than McLuhan
- Art application: media art, glitch art
