## §1. Bach's *Art of Fugue* (1742–1750) — Group Theory·Category·Information Theory

### 1-α — The work's structure

- **14 contrapuncti + 4 canons** + the unfinished *Contrapunctus XIV* (at the point of his death)
- *A systematic exploration of every contrapuntal possibility* over a single *subject*
- Self-reference: the B-A-C-H motif (B♭-A-C-B♮) appears

### 1-β — Mathematical analysis — group action

**The subject-transformation group**:
- *prime* (P) — the original form
- *inversion* (I) — intervals reversed
- *retrograde* (R) — time reversed
- *retrograde-inversion* (RI) — both

→ **the Klein 4-group** $V = \{P, I, R, RI\}$, $V \cong \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$

Additional transformations:
- *augmentation* (A) — note values doubled
- *diminution* (D) — note values halved
- *transposition* $T_n$ — a shift of n semitones — a $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$ action

→ **The overall transformation group**: $\mathbb{Z}_{12} \rtimes (V \times \mathbb{Z})$ — a semi-direct product (transposition + Klein 4 + scaling)

### 1-γ — *Crab canon* (Contrapunctus XII) — time symmetry

- Two voices: $v_1(t)$·$v_2(t)$, $v_2(t) = v_1(T - t)$ (time reversal)
- **Hofstadter's strange loop** (Gödel·Escher·Bach) — self-reference
- Mathematically: an *involution* $\sigma : t \to T - t$, $\sigma^2 = \text{id}$

### 1-δ — Information analysis

- Shannon entropy of the *subject's note distribution*: $H \approx \log_2(7) \approx 2.81$ bits (7 diatonic notes)
- Markov chain order: Bach is *order 3–4* (long-range correlation)
- → a *deeper structure* than the average of popular music (order 1–2)

### 1-ε — Neuroaesthetic interpretation

- **Berlyne's inverted-U**: the Bach fugue is a *golden balance of complexity-simplicity* — Janata 2009 fMRI: activation of the ventromedial prefrontal cortex
- *Predictive coding* (Friston): the fugue's *systematic prediction + variation* — optimal prediction surprise
- **Schmidhuber's compression progress**: the fugue is *the extreme of learning progress* (systematic discovery)

### 1-ζ — Cross-field influence (Q3)

- Bach → Beethoven (*Grosse Fuge*)·Brahms·Bartók·Hindemith·Webern·Hofstadter (literature)·Escher (visual)
- *Influence into mathematics·literature·visual art* — the *cross-arts influence* of a single work in earnest

## §2. Pollock's *Number 1A* (1948) — Fractals·Neuroaesthetics

### 2-α — The work·technique

- *action painting* — canvas on the floor·drip·fling·spatter
- *all-over composition* — no center·homogeneous
- *dripping technique* — the *control + randomness* of paint flow

### 2-β — Fractal analysis (Taylor 1999)

- **Taylor·Micolich·Jonas** *Nature* 1999 — analysis of the *fractal dimension* of Pollock's paintings
- **$D \approx 1.7$** (cumulative 1944–1953, evolving by period)
- Natural matches: clouds $D \approx 1.3$·coastlines $D \approx 1.25$·trees $D \approx 1.6$·mountains $D \approx 1.7$

- Taylor's 2006 sequel — an attempt to *detect non-Pollock forgeries* (commissioned by the Krasner Foundation)
- Academic rebuttal (Jones-Smith·Mathur·Krauss 2009) — the limits of fractal-D detection
- *Unresolved*: whether fractal D is *sufficient as an authenticity-detection tool*

### 2-δ — Neuroaesthetics

- **The natural-match hypothesis** — the human visual cortex is optimized to process *natural fractals* ($D \approx 1.3–1.5$) → a preference for works that parallel nature
- **Spehar et al. 2003** *Universal aesthetic of fractals* — a $D \approx 1.3–1.5$ preference is universal (experimental)
- Pollock $D \approx 1.7$ — a parallel to natural mountains, but *above the most-preferred region* — an artistic *challenge*

### 2-ε — Cross-field influence

## §3. Debussy's *La Mer* (1905) — Fourier·Spectral·Impressionism

### 3-α — The work

- 3 symphonic sketches: *De l'aube à midi sur la mer*·*Jeux de vagues*·*Dialogue du vent et de la mer*
- **Impressionist music** — *timbre·spectral* over harmony
- 9th·11th·13th chords·whole-tone scale·pentatonic — *non-functional harmony*

### 3-β — Fourier analysis

- **spectral composition** — harmony over the *harmonic series* of a tone (a direct parallel to Math 02 §Act 2 spectral)
- 9th chord = *use up to the 9th partial*
- whole-tone scale ($\mathbb{Z}_6$) — a *symmetric* division of tones — *violating* functional harmony (absence of a key)
- pentatonic — a 5-note scale — *non-Western influence* (Japonisme, the 1889 Paris Exposition)

### 3-γ — Mathematical parallel

- **whole-tone scale**: $\{0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10\}$ — $\mathbb{Z}_6 \subset \mathbb{Z}_{12}$ — an *index-2 subgroup*
- Only **2 whole-tone scales** exist ($\{0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10\}$·$\{1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11\}$) — *2 orbits* under the *transposition* action
- *Symmetry* = *loss of direction* — a parallel to the impressionist *aesthetics of ambiguity*

### 3-δ — Impressionist visual·literary parallels

- **Monet** *Impression, soleil levant* (1872) — light·color·spectral
- **Mallarmé** *L'Après-midi d'un faune* (1876) → Debussy's work of the same name (1894)
- **The correspondence of 3 fields (music·visual·literature)** — impressionism = *cross-field influence* in earnest

### 3-ε — The spectralism sequel (1970s–)

- **Grisey·Murail** — the *spectral music* school
- analysis of the *temporal evolution* of timbre (Fourier transform directly)
- Debussy → the spectral school, a direct connection

## §4. Northern Song Landscape Painting — Guo Xi's *Early Spring* 早春圖 (1072)

### 4-α — The work

- silk·ink wash·158 × 108 cm — the National Palace Museum, Taiwan
- **早春** = early spring — the *beginning* of new life
- a direct application of Guo Xi's own *Linquan Gaozhi* 林泉高致 (a theory of landscape painting)

### 4-β — The "three distances" 三遠 structure

- *high distance* 高遠: from below the mountain looking up (the central 主峰 main peak)
- *deep distance* 深遠: the valleys between mountains (descending in a zigzag)
- *level distance* 平遠: distant mist (the flatland at left)
- → **3 viewpoints coexist in one painting** — *a complete violation* of Western single-point perspective

### 4-γ — Mathematical parallel

- Western single-point perspective = $\mathbb{P}^2$ projection — a *single viewpoint*
- the Eastern "three distances" 三遠 = *3 different projections simultaneously* — *the combination of separated coordinate systems*
- Mathematically: an *atlas of charts* (a manifold) — 3 charts integrated *without overlap*
- → a parallel to Math 04 manifolds

### 4-δ — Blank space 餘白

- *more than 1/3 of the painting is empty* — mist·water·sky
- *Shannon entropy of negative space*: 0 = the maximum *receptive space*
- Eastern aesthetics: *emptiness is the essence of fullness*

### 4-ε — Daoist aesthetics

- landscape = an expression of the Dao
- *wu wei* 無爲·*ziran* 自然 — union with a nature that is not artificial
- the Guo family (Northern Song) integrated Daoism and Confucianism

### 4-ζ — Cross-field influence (cross-cultural)

- 19th-century Japonisme (Japanese ukiyo-e → the West) → 20th-century landscape painting → the influence on Klee·Tobey
- modern *minimalism* (Rothko·Reinhardt) — the influence of Eastern emptiness
