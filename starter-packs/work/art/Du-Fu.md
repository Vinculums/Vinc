# Du Fu (杜甫)

## ① One-line essence

**詩聖 (the Sage of Poetry)** — 1,500 poems + the peak of the *regulated-verse (律詩)* form + socially realist poetry integrating *war · the people · his own suffering* through the An Lushan Rebellion (安史之亂, 755–763). Confucian morality + autobiographical depth.

## ② Life and historical context (4 axes)

- **Life**: 712 河南 (Henan, China) — 770, died aboard a boat on the 湘江 (Xiang River). Son of Du Xian (杜閑) · descendant of Du Yu (杜預, a Western Jin scholar). In his 20s–30s, 漫游 (wandering) — roaming across China · meeting Li Bai (李白, 701–762) · Gao Shi (高適) (744). After age 35, attempts and failures to enter office in Chang'an (長安). **The An Lushan Rebellion breaks out in 755** — Du Fu's family scattered · lifelong poverty and suffering. 759 the thatched cottage at Chengdu, Sichuan (四川 成都 草堂, Chengdu Caotang) · 767–770 the frontier · died on the river.
- **Cognition**: *simultaneous expression* of personal suffering + social reality — poetry integrating the personal + the political
- **Evolution**: the peak of Tang poetry (713–770, from the flourishing of the Kaiyuan era 開元盛世 → the An Lushan Rebellion) — Du Fu experienced *both* the age of prosperity and the age of crisis. The evolution of poetry itself: the peak of the regulated-verse form + the free form of ancient-style verse (古詩).
- **Culture**: Confucian (儒) — morality · social duty · loyalty to the throne + Daoist nature (道) + Buddhist impermanence (佛). Absorbing the multi-civilizational culture of the Tang (Buddhist · Persian · Central Asian influences).
- **Society**: of scholar-official (士大夫) origin · failing the civil examinations · lifelong poverty · a single brief entry into the court (757–759). Directly witnessing the suffering of the people through the An Lushan Rebellion — founding *realist poetry*.

## ③ Major works and methodology

**Representative works (of 1,500+ poems)**:
- *春望* ("Spring View" / 757) — "國破山河在·城春草木深" = the state is shattered, yet mountains and rivers remain; spring in the city, grass and trees grow deep. Contrasting the despair of the An Lushan Rebellion with the eternity of nature
- *三吏三別* ("Three Officers, Three Partings" — 760) — *新安吏·石壕吏·潼關吏 + 新婚別·無家別·垂老別* = the misery of military conscription. The peak of socially realist poetry
- *茅屋為秋風所破歌* ("Song of My Cottage Wrecked by the Autumn Wind" — 761) — integrating poverty and the suffering of the people ("安得廣廈千萬間 / 大庇天下寒士俱歡顏")
- *秋興八首* ("Eight Poems on Autumn Meditations" — 766) — the peak of regulated verse · autobiographical depth
- *登高* ("Climbing High" — 767) — "萬里悲秋常作客·百年多病獨登臺" — autumn sorrow · a lifelong sojourner · illness · solitude

**Distinctive methodology**:
- **Peak of 律詩 (lüshi)**: 8 lines of five or seven characters · strict tonal patterns · rhyme · parallel couplets (對句). Du Fu brought the form to *extreme refinement* — a model for every later poet
- **Freedom of 古詩 (gushi)**: non-regulated · free length
- **Autobiography + society**: personal suffering and social crisis are *not separated* — the personal poem = testimony of the age
- **Integration of the visual and auditory**: landscape · sound · figures at once (pictorial poetry + musical rhyme)
- **Integration of Confucian morality + Daoist nature (道) + Buddhist impermanence (佛)** — the confluence of the Tang's three teachings

## ④ 🔴 Cross-field influence and creation (MM-13)

**Within literature**:
- Song dynasty (宋) Su Dongpo · Huang Tingjian · Lu You all deeply influenced by Du Fu
- Ming dynasty Gao Bing's *Tangshi Pinhui* — canonizing Du Fu
- Joseon sijo · hansi — Du Fu's decisive influence on Korean literature (Jeong Mong-ju · Yi Hwang · Jeong Yak-yong)
- Japanese hansi (kanshi) — the canon of imitating Du Fu
- Vietnamese hansi — decisively shaped by Du Fu
- Modern: of presumed Nobel-caliber stature · a canon of world poetry (English translations by Burton Watson · David Hinton · Stephen Owen)
- Ezra Pound's *Cathay* (1915) — English translation of Chinese poetry + Imagism — including some Du Fu
- Kenneth Rexroth · Gary Snyder — Du Fu's influence on American modernism

**Literature → other fields**:
- **Calligraphy** (書法): Du Fu's poems a *calligraphic canon* — integration of poetry + calligraphy + painting (詩書畫一體, returning to jingju)
- **Painting**: Du Fu's poetry influencing Tang–Song landscape painting (a direct parallel with Wang Wei's poetry–painting integration — Wang Wei's *詩中有畫·畫中有詩*)
- **Music**: musical settings of *Du Fu's poems* in the Song and Qing dynasties (詞牌 cipai)
- **Modern film**: *Du Fu: China's Greatest Poet* (BBC 2020 — recited by Ian McKellen)

## ⑤ Answers to the meta-questions

- **Q1**: humans discover and define beauty in *suffering · the crisis of the age* — an *affinity* with Adorno's (aesthetics) negative aesthetics. Yet preceding Adorno by 1,100 years.
- **Q2**: the criterion of beauty = *refinement of regulated-verse form + integration of autobiography and society + confluence of the three teachings (儒道佛, Confucian-Daoist-Buddhist) + integration of the visual and auditory*. The *reverse direction* of Western Kantian disinterestedness (aesthetics) (interest · suffering · political depth are beauty).
- **Q3**: poetry + calligraphy + painting = founding the East Asian 詩書畫一體 (unity of poetry, calligraphy, and painting) (jingju) — 1,100 years ahead of Wagner's Gesamtkunstwerk. A single poet's poetry itself as field integration.
