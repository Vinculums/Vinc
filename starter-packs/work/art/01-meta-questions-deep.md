# why beauty

## Position of this document

Direct successor to Dohun's learning philosophy §2-2 C. The *full-depth* stage of the Art domain — not an exhaustive survey of figures, but a deepening of *the 3 meta-questions themselves*. Mechanism ③, cross-field influence and creation, is *directly combined* with "Math ↔ Art".

The 3 meta-questions:
- **Q1** Why do humans *define* beauty?
- **Q2** By what *criteria* is beauty determined?
- **Q3** How do music, visual art, and literature *influence and create each other*? ⭐ (Dohun's *most important*)

This note deepens not the *answers* but the *structure, layers, combinatorial possibilities, and open problems of the questions*.

## Q1 — Why do humans *define* beauty?

### Q1-α — 4 answer layers

| Layer | Answer | Strength | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cognitive** | evolutionary adaptation — pattern recognition, mate signaling, environmental safety signals | grounded in evolutionary biology | leaves *why abstract music or geometric art is beautiful* unexplained |
| **Evolutionary** | sexual selection (Darwin, Miller, *The Mating Mind* 2000) — art = mating signal | a parallel to birdsong, peacock plumage | insufficient quantification of the *non-utilitarian complexity* of human art |
| **Cultural** | social construction, class, capital (Bourdieu, *Distinction* 1979) — beauty = class distinction | museum, concert data | *why every culture has beauty* (universal vs particular) |
| **Social** | institutional theory (Dickie 1969, Becker, *Art Worlds* 1982) — it is art if the art world recognizes it | explains contemporary art (Duchamp, Warhol) | *why the institution recognizes that work* (circular) |

→ The 4 layers *operate simultaneously* — consistent with the R-11 candidate (the 4 axes: cognitive, evolutionary, cultural, social).

### Q1-β — Why-Why 5-step depth analysis

1. Why do humans define beauty? → *the evolutionary, cognitive, cultural, and social layers simultaneously*
2. Why do the 4 layers operate simultaneously? → the human brain is an integrated system of *prediction, reward, and social learning*
3. Why does the integration of prediction, reward, and social learning produce *beauty*? → Friston's free energy, Schmidhuber's compression progress as *candidates* — beauty = learning progress, prediction surprise
4. Why is learning progress / prediction surprise a *subjective pleasure*? → *unknown* — the hard problem of consciousness
5. Why is the hard problem of consciousness unresolved? → *unknown* — infinite regress

→ *At step 4 it collides with the hard problem of consciousness*. The essence of beauty is deeply co-moving with the *essence of consciousness*.

### Q1-γ — The nature of a discipline without a correct answer

Consistent with Dohun's learning philosophy §2-2 C: Q1 is *a question that may have no answer*. Yet *the very process of seeking an answer* has value.

- Cognitive neuroscience (Zeki, Ramachandran) + evolutionary aesthetics (Dissanayake) + cultural aesthetics (Bourdieu) + institutional aesthetics (Dickie, Becker) — **the 4 schools are not mutually contradictory but *mutually complementary*** (consistent with Dohun's *preserving diverse perspectives equally*)

## Q2 — By what *criteria* is beauty determined?

### Q2-α — 6 criterion candidates

| Criterion | Definition | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Proportion, symmetry** (classical, Pythagoras, Vitruvius) | golden ratio, integer ratios, equilibrium | Parthenon, Stradivarius, Bach fugue — partial verification via *statistical frequency* |
| **Complexity–simplicity balance** (Berlyne 1971) | inverted-U — both too simple and too complex are disliked | music (Cage's 4'33" vs serialism), art (Mondrian vs Pollock) — partial verification |
| **Imitation, truth** (Plato, Aristotle mimesis) | fidelity of the representation of reality | violated by modern abstract art (Mondrian, Kandinsky, Suprematism) |
| **Expression** (Romantic, Tolstoy) | intensity, authenticity of emotional transmission | hard to measure, culture-dependent |
| **Novelty, surprise** (Schmidhuber compression progress, Itti–Baldi Bayesian surprise) | learning progress, prediction surprise | temporal evolution of popular music (Mauch et al. 2015, *Pop music evolution*) — partial verification |
| **Social, institutional recognition** (Dickie, Becker) | art-world authority, exhibition, market | circular, *why that work* is unanswered |

→ The 6 criteria are *not mutually contradictory*. Their *weights change* by era, genre, and culture. The R-11 candidate's 4 axes (cognitive, evolutionary, cultural, social) operate as an *integrating coordinate system* for the 6 criteria.

### Q2-β — An attempt at a mathematical measure

The *core of this note's mathematical combination*. A direct application of "Math ↔ Art" §axis 4 neuroaesthetics:

| Mathematical measure | Criterion of beauty | Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Birkhoff 1933** $M = O/C$ (order/complexity) | proportion, symmetry + complexity balance | polygon, vase, tile — *generalization* incomplete |
| **Shannon entropy** $H$ | informational complexity | musical surprise, literary stylometry |
| **Bayesian surprise** $D_{KL}(P_{prior} \| P_{post})$ | novelty | Itti–Baldi 2009 — visual saliency |
| **Schmidhuber compression progress** $-\Delta K$ | learning progress | candidate for beauty/creativity (1991) |
| **Fractal dimension** $D$ (Hausdorff) | matching nature | Pollock $D \approx 1.7$ — matches clouds, trees in nature (Taylor 1999) |
| **Free energy** $F = \mathbb{E}[-\log p(x)] - H[q(z)]$ | predictive processing | Friston aesthetic processing |
| **Persistent homology** | structure, form | analysis of Bach, Beethoven works (Bigo et al. 2013, *Tonnetz*) |

→ **Each mathematical measure is a *partial fit*** — *a complete aesthetic measurement is incomplete*. R-08 open problem = the combination itself is unresolved.

### Q2-γ — Diversity preservation vs average statistics (directly the U-22 candidate)

Dohun states explicitly: "A high percentage of majority opinion may itself become reputation, but why do people define these diverse perspectives and by what criteria..."

- **Reputation** (majority-opinion statistics): *measurable*, its *temporal evolution* traceable (Mauch et al. 2015)
- **Individual perspective**: *not measured — the essence of diversity is preserved*

→ **Consistent with the U-22 candidate** (recognizing the separation of reputation vs individual perspective). This note tracks both dimensions simultaneously: the measurement attempt + the *equal recognition* of preserving the essence of diversity.

## Q3 — The mechanism of cross-field influence and creation ⭐ (Dohun's *most important*)

### Q3-α — Classification of 5 mechanisms

Dohun states explicitly, "music, literature, and the visual arts are one, and, though separated, they influence one another and create novelty":

1. **Direct combination** (lieder, illustration, ekphrasis, synesthesia) — two fields combined *within the same work*
2. **Style transfer** (cubist painting → modernist literature *Ulysses*) — one field's style *migrates to another field*
3. **Sharing a common foundation** (Bauhaus, minimalism, conceptual art) — multiple fields sharing *common aesthetic principles*
4. **Technological medium integration** (cinema, VR, generative AI, NFT) — a new medium as the *stage integrating all fields*
5. **Meta-criticism** (Greenberg, Adorno, Baudrillard) — one field's criticism *reinterpreting another field*

### Q3-γ — The creation mechanism (Boden 1990, *The Creative Mind*)

Margaret Boden's 3 creativities:
1. **Combinatorial** — new combination of existing elements (collage, remix, sampling)
2. **Exploratory** — exploring new possibilities within a style (Bach fugue, late Picasso cubism)
3. **Transformational** — transforming the style itself (Schoenberg's 12-tone, Duchamp's Fountain, Stable Diffusion)

→ **Mathematical parallel**:
- Combinatorial = combinatorics ($\binom{n}{k}$, permutation)
- Exploratory = exploring the style *state space* (Markov, MCMC)
- Transformational = transforming the style *space itself* (group action, categorical transformation)

→ **Cross-field influence** is *transformational creativity* — one field's style transforms *another field's style space itself*. Mathematically: a *categorical functor* (e.g., cubist painting functor → literary style → modernist novel).

### Q3-δ — Cross-field influence in the *generative AI era*

Since 2022, Stable Diffusion, Sora, MusicLM, Suno, etc., *integrate all modalities*. Cross-field influence is realized as *AI multi-modality*:

- text → image (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, DALL-E)
- text → music (MusicLM, Suno, Udio)
- text → video (Sora, Veo, Kling)
- image → text (CLIP, BLIP, LLaVA)
- music → image (synesthesia AI)
- *all → all* (GPT-4o, Gemini, Claude multi-modal)

→ **An era in which cross-field influence is *automated by AI***. Dohun's most important mechanism (Q3) proceeds *in earnest in the 21st century* — AI accelerates the *work of building bridges between fields* that human artists do.

## Matrix of open problems (the 3 meta-questions themselves)

Self-identified by this note — *the core open problems of the Art domain*:

1. **Q1 hard problem of consciousness** — all 4-layer answers leave the essence of *subjective pleasure* unanswered
2. **Q2 universal criteria of beauty vs cultural relativism** — Zeki's neural universals vs Bourdieu's cultural separation, incomplete
3. **Q2 the *completeness* of the mathematical measures** — Birkhoff, Schmidhuber, Berlyne are all *partial*, a *complete measure* incomplete
4. **Q3 *mathematical refinement* of cross-field influence** — synesthesia, ekphrasis, etc. are *intuitive mappings*, an *exact function* incomplete
5. **Q3 the mechanism of transformational creativity** — Boden's 1990 classification OK, the *concrete mechanism* unanswered
6. **The *genuine creativity* of AI art** — the *transformational* possibility of Stable Diffusion, Sora unresolved
7. **The *mathematical balance* of diversity preservation vs average statistics (U-22)** — a tool for *tracking both dimensions simultaneously* is incomplete
8. **The *loss/gain* of *translation* between fields** — quantifying the *information preservation/generation* of ekphrasis, illustration is incomplete
9. **The *cultural invariance* of neuroaesthetics** — Zeki's V4 color universal vs the cultural relativity of color meaning (e.g., white = wedding/funeral, East–West difference)
10. **The *evolutionary adaptive function* of art** — Darwin's sexual selection, Dissanayake's artification hypothesis remain unverified
