## §1. Wagner's *Tristan und Isolde* (1859)

### 1-α — The Tristan chord

- Measure 1 of the prelude: **F-B-D♯-G♯** (enharmonic spelling)
- Notation: half-diminished seventh + appoggiatura
- *unresolved* — 19th-century traditional functional harmony *reaching its limit*

### 1-β — Mathematical analysis

- Traditional functional harmony: $T_n \to S_n \to D_n \to T_n$ (tonic-subdominant-dominant)
- The Tristan chord: *resolving nowhere* — the *absence* of the functional form
- Ultimately the foundation of Schoenberg's atonality (case applications 3 §1)
- pitch-class set theory: {0,3,6,9} or the set [0,1,4,7,10]

### 1-γ — The influence of Schopenhauer + Nietzsche

- The foundation of Schopenhauer's *Will*·Nietzsche's *Tragödie*
- Wagner's *Gesamtkunstwerk* (film VR AI integration §6)
- → Adorno's defense (the start of modernism) — Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard §2

### 1-δ — Q3 cross-field influence

- → Mahler·Strauss·Debussy·Schoenberg (music)
- → Symbolism (visual·literature) — Mallarmé·Beardsley
- → the foundation of the *whole* of 20th-century modernism

## §5. Kurosawa Akira's *Rashomon* (1950)

### 5-α — The work

- The 1950 Venice Golden Lion·the 1951 Academy Honorary Award in the foreign-language film category
- Combining Akutagawa's 1922 short stories *In a Grove* 藪の中 + *Rashōmon* 羅生門
- 4 narrators (the bandit·the wife·the husband through a medium·the woodcutter) — *4 different accounts of the same event*

### 5-β — The Rashomon effect — epistemological multi-layering

- Is truth *plural*, or *absent*?
- Narrator reliability·memory·self-protection — all differ
- → directly into cognitive psychology·law·historiography·post-modern theory

### 5-c — Mathematical parallel

- 4 narrators = 4 *partial views* of the *underlying event*
- The Rashomon effect = *non-unique parameter estimation* (statistics)
- an *underdetermined system* (Math 02) — 4 equations, the truth unknown
- → Bayesian inference·posterior diversity

### 5-d — Integrating Japanese aesthetics + Western film grammar

- The influence of the Noh style·*mono no aware*·*yūgen*
- The integration of Hollywood + Japan (non western deep integration §3-2)
- Mifune Toshirō's *physical acting* — a Noh parallel

### 5-ε — Q3 cross-field + Deleuze

- A direct case of the *time-image* (Deleuze, Spinoza-Deleuze immanence §4)
