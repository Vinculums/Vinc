# Johann Sebastian Bach

## ① One-line essence

**"Music is for the glory of God and the refreshment of the soul"** (in Bach's own hand, *Soli Deo Gloria* — signed on every score) — the summit of mathematical proportion and counterpoint + the musical manifestation of Lutheran faith.

## ② Life and historical context (4 axes)

- **Life**: 1685 Eisenach (Thuringia) — 1750 Leipzig. A musical family of six generations (the Bach clan produced 50+ composers). Confined to Germany his whole life (never experiencing Italy or France) · a lifelong salaried servant (Arnstadt · Mühlhausen · Weimar · Köthen · Leipzig St. Thomas Kantor 1723–1750).
- **Cognition**: *extreme cognition of polyphony and counterpoint* — tracking 4–6 voices simultaneously at once + a conscious awareness of mathematical proportion
- **Evolution**: the *historical summit* of polyphonic music (Palestrina · Lassus in the 16th century → Bach in the 18th century) — after which the style shifted into the homophonic era (Mozart · Haydn)
- **Culture**: Lutheran Germany + Baroque doctrine of the affections (Affektenlehre) + rhetoric — music as an art that *speaks*
- **Society**: feudal salaried service (serving king · church · city) — Beethoven the summit of the free artist ↔ Bach the summit of the servant artist. Never wealthy in his lifetime, and *largely forgotten* for 80 years after his death (Mendelssohn's 1829 revival of the *St. Matthew Passion*)

## ③ Major works and methodology

**Representative works**:
- *Brandenburg Concertos* (1721) — 6 concertos
- *The Well-Tempered Clavier* (1722 · 1742) — fugues and preludes in 24×2 keys = the founding of well temperament
- *St. Matthew Passion* (1727) — the summit of sacred music
- *Mass in B minor* (1749)
- *Goldberg Variations* (1741) — 32 variations
- *The Art of Fugue* (1750, unfinished) — the abstract summit of the fugue
- *The Musical Offering* (1747)

**Distinctive methodology**:
- **Counterpoint**: the ultimate refinement of independent melodic lines advancing simultaneously — a single subject transformed at once by *retrograde · retrograde inversion · augmentation · diminution · transposition*
- **Fugue**: subject → answer → countersubject → development → recapitulation — the summit of polyphonic form
- **Well temperament**: modulation through all 24 keys made possible — *equality among the keys* (a 200-year forerunner of Schoenberg's twelve-tone method)
- **Mathematical proportion**: bar counts · meters · intervals carrying *numerology* + theological meaning (3 = the Trinity · 14 = the alphabetical sum of B-A-C-H) — a hypothesis held by some scholars
- **Lutheran faith**: *Soli Deo Gloria* — all music is theological

## ④ 🔴 Cross-field influence and creation (MM-13 acceleration)

**Influence within music**:
- Mozart · Beethoven · Mendelssohn · Brahms · Schoenberg · Hindemith · Webern · Shostakovich — the *common foundation* of every subsequent composer
- Mendelssohn's 1829 revival of the *St. Matthew Passion* → the beginning of the 19th-century *Bach revival*
- **Schoenberg's twelve-tone method** (1921, music-foundations) = the *logical successor* to Bach's well temperament
- **Jazz** (Charlie Parker · John Coltrane's *Giant Steps*) — harmonic progressions as a direct application of Bach's counterpoint
- **Modern music** — Glenn Gould's 1955 *Goldberg* recording = the definitive 20th-century Bach interpretation

**Music → influence on other fields**:
- **Mathematics**: Hofstadter's *Gödel, Escher, Bach* (1979) — formal systems · self-reference + a parallel to Bach's fugues (Pulitzer Prize)
- **Computer science**: algorithmic composition · AI music generation (David Cope's EMI, 1980s — the first full-scale imitation of Bach's style)
- **Architecture**: the structure of the Goldberg Variations inspiring architectural proportion (in part in Le Corbusier's *Modulor*)
- **Visual art**: visualizing Bach's fugues (Wassily Kandinsky's auditory-visual synaesthesia, returning to neuroaesthetics · comparative-arts)
- **Mathematical patterns**: the Fibonacci · golden-ratio hypothesis (debated among scholars)

## ⑤ Answers to the meta-questions

- **Q1 (why define beauty?)**: *to glorify God* + *to refresh the soul* — a theological answer. Beauty = the sonic manifestation of divine order
- **Q2 (by what criterion?)**: *mathematical proportion + contrapuntal refinement + theological meaning + emotional precision (Affektenlehre)*. The musical summit of Plato's Idea + proportion (aesthetics)
- **Q3 (cross-field influence and creation)**: within music, the *foundation of all successors* + crossing fields (mathematics · CS · architecture · the visual) — *form itself* as the cross-field mechanism

## ⑥ Connections and successors

- Precursors: Palestrina · Buxtehude (whom Bach visited on foot, a 200 km walk in 1705) · Vivaldi (Bach arranged his concertos)
- Contemporaries: Handel (born the same year, 1685, never met in their lifetimes) · Telemann · Scarlatti
- Direct successors: his sons C.P.E. Bach · J.C. Bach (an inspiration to Mozart)
- 19th-century successors: Mendelssohn's revival · Brahms · Wagner (absorbing counterpoint)
- 20th-century successors: Schoenberg's twelve-tone method · Webern · Shostakovich
- Crossings: Hofstadter's *GEB* · Gould's interpretation · jazz harmony
