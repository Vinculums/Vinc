## §2. Cross-cultural cross-arts mutual influence

### 2-α — Eastern → Western influence (19th–20th centuries)

- **Japonisme** (1860–1900) — Manet, Whistler, Van Gogh, Klimt — *Eastern art launching Western modernism*
- **Cage** (1912–1992) — Suzuki's *Zen* + *I Ching* influence → *4'33"*, *Music of Changes* (chance)
- **Tobey** (1890–1976) — Japanese calligraphy → influence on action painting (foundation for Pollock)
- **Glass, Reich, LaMonte Young** — Indian raga + African polyrhythm + Balinese gamelan → minimalism
- **Sondheim** *Pacific Overtures* (1976) — Japanese kabuki + American musical

→ **20th-century Western modernism + postmodernism = *full-fledged absorption and integration of the non-Western***. *Cross-field influence + cross-cultural influence simultaneously*.

### 2-β — Western → Eastern influence

- **Meiji Japan** (1868–) — integration of Western-style painting (洋画 yōga) + Japanese painting (日本画 nihonga)
- **China's May Fourth Movement** (1919) — absorption of Western literature (Tolstoy, Nietzsche, Marx)
- **Contemporary K-pop** — American R&B/hip-hop + Korean tradition + Japanese anime visuals + Latin rhythm = *integration of 5 cultural spheres*

### 2-γ — 21st-century *AI-era cross-cultural*

- **Stable Diffusion, Midjourney** — training data *Western-dominant* (English, American museums) → cultural bias
- **Multi-lingual LLMs** (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini) — attempt to *learn all cultures equally* (though English-dominant)
- **AI music (MusicLM, Suno)** — Western pop/classical dominant → non-Western reinforcement needed
- **The *global hegemony* of K-pop/J-pop/C-pop** — the *first full-fledged case in history* of Eastern → Western influence

## §3. Rasa in earnest — the *mathematical parallel* of Indian aesthetics

| Rasa | Emotion | Color | Deity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sringara | love | dark blue | Vishnu |
| Hasya | laughter | white | Shiva |
| Karuna | sorrow | dove gray | Yama |
| Raudra | anger | red | Rudra |
| Vira | courage | yellow-orange | Indra |
| Bhayanaka | fear | black | Kala |
| Bibhatsa | disgust | blue | Mahakala |
| Adbhuta | wonder | yellow | Brahma |

→ **Mathematical parallel**: the *9 emotions = a classificatory coordinate system for aesthetic experience*. A parallel to Western analytic aesthetics (Goodman's *symbol system*). *Partial consistency*: the color mapping is *India-specific* (a synesthesia parallel), and the universal question remains unresolved.

### 3-β — Rasa 8 vs 9 (the dispute over adding Shanta)

- Bharata's original text: **8 rasa**
- Abhinavagupta, 10th century: **adds a 9th, Shanta (serenity)** — the absolute emotion, spiritual liberation
- → the *historical evolution* is *school-dependent*

### 3-γ — The Bhava–Rasa relation

- **Bhava** (an emotional state) → **Rasa** (emotion generalized into aesthetic experience)
- $\text{Bhava} \to \text{Rasa}$: a *function* (individual emotion → universal aesthetic experience)
- Mathematically: an *equivalence class* — equivalent emotions → the same rasa

## §4. East Asian aesthetics in earnest — integration of Zen, Dao, and Confucianism

### 4-α — Zen *no-mind* in earnest

- **無心 (mushin)** — the core of Zen aesthetics. *Emptying the mind* = *the highest state of art*
- **頓悟** (sudden enlightenment) vs **漸修** (gradual cultivation) — the two modes of aesthetic awakening
- **Japanese tea ceremony 茶道, calligraphy 書道, flower arranging 華道** — every art with *道* attached = a *practice of self-cultivation*
- Parallel to **Schmidhuber's compression progress / Friston's free energy** — *no-mind = minimal surprise* as a candidate

### 4-β — Landscape painting *三遠* (three distances)

- Guo Xi's *林泉高致* (Northern Song, 11th century) — 3 modes of perspective:
  - **高遠** (high distance) — looking up from the foot of the mountain (emphasizing height)
  - **深遠** (deep distance) — looking from the front of the mountain to the back (emphasizing depth)
  - **平遠** (level distance) — looking far across flat ground (emphasizing calm)
- **Western one-point perspective vs 三遠** — two *entirely different coordinate systems*
- Mathematically: the West = a $\mathbb{P}^2$ projection (a point vanishing point), the East = a *fluid viewpoint* (multiple viewpoints) — non-projective

### 4-c — 餘韻·餘白 (resonance, blank)

- In calligraphy, landscape painting, and poetry alike, *emptiness matters more than filling*
- Mathematical parallel: *negative space* — the absence of information is *part of the information*
- **Shannon entropy parallel**: 0 = silence = a *container* of information
- Cage's *4'33"* is an Eastern parallel (Suzuki's influence)

## §5. Islamic aesthetics in earnest — Tawhid, tessellation

### 5-α — Tawhid (oneness) aesthetics

- *All beauty reflects the unity of God* — the 99 names of Allah are the *archetype* of beauty
- **Prohibition of figural representation** → abstraction in earnest (1000 years ahead of Western modernism)
- → calligraphy, arabesque, geometric pattern — the *three principal styles*

### 5-β — The Alhambra and the 17 wallpaper groups

- **The Alhambra** (Granada, Spain, 14th century) — *uses all 17 wallpaper groups* (reached empirically)
- Fedorov's 1891 *classification of the 17 wallpaper groups* — the Alhambra had *reached them all before the mathematical classification*
- Mathematically: the *classification of finite subgroups* of $\text{Sym}(\mathbb{R}^2)$ — Islamic artisans *realized it before the theorem*

### 5-c — Penrose tiling parallel

- 1974: Penrose's five-fold aperiodic tiling
- Islamic five-fold symmetry (Gunbad-i Kabud, 1197) — 800 years ahead of Penrose (Lu–Steinhardt 2007, *Science*)
- Mathematically: aperiodic + 5-fold = quasi-crystal (Shechtman, 2011 Nobel Prize in Chemistry)

### 5-d — Sufi whirling dance (whirling dervish)

- **Mevlana Rumi**, 13th century — the *sema* ritual
- Bodily rotation = union with *celestial motion* (a parallel to Pythagoras's *Musica Universalis*)
- Mathematically: an $SO(2)$ group action — rotational symmetry

## §6. African and Latin aesthetics — polyrhythm, body, community

### 6-α — Polyrhythm in earnest

- West African drum ensembles — 3+ independent rhythms at once
- Mathematically: *simultaneous independent Markov chains* + *period LCM*
- Reich's *Drumming* (1971), Steve Coleman's M-Base — direct absorption
- *2 vs 3*, *4 vs 5*, *polymeter* — essentially different from the West's 4/4, 3/4

### 6-β — Mask, body, community

- *Art = part of ritual* (not separated) — universal before the West's *autonomy of art*
- Mathematically: art is *embedded in community function* — *not separable*

### 6-c — Latin — the mixed aesthetics of mestizaje

- Latin = indigenous + European + African = a *mixture of 3 cultures*
- *magical realism* (a parallel to García Márquez, Borges) — *mixture in earnest* aesthetically
- *bossa nova, salsa, tango* — multicultural music

## §7. The age of East–West integration (20th–21st centuries) — authenticity vs appropriation

### 7-α — appropriation vs respect

- **cultural appropriation** disputes — when the West *borrows the non-Western*, there is a risk of *stripping meaning and context*

### 7-β — Hybridity

- **Homi Bhabha**, *The Location of Culture* (1994) — the *third space* in earnest
- *global + local* = glocal — K-pop, anime, Latin music
- Mathematically: *colimit, pushout* — integrating the *common part + difference* of two cultures

### 7-c — Decolonizing aesthetics

- **Salikoko Mufwene, Walter Mignolo** — *dismantling the centrality of Western aesthetics*
