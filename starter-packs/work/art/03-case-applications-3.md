## §1. Schoenberg — Atonality·12-Tone

### 1-α — Works·evolution

- *Verklärte Nacht* 1899 (late Romantic)
- *Pierrot lunaire* 1912 — *Sprechgesang*·atonal·expressionism
- *Suite for Piano Op.25* 1923 — **the first fully 12-tone-technique** work
- *Moses und Aron* 1932 (unfinished)

### 1-β — Mathematical analysis — the 12-tone group

- **row** $P_0 = (n_0, n_1, \ldots, n_{11})$, $n_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{12}$ all distinct
- Transformations:
  - $P_n$ (transposition): $P_n(i) = P_0(i) + n \mod 12$
  - $I_n$ (inversion): $I_n(i) = n - P_0(i) \mod 12$
  - $R_n$ (retrograde): $R_n(i) = P_n(11-i)$
  - $RI_n$ (retrograde-inversion)
- → **48 row forms** = $12 \times 4$ (12 transpositions each of P·I·R·RI)

### 1-γ — Group action

- $\text{group} = D_{12} \times \mathbb{Z}_2 = $ dihedral × retrograde
- $|D_{12} \times \mathbb{Z}_2| = 48$
- → the 12-tone system = the action of a regular matrix group over $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$

### 1-δ — Adorno's defense·critique

- **Adorno** *Philosophy of New Music* 1949 — defending Schoenberg·criticizing Stravinsky
- *truth content* — reflecting society's *negativity*
- → Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard §2-2 directly

### 1-ε — Neuroaesthetics·the limit of reception

- Listener rejection of atonal music → even after learning, *the far end of Berlyne's inverted-U*
- 'fingerprint' learning (Krumhansl) — conscious recognition of the 12-tone row is difficult
- Yet its *historical shock·subsequent influence* is immense

### 1-ζ — Cross-field influence (Q3)

- → Webern·Berg·post-WWII serialism (Boulez·Stockhausen·Babbitt)
- → Mondrian·de Stijl (a painterly parallel) — contemporaneous modernism
- → Hofstadter's *GEB* (the integration of literature·mathematics·music)

## §2. Rothko — Color Field·Spiritual

### 2-α — Evolution of the work

- Expressionism 1940s → multiforms 1946–1949 → settling into **color field 1949–**
- *Untitled (Black on Grey)* 1969·*Black on Maroon* 1958
- **Rothko Chapel** (Houston 1971) — 14 black paintings·non-denominational

### 2-β — Form·neuroaesthetics

- 2–3 large color planes·soft edges·a *hovering* feeling
- **Visual-cortex response**: V4 (color)·MT (motion)·low spatial frequency
- *border halation* — Rothko's own intention

### 2-γ — Aesthetic measure

- The left side of *Berlyne's inverted-U* (low complexity) — yet *deep* aesthetic value
- *Friston's free energy* — *minimal surprise + a meditative state*
- *meditation art* — a parallel to Eastern Zen 禪 (non western deep integration §4-α)

### 2-δ — Greenberg's formalism

- Greenberg canonized abstract expressionism — Rothko·Pollock·Newman·Still
- *medium specificity* — painting = the essence of flatness·color
- → Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard §1-α directly

### 2-ε — Spiritual·non-denominational

- Rothko himself *non-religiously spiritual*
- Rothko Chapel = *a modern meditation space* — accommodating Quaker meeting·zen·sufi alike
- A parallel to Eastern aesthetics (Zen 禪 *no-mind*·Daoist *wu wei* 無爲)

### 2-ζ — Cross-field influence

- → minimalism (Reinhardt·Newman·Stella)
- → Cage's *4'33"*·Feldman's *Rothko Chapel* 1971 (a work of the same name)
- → meditation art·environmental art

## §3. Borges — Infinity·Mirrors·the Library

### 3-α — Works

- *La Biblioteca de Babel* 1941 — the infinite library
- *El Aleph* 1949 — the infinite point
- *Pierre Menard, Autor del Quijote* 1939 — the same text by a different author
- *El jardín de senderos que se bifurcan* 1941 — branching time

### 3-β — Mathematical parallel

- **Library of Babel** — every possible 410-page book = $25^{1312000}$ volumes (combinatorics·Math 06)
- **El Aleph** — the entire universe contained in an infinite point (Cantor·Math 01 §Act 1)
- **Garden of Forking Paths** — branching time = the game tree·many-worlds (Math 03·07)
- **Pierre Menard** — the same text as identical *yet not equivalent* = non-trivial equivalence (Math 01 ZFC)

### 3-γ — Influence — Foucault·deconstruction·the post-modern

- Foucault's *Les mots et les choses* 1966 — the first chapter quotes Borges (the Chinese animal taxonomy)
- The *episteme* citation — the *contingency·cultural dependence* of classification itself
- → the foundation of deconstruction·post-structuralism

### 3-δ — Mathematics·CS directly

- The *Library of Babel* followed by the *Library of Babel website* (Jonathan Basile 2015) — an algorithmic implementation
- The possibility of a parallel between the infinite library ↔ AlphaProof·Mathlib

### 3-ε — Cross-field influence

- → Calvino's *Invisible Cities*·*Cosmicomics*

## §5. Hilma af Klint — Mystical Abstraction

### 5-α — Works·history

- ***Paintings for the Temple*** (1906–1915) — a series of 193 works
- Born in Sweden 1862·died 1944
- First exhibited 20 years after her death, in 1986 (LA *The Spiritual in Art*)
- The 2018–2019 Guggenheim retrospective — *historical rediscovery* in earnest

### 5-β — Abstraction *several years ahead of* Kandinsky·Mondrian

- 1907 *The Ten Largest* — ahead of Kandinsky's first abstraction of 1910
- But *no exhibitions·a will sealing the works for 20 years after death*
- → a direct case of the critique of canon-making power (Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard §6)

### 5-γ — Mysticism·Theosophy

- Blavatsky·Steiner·a medium of the group of five (De Fem)
- Her own paintings = *spiritual transmission*
- Color·geometry·letters·nature motifs

### 5-δ — Mathematical parallel

- **circle·spiral·triangle** — basic figures (Math 04 geometry)
- Color codes (yellow·blue·rose) → spiritual mapping (a synesthesia parallel)
- Series structure = order + period ($\mathbb{Z}_n$)

### 5-ε — Feminist·post-canon reinterpretation

- A direct case for Nochlin's *Why no great women artists?* 1971 (canon critique)
- Pollock's *Vision and Difference* 1988
- → Greenberg Adorno Frankfurt Foucault Baudrillard §5 feminist directly

### 5-ζ — Cross-field influence

- Music: Scriabin's *Mysterium* (1903–, unfinished) — a mysticism·synesthesia parallel
