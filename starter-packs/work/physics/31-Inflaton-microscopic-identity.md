# inflaton

The microscopic identity of the *inflaton field* $\phi$ of Cosmology inflation — the 5 candidates: Starobinsky $R^2$ inflation, Higgs inflation, **axion monodromy** (String Theory moduli), α-attractors, and string moduli + the compatibility crisis with the Swampland dS conjecture / TCC + the Static Patch Holography static patch dS combination. **The 2014 BICEP2 false discovery** (a fake $r$ signal) + **the 2018 OOSV dS conjecture crisis** + **the 2019 Bedroya-Vafa TCC ($\Delta N < \ln(M_{\rm pl}/H)$, the natural 60 e-fold limit)** + **2024~ integration of axion monodromy + α-attractors with the swampland in progress**. **The last remaining P2 item (unremedied since the round-1 check) ✅** — *the microscopic identity of the inflation of our universe* is studied last, outside the full quantum-gravity domain.

## Physical picture

**Why the microscopic identity of the inflaton is needed**:
- Cosmology inflation: exponential expansion of the early universe ($t \sim 10^{-35}$ s)
- $a(t) \propto e^{Ht}$, $N \approx 60$ e-folds
- quantum fluctuations $\delta\phi \sim H/2\pi$ seed the CMB anisotropy + galactic structure
- but *what is the inflaton field $\phi$?* — the microscopic identity is unsolved
- not a Standard Model particle (the Higgs is only a candidate)
- a **microscopic derivation from quantum gravity + string theory** is needed

**Analogy — a flashlight of unknown identity**:
- the early-universe flashlight (the inflaton) causes *exponential expansion*
- when the flashlight turns off (end of slow-roll), the ordinary universe
- we do not know the *working principle* of the flashlight — the microscopic identity

**The 5 candidate inflatons**:

### ① Starobinsky inflation ($R^2$)
- **Starobinsky 1980** — the first inflation model (a year before Guth 1981)
- action: $S = \int d^4x \sqrt{-g}\,(R + R^2/(6M^2))$
- the $R^2$ term is the *effective inflaton* (Einstein-frame transformation)
- slow-roll natural
- **Planck 2018 best fit** — the best-matching candidate
- can arise *naturally* from quantum-gravity corrections

### ② Higgs inflation (Bezrukov-Shaposhnikov 2008)
- the Standard Model Higgs $H$ + non-minimal coupling $\xi |H|^2 R$
- $\xi \sim 10^4$ — a large coupling
- unification of the Standard Model + inflation
- but a unitarity limit (the EFT breaks for large $\xi$)
- **the only candidate that is a *known particle***

### ③ Axion monodromy (Silverstein-Westphal 2008)
- the axion of String Theory (4-form flux on an extra-dimension cycle)
- the periodicity $\phi \to \phi + 2\pi f$ broken by monodromy
- *naturally* enables large-field inflation ($\Delta\phi > M_{\rm pl}$)
- a direct string-theory candidate
- crisis with the **24 Swampland Distance conjecture** (an infinite light tower at large $\phi$)

### ④ α-attractors (Kallosh-Linde 2013)
- a flat plateau potential
- $V(\phi) = V_0 \tanh^2(\phi/\sqrt{6\alpha})$
- the parameter $\alpha$ unifies diverse inflation models
- conformal symmetry natural
- arises naturally in string theory / SUGRA

### ⑤ String moduli (KKLT-like)
- the *moduli scalars* of string-theory compactification
- Kähler moduli, complex-structure moduli, dilaton
- KKLT 2003 — moduli stabilization
- inflaton = a light modulus
- *countless possibilities* — the landscape ($10^{500}$)

**Slow-roll conditions**:
- $\epsilon = (M_{\rm pl}^2/2)(V'/V)^2 \ll 1$
- $\eta = M_{\rm pl}^2 V''/V \ll 1$
- inflation when $\epsilon, \eta < 1$
- **e-fold number**: $N = \int_{\phi_{\rm end}}^{\phi_*} d\phi/M_{\rm pl}\sqrt{2\epsilon}$

**Observational parameters** (Planck 2018):
- Spectral index $n_s = 0.965 \pm 0.004$
- Tensor-to-scalar ratio $r < 0.06$ (95% C.L.)
- $\Delta N \approx 60$ (CMB fit)
- **Starobinsky has the highest consistency** (both $n_s$ and $r$ match)

**Crisis with the Swampland dS conjecture**:
- $|\nabla V|/V \geq c \sim O(1)$
- for inflation to *work exactly*, $|\nabla V|/V \ll 1$ (slow-roll)
- **contradiction** — are inflation models in the swampland?
- **Responses**:
  - Refined dS (Andriot 2018): $\min \nabla^2 V / V \leq -c'$ — saddles allowed
  - a quintessence-inflation candidate
  - possible fit of the α-attractors

**The Swampland TCC (Bedroya-Vafa 2019)**:
- $\Delta N < \ln(M_{\rm pl}/H)$
- our universe $H_{\rm inf} \sim 10^{14}$ GeV → $\Delta N < 56$ — *collides with 60 e-folds*
- **the natural inflation limit 56~60** — the TCC is precise
- the compatibility of slow-roll + TCC *constrains* the inflaton candidates
- the α-attractors may be TCC-compatible

**Static Patch Holography dS combination**:
- inflation = *transient dS* (transient = the TCC limit)
- static patch holography sharpens the inflationary dS
- Penington-Witten 2024 — static patch + Page curve
- combination of inflation moduli + the dS algebra (CLPW 2022)

**Lyth bound** (Lyth 1997):
- $\Delta\phi/M_{\rm pl} \geq \sqrt{r/8}\,\Delta N$
- if $r > 0.01$ then $\Delta\phi > M_{\rm pl}$ — large-field
- Distance conjecture (24): a light tower at large $\Delta\phi$
- a verifiable signal (CMB B-mode polarization)

**Page curve / Replica wormholes inflation information paradox**:
- reheating after inflation → information conserved?
- the macroscopic conversion of quantum fluctuations = *measurement* (Polchinski 1989, Kiefer 1998)
- can replica wormholes apply to the inflationary universe?
- in progress

**Eternal inflation vs TCC**:
- Eternal inflation: infinite e-folds (Linde 1986)
- TCC: a finite e-fold limit
- **collision** — is eternal inflation in the swampland?
- a crisis of the multiverse hypothesis

## Mathematical structure

**FRW + Inflaton**:
$$ds^2 = -dt^2 + a(t)^2 d\vec x^2$$
$$\rho_\phi = \frac{1}{2}\dot\phi^2 + V(\phi)$$
$$p_\phi = \frac{1}{2}\dot\phi^2 - V(\phi)$$
- $H^2 = (\rho_\phi + \rho_{\rm rad} + \cdots)/(3M_{\rm pl}^2)$
- Slow-roll: $\dot\phi^2 \ll V$, $w = -1$ (dS-like)

**Klein-Gordon equation in FRW**:
$$\ddot\phi + 3H\dot\phi + V'(\phi) = 0$$
- Slow-roll: $\ddot\phi \to 0$
- $3H\dot\phi \approx -V'(\phi)$

**Slow-roll parameters**:
$$\epsilon = \frac{M_{\rm pl}^2}{2}\left(\frac{V'}{V}\right)^2$$
$$\eta = M_{\rm pl}^2 \frac{V''}{V}$$

**E-fold number**:
$$N(\phi) = \int_{\phi_{\rm end}}^{\phi}\frac{d\phi'}{M_{\rm pl}\sqrt{2\epsilon}}$$

**Spectral index** (CMB):
$$n_s - 1 = -6\epsilon + 2\eta$$
- Planck 2018: $n_s = 0.965 \pm 0.004$
- $\epsilon \sim 0.005$, $\eta \sim -0.01$

**Tensor-to-scalar ratio**:
$$r = 16\epsilon$$
- Planck 2018: $r < 0.06$
- BICEP/Keck Array: $r < 0.036$ (2021)

**Lyth bound**:
$$\Delta\phi \geq M_{\rm pl}\sqrt{\frac{r}{8}}\,\Delta N$$
- $r = 0.01$, $\Delta N = 60$ → $\Delta\phi \geq 2 M_{\rm pl}$ (large-field)

**Starobinsky inflation**:
$$S = \frac{M_{\rm pl}^2}{2}\int d^4x \sqrt{-g}\,\left(R + \frac{R^2}{6M^2}\right)$$
- Conformal transformation → Einstein frame:
$$V(\phi) = \frac{3}{4}M_{\rm pl}^2 M^2 \left(1 - e^{-\sqrt{2/3}\phi/M_{\rm pl}}\right)^2$$
- Slow-roll plateau

**Higgs inflation**:
$$S = \int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{M_{\rm pl}^2 + \xi |H|^2}{2}R - \cdots\right]$$
- Einstein frame:
$$V(\phi) \approx \frac{\lambda M_{\rm pl}^4}{4\xi^2}\left(1 - e^{-\sqrt{2/3}\phi/M_{\rm pl}}\right)^2$$
- the same form as Starobinsky!

**Axion monodromy**:
$$V(\phi) = \mu^3 \phi + \Lambda^4 \cos(\phi/f) + \cdots$$
- Linear (free without monodromy) + oscillatory (moduli effect)
- $\Delta\phi \gg M_{\rm pl}$ possible

**α-attractors**:
$$V(\phi) = V_0 \tanh^2\left(\frac{\phi}{\sqrt{6\alpha}\,M_{\rm pl}}\right)$$
- $\alpha \to \infty$: chaotic inflation
- $\alpha \to 0$: the Starobinsky limit
- $n_s = 1 - 2/N$ (for all $\alpha$)
- $r = 12\alpha/N^2$ ($\alpha$ determined by observational bounds)

**TCC bound**:
$$\Delta N < \ln(M_{\rm pl}/H_{\rm inf})$$
- $H_{\rm inf} \sim 10^{14}$ GeV (max for observed $r$)
- $M_{\rm pl}/H \sim 10^4$ → $\Delta N < 9.2 \cdot \ln 10 \approx 21$ (overly strict)
- Refined: $\Delta N < (M_{\rm pl}/H)\ln$
- naturally collides with 60 e-folds

**dS conjecture vs slow-roll**:
- $|\nabla V|/V = \sqrt{2\epsilon} \geq c$
- Slow-roll: $\epsilon \ll 1$ → $\sqrt{2\epsilon} \ll 1$
- **contradiction** — slow-roll impossible if $c \sim O(1)$
- Refined: $|\nabla V|/V \geq c$ OR $\min \nabla^2 V/V \leq -c'$ — the latter possible

**Inflaton quantum fluctuations**:
$$\delta\phi \sim H/(2\pi)$$
- mode $k$: $\delta\phi_k = H/(2\pi)$ at horizon exit ($k = aH$)
- Frozen on super-horizon
- Re-enters horizon → classical perturbation

**Power spectrum**:
$$\mathcal P_{\zeta}(k) = \frac{1}{8\pi^2}\frac{H^2}{M_{\rm pl}^2 \epsilon}\bigg|_{k=aH}$$
- Curvature perturbation
- CMB $\Delta T/T \sim \mathcal P_\zeta^{1/2}/5 \sim 10^{-5}$
