# General Relativity

## One-line essence
Gravity is not a force but the curvature of spacetime — a freely falling object follows the geodesic of curved spacetime as if it were a straight line.

## Physical picture
**Einstein's happiest thought** (1907): a person in free fall does not feel their own weight. Inside a falling elevator there is weightlessness. Conversely, a person inside a spaceship uniformly accelerating at 1g in space and a person standing on the surface of the Earth are locally indistinguishable. **A gravitational field = an accelerating coordinate frame.** This is the equivalence principle.

Just one step further from here: in an accelerating frame light bends (obvious by SR) → so light bends in a gravitational field too → **light always travels straight at c, but if spacetime itself is curved, that straight line looks curved to us.** In 1919 Eddington confirmed this by measuring the 1.75″ deflection of starlight during a solar eclipse.

Analogy: a heavy ball on a trampoline dents the fabric, and a light marble rolls along that curved surface. But there is a catch — **it is not only space but spacetime that curves**. Most of the curvature of a planetary orbit is made not by spatial curvature but by **curvature in the time direction**. The fact that time flows differently at your head and your feet is the true mechanism pulling you toward the ground.

**The intuition of GPS**: the clock of a satellite at 20,000 km altitude runs faster than at the surface (weaker gravity). Adding SR (velocity → time slowing) and GR (weaker gravity → time faster) gives about 38 microseconds fast per day. Without correction, the position drifts by about 11 km in 24 hours. **Without GR, navigation is impossible.**

**The light cone tilts**: the closer to a black hole, the more severely spacetime curves and the light cone tilts inward. At the event horizon the light cone tilts completely inward and the future direction of escape disappears — this is the definition of a black hole.

## Mathematical structure
**Equivalence principle**: inertial mass = gravitational mass (Eötvös experiment, $10^{-13}$ precision)

**Spacetime = a pseudo-Riemannian manifold**, with the metric $g_{\mu\nu}(x)$ as the dynamical variable.

**Geodesic equation** (free fall = geodesic):
$$\frac{d^2 x^\mu}{d\tau^2} + \Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}\frac{dx^\alpha}{d\tau}\frac{dx^\beta}{d\tau} = 0$$

Christoffel symbol: $\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta} = \tfrac{1}{2}g^{\mu\lambda}(\partial_\alpha g_{\lambda\beta} + \partial_\beta g_{\lambda\alpha} - \partial_\lambda g_{\alpha\beta})$

**Riemann curvature tensor** (second derivative of the metric):
$$R^\rho_{\sigma\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu \Gamma^\rho_{\nu\sigma} - \partial_\nu \Gamma^\rho_{\mu\sigma} + \Gamma^\rho_{\mu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda_{\nu\sigma} - \Gamma^\rho_{\nu\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\sigma}$$

Contractions: Ricci tensor $R_{\mu\nu} = R^\lambda_{\mu\lambda\nu}$, Ricci scalar $R = g^{\mu\nu}R_{\mu\nu}$.

**Einstein field equations** (1915):
$$G_{\mu\nu} \equiv R_{\mu\nu} - \tfrac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$$

- Left side: spacetime geometry (curvature information), $\Lambda$ is the cosmological constant
- Right side: the distribution of matter and energy, $T_{\mu\nu}$ is the energy-momentum tensor
- Wheeler: **"Matter tells spacetime how to curve, and spacetime tells matter how to move."**

**Einstein-Hilbert action**:
$$S = \frac{c^4}{16\pi G}\int R\sqrt{-g}\, d^4x + S_{\text{matter}}$$

The result of applying the variational principle of Lagrangian Mechanics to spacetime geometry itself.

**Schwarzschild solution** (1916, static, spherically symmetric, vacuum):
$$ds^2 = -\left(1 - \frac{r_s}{r}\right)c^2 dt^2 + \left(1 - \frac{r_s}{r}\right)^{-1} dr^2 + r^2 d\Omega^2$$

- $r_s = 2GM/c^2$ (Schwarzschild radius) → the event horizon
- Gravitational time dilation: $d\tau = \sqrt{1 - r_s/r}\, dt$
- Gravitational redshift: $\Delta\nu/\nu = -GM/(rc^2)$ — verified by Pound-Rebka 1959

**FLRW metric** (isotropic, homogeneous universe):
$$ds^2 = -c^2 dt^2 + a(t)^2\left[\frac{dr^2}{1-kr^2} + r^2 d\Omega^2\right]$$

Evolution of the scale factor $a(t)$ — the **Friedmann equation**:
$$\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho - \frac{kc^2}{a^2} + \frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}$$

The Big Bang, the CMB, and dark energy all come out of this.

**Gravitational waves** (linearization $g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu}$, $|h| \ll 1$):
$$\Box \bar h_{\mu\nu} = -\frac{16\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$$

Spacetime vibrating at c. This is what LIGO saw.

## Why it matters
**Resolving the incompatibility of Newtonian gravity + SR**: Newton has instantaneous action, SR has c as an absolute limit → incompatible. GR makes gravity a dynamical field of spacetime, naturally satisfying the c-limit.

Deeper positioning:
- **Cosmology = GR**: the Big Bang, the CMB, nucleosynthesis, structure formation, dark energy, inflation — the backbone of the Lambda-CDM standard model
- **Black holes**: the terminus of gravitational collapse. The event horizon, the singularity, the no-hair theorem, Hawking radiation ($T_H = \hbar c^3/(8\pi GMk_B)$, $S_{BH} = k_B A/(4l_P^2)$). The home ground of the Carnot ↔ BH entropy recognized in Statistical Mechanics
- **Gravitational-wave astronomy** (2015–): a new observational window since GW150914. Direct observation of BH-BH and NS-NS mergers invisible to optical and radio
- **Technological applications**: GPS, optical-atomic-clock synchronization, geodesy
- **Unsolved — quantum gravity**: GR (continuous spacetime) and QM (quantum fluctuations) are incompatible. At the Planck scale $l_P \approx 10^{-35}$ m, spacetime quantization is needed. String theory, LQG, CDT — only candidates, no answer. The greatest unsolved problem in physics
- **Geometry = physics**: Riemann's 1854 abstract geometry became, 60 years later, the real structure of gravity. The deepest meeting of mathematics and physics

## Historical context
**1907 Einstein** — while doing follow-up work to SR at the Bern patent office, "the happiest thought": free fall = weightlessness. The equivalence principle.

**1907–1915 — eight years of struggle**: it was daunting to figure out what mathematics could express general covariance. His friend Marcel Grossmann taught him Riemannian geometry (Ricci-Civita differential geometry). The 1913 "Entwurf" was nearly right but did not achieve general covariance. In November 1915, a four-week burst — a new paper every week, each result exactly correct. On November 25, the final field equations.

**The priority dispute with Hilbert**: Hilbert, starting from the variational principle, derived the identical equations at almost the same time (announced November 20). It is now regarded as independent and nearly simultaneous. Hilbert ceded priority to Einstein.

**1916 Schwarzschild** — derived the exact solution in a trench on the Russian front of the First World War. He died that year of an autoimmune disease.

**1919 Eddington** — an eclipse-observation expedition (the island of Príncipe). Measured the 1.75″ deflection of starlight (twice the Newtonian prediction). The next day's newspaper front page made Einstein a world hero.

**1929 Hubble** — the expansion of the universe. Einstein discarded the cosmological constant $\Lambda$, introduced for a static universe, as "the biggest blunder of my life." **Revived in 1998** — with Riess, Schmidt, and Perlmutter's discovery of accelerated expansion (2011 Nobel), $\Lambda$ returned to the core of the standard model.

**1939 Oppenheimer-Snyder** — gravitational collapse of a star → the prediction of black holes. At the time a mathematical curiosity.

**The GR renaissance of the 1960s–70s**: Penrose's singularity theorem (1965), Hawking (1970), Bekenstein-Hawking black hole entropy (1973), Hawking radiation (1974).

**1974 Hulse-Taylor** — the orbital decay of the binary pulsar PSR B1913+16 agreed within 0.2% with GR's prediction of gravitational-wave radiation → 1993 Nobel.

**September 14, 2015** — LIGO detected GW150914. Direct measurement of the spacetime vibration of two BHs merging. Direct confirmation after 100 years. 2017 Nobel.

**2019 EHT** — the first image of the shadow of the black hole at the center of M87. In 2022, Sgr A* at the center of our galaxy.
