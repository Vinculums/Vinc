# Cosmology

## One-line essence
Cosmology is the discipline that treats the entire universe as a single quantum-field-gravity system — on the homogeneous, isotropic spacetime given by the FLRW metric, the Big Bang, inflation, the CMB, structure formation, and dark energy are consistently explained, and quantum fluctuations are converted into the seeds of macroscopic structure.

## Physical picture
**The 4 basic facts of the universe**:
1. **Homogeneous and isotropic** (on large scales above 100 Mpc): it looks the same from any direction and any location
2. **Expanding**: galaxies recede from one another (Hubble 1929)
3. **CMB**: the most precise blackbody radiation (2.725 K), coming from the entire universe
4. **Accelerating expansion** (1998): the recession velocity grows faster with increasing distance

**Big Bang timeline** (13.8 billion years):
- $t \sim 0$: the quantum-gravity epoch ($t < 10^{-43}$ s, the Planck time). Unsolved
- $t \sim 10^{-36} \sim 10^{-32}$ s: **inflation** — the universe expands by a factor of $10^{26}$. Quantum fluctuations seed the macroscopic anisotropy
- $t \sim 10^{-12}$ s: electroweak symmetry breaking, the emergence of particle masses
- $t \sim 1$ s: neutrino decoupling, determination of the proton/neutron abundance
- $t \sim 3$ min: **Big Bang nucleosynthesis** — formation of hydrogen, helium, deuterium
- $t \sim 380{,}000$ yr: **CMB emission** — photons become able to move freely
- $t \sim 100$ million yr: first star formation, reionization
- $t \sim 13.8$ billion yr: the present

**The Lambda-CDM standard model — the composition of the universe**:
- 5% ordinary matter (stars, galaxies, planets, us)
- 27% dark matter (acts only through gravity, its identity unsolved)
- 68% dark energy (the cause of accelerating expansion, expressed by $\Lambda$)

**CMB anisotropy — the seed of all structure**: measured by COBE (1992), WMAP (2003), and Planck (2013). Minute temperature variations at the level of $\Delta T/T \sim 10^{-5}$. The acoustic peaks of this pattern precisely determine the 6 Lambda-CDM parameters.

**The magic of inflation**: for a very brief instant right after the Big Bang the universe expands explosively. It solves three problems at once:
1. **The horizon problem**: why do regions never causally connected have the same temperature? — before inflation they were close together
2. **The flatness problem**: why is the universe exactly flat? — the expansion irons the curvature to 0
3. **The magnetic-monopole problem**: why are none seen? — inflation dilutes them away

**The key mechanism**: during inflation the quantum fluctuations $\delta\phi \sim H/(2\pi)$ of the inflaton field $\phi$ are, after slow-roll, converted into macroscopic density fluctuations $\delta\rho/\rho$. **The microscopic fluctuations of quantum mechanics are the origin of the structure of the entire universe.** The largest stage where QFT (12) + GR (09) meet.

**The shock of dark energy**: before 1998 the universe was expected to expand while decelerating. Type Ia supernova observations showed the exact opposite → accelerating expansion → 68% of the universe is an unknown energy. **The cosmological constant $\Lambda$ that Einstein had discarded was resurrected.** QFT predicts a vacuum energy, but there is a **120-order-of-magnitude discrepancy** with the observed value — one of the greatest unsolved problems in physics.

**Analogy**: the universe is a giant laboratory. At scales of energy, temperature, curvature, and time we cannot produce, it simultaneously tests Quantum Field Theory + General Relativity. The CMB is the largest blackbody-radiation experiment, Big Bang nucleosynthesis is the largest nuclear-physics experiment, and the accelerating expansion is the largest precision measurement.

## Mathematical structure

**FLRW metric** (the most general form of a homogeneous, isotropic universe):
$$ds^2 = -c^2dt^2 + a(t)^2\left[\frac{dr^2}{1-kr^2} + r^2(d\theta^2 + \sin^2\theta\, d\phi^2)\right]$$
- $a(t)$: scale factor (normalized to $a_0 = 1$ today)
- $k = +1, 0, -1$: closed, flat, open universe (observation: $k \approx 0$)

**Friedmann equations** (the FLRW application of the General Relativity field equations):
$$\boxed{\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho - \frac{kc^2}{a^2} + \frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}}$$
$$\frac{\ddot a}{a} = -\frac{4\pi G}{3}\left(\rho + \frac{3p}{c^2}\right) + \frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}$$

**Equation of state** $p = w\rho c^2$:
- matter ($w = 0$): $\rho \propto a^{-3}$
- radiation ($w = 1/3$): $\rho \propto a^{-4}$
- cosmological constant ($w = -1$): $\rho$ constant — the energy density holds even as the volume grows
- condition for accelerating expansion: $w < -1/3$

**Hubble parameter**:
$$H \equiv \frac{\dot a}{a},\quad H_0 = ?$$
- Planck (CMB-based): $H_0 \approx 67.4 \pm 0.5$ km/s/Mpc
- SH0ES (local distance ladder): $H_0 \approx 73.0 \pm 1.0$ km/s/Mpc
- **a discrepancy above 5σ = the H₀ tension** (currently unsolved)

**Redshift**:
$$1 + z = \frac{a_{\rm now}}{a_{\rm emit}} = \frac{\lambda_{\rm obs}}{\lambda_{\rm emit}}$$
The larger $z$, the more distant and more ancient the universe.

**Critical density and density parameters**:
$$\rho_c = \frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G},\quad \Omega_i = \frac{\rho_i}{\rho_c}$$
Observation: $\Omega_m \approx 0.31$, $\Omega_\Lambda \approx 0.69$, $\Omega_k \approx 0$.

**Inflation — slow-roll parameters**:
$$\epsilon = \frac{m_P^2}{2}\left(\frac{V'}{V}\right)^2,\quad \eta = m_P^2\frac{V''}{V}$$
Inflation continues if $\epsilon, |\eta| \ll 1$. The ending condition is $\epsilon = 1$.

**Macroscopization of quantum fluctuations** (the combination of Quantum Field Theory + General Relativity):
$$\langle\delta\phi^2\rangle \sim \left(\frac{H}{2\pi}\right)^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\delta\rho}{\rho}\bigg|_{\rm horizon} \sim \frac{H^2}{2\pi\dot\phi}$$
The quantum fluctuations of a free scalar field in de Sitter space → conserved after slow-roll → appear as CMB anisotropy.

**CMB acoustic peaks — Lambda-CDM parameters**:
$$C_\ell = \frac{2}{\pi}\int dk\, k^2 |\Theta_\ell(k)|^2 P(k)$$
The position of the first peak ($\ell \approx 220$) → $\Omega_k$, the peak height → $\Omega_b$, the acoustic distance → $\Omega_m$.

**Big Bang nucleosynthesis** (BBN, about $t = 1$ s to 3 min):
$$\frac{n}{n+p} \approx \frac{1}{7},\quad Y_p \equiv \frac{4n_{He}}{n_b} \approx 0.247$$
The primordial helium abundance $Y_p$ depends strongly on $\Omega_b$ → a test of the Standard Model.

**Structure formation — Jeans instability**:
$$\lambda_J = c_s\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{G\rho}}$$
If $\lambda > \lambda_J$ it condenses → nonlinear → formation of galaxies and galaxy clusters.

## Why it matters

**A single formalism treating the entire universe as one system**:
- FLRW + Friedmann + Lambda-CDM = explains all cosmological observations with only 6 parameters
- the largest precision test bench — Planck parameter precision at the $10^{-3}$ level

**The essence of combining Quantum Field Theory + General Relativity**:
- inflation = quantum fluctuations of a scalar field on curved spacetime
- quantum microscopic fluctuations → macroscopic cosmic structure (the largest quantum→classical conversion in history)
- if Hawking radiation is the first case of QFT on curved spacetime, inflation is the most enormous case

**Testing Statistical Mechanics and the Standard Model via Big Bang nucleosynthesis**:
- the abundances of $^4$He, D, $^7$Li determine $\Omega_b$ → agreement independent of the CMB
- a single event in which Standard Model particle physics, nuclear physics, statistical mechanics, and GR are all tested simultaneously

**Determining the future and past of the universe**:
- if $w = -1$ (cosmological constant), eternal accelerating expansion → the cold death of the universe (Heat Death)
- if $w < -1$, the Big Rip
- before the Big Bang is unsolved quantum gravity

**Technological applications**:
- CMB measurement = the evolution of precision optics and quantum detection technology
- gravitational-wave astronomy (LIGO, LISA) — a new window for directly observing the universe
- cosmic-microwave-background detectors → multi-messenger astronomy

**Unsolved — the greatest problems in physics**:
- **the identity of dark matter**: WIMP, axion, primordial black hole candidates. Direct detection undetermined
- **the dark-energy / cosmological-constant problem**: a 120-order-of-magnitude discrepancy between QFT prediction and observation
- **the H₀ tension**: new-physics candidates (early dark energy, new neutrinos, modified gravity)
- **the microscopic identity of inflation**: which scalar field? the string-theory landscape?
- **multiverse vs single universe**: the anthropic-principle debate
- **before the Big Bang**: the quantum-gravity regime — a Big Bounce? eternal inflation? the genesis of the universe itself?

**Connections to other fields**:

## Historical context

**Act I — from a static universe to an expanding universe (1915–1929)**:
- 1915 Einstein — completion of GR
- 1917 Einstein — introduction of $\Lambda$ for a static universe (which he himself called his "greatest blunder")
- 1922 Friedmann — derivation of expanding/contracting solutions, refuting the static assumption
- 1927 Lemaître — independent discovery of Friedmann + interpretation of redshift as expansion
- **1929 Hubble** — galaxy recession velocity is proportional to distance. Observational confirmation of the expanding universe
- 1931 Einstein discards $\Lambda$. Lemaître proposes the "primeval atom" (the prototype of the Big Bang)
- **The decisive turn**: 1929 — accepting that the universe is a dynamical system. The static-universe assumption is permanently discarded. Thereafter all cosmology uses time-dependent models

**Act II — the Big Bang and the discovery of the CMB (1948–1989)**:
- 1948 Gamow-Alpher-Herman — Big Bang nucleosynthesis + a prediction of a 5K CMB
- 1948– Hoyle's steady-state universe model (an alternative hypothesis)
- **1965 Penzias-Wilson** — accidental discovery of the CMB (3K). Nobel 1978. **Decisive verification of the Big Bang hypothesis**
- 1965 Dicke-Peebles — the cosmological interpretation of the Penzias-Wilson signal
- 1980 Guth — proposal of **inflation** (solving the horizon, flatness, and magnetic-monopole problems at once)
- 1981– Linde, Albrecht-Steinhardt — new inflation
- 1989 COBE launched
- **The decisive turn**: 1965 — with the discovery of the CMB the Big Bang beat the steady state. The steady-state model is permanently discarded. Cosmology goes from conjecture to a precision science

**Act III — Lambda-CDM precision cosmology (1992–2013)**:
- **1992 COBE** — the first detection of CMB anisotropy ($10^{-5}$). Nobel 2006
- **1998 Riess-Schmidt-Perlmutter** — discovery of accelerating expansion via type Ia supernovae. **The resurrection of $\Lambda$.** Nobel 2011
- 2003 first WMAP results — a precise determination of the age of the universe at 13.7 billion years
- 2010s– **Planck** — the pinnacle of precision cosmology. The 6-parameter Lambda-CDM determined very accurately
- 2013 first Planck results, 2018 final results
- **The decisive turn**: the 1998 discovery of accelerating expansion — 68% of the universe is unknown dark energy. The path along which standard cosmology heads beyond the Standard Model becomes clear. The cosmological-constant problem rises as the greatest unsolved problem in physics

**Act IV — unsolved areas and quantum gravity (2010–)**:
- 2014 BICEP2 — claim of a detection of inflationary gravitational waves → refuted as a dust effect. Direct verification of inflation incomplete
- 2015 LIGO's first detection of gravitational waves, **2017 GW170817** — multi-messenger astronomy (NS-NS merger + optical)
- **the H₀ tension deepens** — Planck (67) vs SH0ES (73), a discrepancy above 5σ. New-physics candidates (early dark energy, new neutrinos, modified gravity)
- 2017 Maldacena-Susskind — ER=EPR (entanglement and spacetime geometry)
- attempts to apply AdS/CFT — quantum-gravity tools for inflation and dark energy
- 2022– JWST — observation of the abundance of early galaxies, a quantitative challenge to the Standard Model
- 2023– pulsar timing arrays — the ultra-low-frequency gravitational-wave background (NANOGrav)
- **The decisive turn**: 2010s– as precision cosmology stabilizes, the unsolved areas (H₀ tension, the identity of dark energy, the multiverse, before the Big Bang) converge onto quantum gravity. Cosmology becomes the largest laboratory of quantum gravity

**Key insight**: cosmology is a field that ran the full 4-stage course of **conjecture → discovery → precision science → new unsolved problems** in 100 years. That the $\Lambda$ Einstein introduced for a static universe and then discarded was resurrected 100 years later is a rare case of **a theory being overturned twice before observation.** Moreover, the universe is **the only stage where every field of physics is tested simultaneously** — SR, GR, QM, QFT, statistical mechanics, and nuclear physics must all be consistently explained within one system. If condensed matter was the small stage of emergence, the universe is the largest stage.
