# Quantum Information

## One-line essence
Quantum information is the discipline that treats quantum states as an information resource — qubits, entanglement, and superposition create a new resource that the classical does not possess; quantum computing, quantum cryptography, and quantum communication are its applications; and at the deepest theoretical level, entanglement entropy becomes equivalent to spacetime geometry (Ryu-Takayanagi, ER=EPR), becoming a central bridge of quantum gravity.

## Physical picture
**Qubit = the simplest quantum system**: the two states $|0\rangle, |1\rangle$ of spin 1/2. The decisive difference from a classical bit is **superposition** $\alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$ — a point on the Bloch sphere. The Pauli-matrix and rotation representations of Quantum Mechanics II are directly the single-qubit gates.

**Entanglement = the core resource of quantum information**: a Bell state such as $|\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}(|00\rangle + |11\rangle)$ is inseparable as a tensor product. Measuring one qubit instantly determines the other — a direct exploitation of the EPR and Bell violation of Quantum Mechanics. Independent of distance but not information transfer (no-signaling).

**The 4 key protocols**:
1. **No-cloning** (1982): an arbitrary quantum state cannot be copied. The security foundation of quantum cryptography
2. **Quantum teleportation** (1993): entanglement + 2 bits of classical communication → transfer of an unknown quantum state. The state moves, the particle does not
3. **BB84 quantum key distribution** (1984): Alice and Bob measure in random bases. If an eavesdropper measures, a trace is left. **Security guaranteed by physical law**
4. **Entanglement swapping and redistribution**: the basic unit of the quantum internet

**Quantum computing algorithms — the domain of classical superiority**:
- **Shor (1994)**: integer factorization in polynomial time. **2048-bit RSA breaks in front of a quantum computer.** The birth of the post-quantum cryptography industry
- **Grover (1996)**: unsorted search $O(\sqrt N)$. A square-root speedup for all NP problems
- **HHL** (Harrow-Hassidim-Lloyd 2009): quantum speedup for linear systems
- **Quantum simulation** (Feynman's 1982 vision): quantum many-body systems cost exponentially on a classical computer → polynomial on a quantum computer. Direct application to **quantum chemistry, materials, high energy**

**Quantum error correction — the real challenge of quantum computing**: qubits are quickly destroyed by decoherence with the environment. The solution: **encode one logical qubit with several physical qubits.** The Shor 9-qubit code, the Steane 7-qubit, and the **surface code** are the current standard. The surface code has the same structure as the **topological order** of Condensed Matter — the stabilizers on the lattice are the same as the lattice gauge theory of a condensed-matter topological phase.

**Topological qubit — a quantum-information application of condensed matter**: a Majorana fermion is its own antiparticle. Two Majorana pairs make one ordinary fermion, and the exchange between Majoranas is **non-abelian statistics** — the gates are topologically protected and robust against decoherence. Pushed by Microsoft·Delft from 2010, in the stage of decisive verification from 2024.

**The deepest level — quantum information ↔ quantum gravity**:
- **Ryu-Takayanagi formula (2006)**: in AdS/CFT the entanglement entropy of a boundary CFT region $A$ = the bulk minimal area / $4G\hbar$. **Entanglement is area**
- **ER=EPR (Maldacena-Susskind 2013)**: entanglement between two particles (EPR) ↔ a wormhole between two black holes (ER, Einstein-Rosen). **Entanglement is a spacetime bridge**
- **HaPPY code (2015)**: the bulk reconstruction of AdS/CFT has the same structure as a quantum error-correcting code
- **Page curve and the island formula (2019–)**: the quantum-information solution of the black-hole information paradox — using quantum-information tools for a problem Hawking could not solve

**Analogy — quantum information is the information interface of quantum mechanics**: if QM is "how nature works," quantum information is "how to use, transmit, and protect that working as a resource." And the possibility that this interface can become the spacetime itself of General Relativity is the deepest level.

## Mathematical structure

**Qubit state**:
$$|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle,\quad |\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1$$
Bloch representation: $|\psi\rangle = \cos(\theta/2)|0\rangle + e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)|1\rangle$, the point $(\theta, \phi)$ on the unit sphere.

**N-qubit Hilbert space**:
$$\mathcal H = (\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes N},\quad \dim = 2^N$$
**Exponential dimension** — even just 50 qubits cannot be simulated in classical memory ($2^{50} \approx 10^{15}$ complex numbers).

**Single-qubit gates** (Pauli + Hadamard + phase):
$$X = \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\ Y = \begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\ Z = \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\ H = \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\ T = \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&e^{i\pi/4}\end{pmatrix}$$

**CNOT gate** (entanglement generation):
$$\text{CNOT}|a, b\rangle = |a, a\oplus b\rangle$$
A universal gate set = $\{H, T, \text{CNOT}\}$ — approximates an arbitrary unitary (Solovay-Kitaev).

**Bell states** (the 4 maximally entangled):
$$|\Phi^\pm\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}(|00\rangle \pm |11\rangle),\quad |\Psi^\pm\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}(|01\rangle \pm |10\rangle)$$

**Entanglement measures**:
- von Neumann entanglement entropy: $S(\rho_A) = -\text{Tr}(\rho_A \log \rho_A)$, $\rho_A = \text{Tr}_B|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$
- maximal entanglement: $S = \log d_A$
- Concurrence (2 qubits): $C = \max(0, \lambda_1 - \lambda_2 - \lambda_3 - \lambda_4)$

**No-cloning theorem** (Wootters-Zurek 1982):
$$\text{no } U \text{ exists with } U(|\psi\rangle|0\rangle) = |\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle$$
Contradicts linearity. Proof: cloning two different states $|\psi\rangle, |\phi\rangle$ does not preserve the inner product.

**Shor's algorithm (factorization)**:
Period finding → quantum Fourier transform:
$$\text{QFT}|x\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt N}\sum_y e^{2\pi i xy/N}|y\rangle$$
Complexity: classical $\exp(O(\log N)^{1/3})$ vs quantum $O((\log N)^3)$ — **exponential → polynomial**.

**Grover's algorithm (search)**:
$O$: assigns a −1 sign to the target $|w\rangle$
$D$: reflection about the average
Number of iterations $\approx \frac{\pi}{4}\sqrt N$. The standard quantum limit ↔ classical $N$.

**Quantum channel** (Kraus representation):
$$\mathcal E(\rho) = \sum_i K_i \rho K_i^\dagger,\quad \sum_i K_i^\dagger K_i = \mathbb I$$

**Stabilizer code [[n, k, d]]**:
$n$ physical qubits, $k$ logical qubits, distance $d$ (the number of detectable errors). Examples:
- Shor [[9, 1, 3]]: 9 physical → 1 logical, single-qubit error correction
- Steane [[7, 1, 3]]: the first case of a CSS code
- **surface code**: on a lattice, stabilizers = the $X$ or $Z$ product of the 4 qubits of a plaquette. Distance $d$ = the lattice size

**Ryu-Takayanagi formula** (holography, General Relativity + quantum information):
$$S_A^{\text{CFT}} = \frac{|\gamma_A|}{4G\hbar}$$
$\gamma_A$: the **minimal area** in the bulk surrounding the boundary region $A$. **Entanglement entropy = area / 4** — the generalization of the Bekenstein-Hawking $S_{BH} = A/(4G\hbar)$.

**ER=EPR** (Maldacena-Susskind 2013):
$$\text{entanglement}_\text{EPR}(A, B) \;\Leftrightarrow\; \text{wormhole}_\text{ER}(A, B)$$
The entanglement of two qubits = a (non-information-transmitting) wormhole between two black holes.

## Why it matters

**A paradigm shift of information processing** — the domain the classical cannot solve:
- **Shor**: RSA·ECDSA broken (2048-bit factorization possible). The post-quantum cryptography industry
- **Quantum simulation**: direct simulation of quantum-chemistry, materials, and high-energy many-body systems (Feynman's 1982 vision)
- **Quantum metrology**: the Heisenberg limit $1/N$ vs the classical limit $1/\sqrt N$ — LIGO, optical-atomic clocks, magnetometers
- **Quantum communication**: BB84 is un-eavesdroppable. The foundation of the quantum internet

**A direct bridge to Condensed Matter**:
- topological qubit = the non-abelian statistics of a Majorana fermion pair
- surface code = topological order (toric code, Kitaev 2003)
- quantum error correction = the same mechanism as the stability of a condensed-matter topological phase
- **the topology of condensed matter secures the robustness of quantum information**

**The deepest bridge to Quantum Field Theory + General Relativity** — the core of quantum gravity:
- **Ryu-Takayanagi**: entanglement entropy is area. Quantum information → spacetime geometry
- **ER=EPR**: entanglement is a wormhole. Quantum information → spacetime topology
- **HaPPY code**: the bulk reconstruction of holography = a quantum error-correcting code. Is spacetime itself a quantum code?
- **Page curve / island formula (2019–)**: the quantum-information solution of the black-hole information paradox
- Implication: **the possibility that spacetime emerges from entanglement** — the most active branch of quantum gravity

**Technological applications** (as of 2024):
- IBM·Google superconducting qubits (reaching ~1000 qubits)
- IonQ·Quantinuum ion traps (high fidelity)
- Xanadu photonic quantum (room-temperature operation)
- QuEra·Atom Computing neutral atoms
- Microsoft·Quantinuum topological qubits (verification stage)
- IBM Heron 133 qubits + surface-code break-even reached (2024)

**Unsolved**:
- **a scalable error-corrected quantum computer** — needs 1 million+ physical qubits
- **quantum supremacy vs quantum advantage** — a clear quantum advantage in practical applications is undetermined
- **decisive verification of the topological qubit** — many Majorana detection candidates, decisive evidence in progress
- **the depth of quantum information ↔ quantum gravity** — AdS/CFT is limited to negative curvature (anti-de Sitter), direct application to our universe (de Sitter) is unsolved

## Historical context

**Act I — the vision of a quantum machine (1980–1993)**:
- 1980 Manin — the first proposal of quantum computing (Russian *Computable and Uncomputable*)
- **1982 Feynman** — *Simulating Physics with Computers*: exponential cost to simulate quantum on a classical computer → the quantum-computer vision
- **1982 Wootters-Zurek** — the no-cloning theorem. The security foundation of quantum cryptography
- **1984 Bennett-Brassard BB84** — the quantum-key-distribution protocol
- 1985 Deutsch — formulation of the quantum Turing machine. The first quantum algorithm (Deutsch-Jozsa)
- 1991 Ekert — E91 (entanglement-based QKD)
- 1993 Bennett·Brassard·Crepeau·Jozsa·Peres·Wootters — the theory of **quantum teleportation**
- **The decisive turn**: 1994 — with the announcement of Shor's algorithm, quantum computing went from a theoretical curiosity to a **national-security and industrial threat.** The US NSA took notice immediately

**Act II — algorithms and error correction take hold (1994–2009)**:
- **1994 Shor** — factorization in polynomial time. The RSA threat. The watershed of quantum computing
- 1995 Shor + Steane — the first codes of quantum error correction
- **1996 Grover** — the search algorithm $O(\sqrt N)$
- 1996 CSS code (Calderbank-Shor-Steane)
- 1997 Innsbruck·Rome — the first quantum-teleportation experiments
- 1997 Gottesman — the stabilizer formalism
- **2003 Kitaev — toric code** (topological quantum computing): condensed-matter topological order as a quantum code. Robust against decoherence
- 2003 Aharonov-Kitaev-Preskill — adiabatic quantum computing
- 2005– the surface code takes hold (Bravyi-Kitaev, Fowler et al.)
- 2009 HHL — quantum speedup for linear systems
- **The decisive turn**: 2003 Kitaev — condensed matter as the robust foundation of quantum computing. Quantum information and condensed matter are bound by the same tools

**Act III — the hardware age (2010–2023)**:
- 2011 D-Wave's first commercial quantum annealer (limited)
- 2014– the explosion of Google·IBM·Rigetti superconducting qubits
- 2016– IBM Quantum Experience (a cloud quantum computer)
- 2019 Google **quantum supremacy** Sycamore 53 qubits — a demonstration of overtaking a classical supercomputer in random-circuit sampling
- 2020 IonQ·Honeywell ion traps commercialized
- 2021 Xanadu Borealis — photonic quantum supremacy
- 2022 entry into the NISQ → error-correction-code era
- 2023 IBM Heron 133 qubits, surface-code break-even (logical error < physical error)
- 2023 QuEra 256 neutral atoms
- **The decisive turn**: 2019– with the quantum-supremacy demos, quantum computing moved from theory to the **engineering and industrial** stage. The NISQ era begins

**Act IV — quantum information ↔ quantum gravity (1997–)**:
- **1997 Maldacena AdS/CFT** — the exact equivalence of quantum gravity ↔ CFT. Quantum information is elevated to a quantum-gravity tool
- **2006 Ryu-Takayanagi** — entanglement entropy = minimal area
- **2013 Maldacena-Susskind ER=EPR** — entanglement ↔ wormhole
- 2015 **HaPPY code** (Pastawski-Yoshida-Harlow-Preskill) — the bulk reconstruction of holography = a quantum error-correcting code
- 2019 Penington / Almheiri et al. — quantum-information derivation of the Page curve. **The quantum-information solution of the black-hole information paradox**
- 2020s the island formula, replica wormholes — a partial solution of Hawking's 1976 problem
- 2024– quantum information as the most active branch of quantum gravity. Attempts to generalize beyond AdS/CFT to dS and flat spacetime
- **The decisive turn**: 2013 ER=EPR — suggests the possibility that spacetime emerges from entanglement. "It from Qubit" becomes the slogan of quantum gravity

**Key insight**: quantum information **leapt three times.** First (1980s) as vision and cryptography, second (1994 Shor) as an industrial threat, third (1997 AdS/CFT–) as a quantum-gravity tool. The last leap is the deepest — because quantum-information tools opened the **possibility of explaining spacetime itself.** Just as condensed matter inspired particle physics, it is an age in which quantum information inspires quantum gravity.
