# Quantum Field Theory

## One-line essence
Quantum field theory is the inevitable unification of SR + QM, a formalism in which a particle is reinterpreted as an excited state of a quantized field spread across every point of spacetime — canonical quantization and the path integral are extended to infinite degrees of freedom, interactions are computed via the Feynman diagrams of a perturbation series, and divergences are tamed by renormalization.

## Physical picture
**Why QFT is needed**: The Schrödinger equation is non-relativistic. Attaching SR to it directly yields ① negative-energy solutions (Dirac 1928), ② single-particle wavefunctions that violate causality, ③ non-conservation of particle number (in beta decay an electron is newly created). Conclusion: a formalism that handles particle creation and annihilation is required. **Quantum field theory is quantum mechanics with particle number promoted to a dynamical variable.**

**Dirac's insight (1928)**: $(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi = 0$. The negative-energy solutions forced by SR are reinterpreted as "a sea of negative energy that is completely filled, with a hole in it being the positron." In 1932 Anderson directly discovered the positron in cosmic rays. **The first case of a theory predicting a new particle.**

**Field quantization = a collection of infinitely many oscillators**: one harmonic oscillator at each point of spacetime. Expanding in modes, each momentum mode is an independent oscillator. An excited state ($\hat a^\dagger|0\rangle$) = one particle. Doubly excited ($\hat a^\dagger\hat a^\dagger|0\rangle$) = two particles. **The photon is the quantum of the electromagnetic field, the electron the quantum of the Dirac field, the Higgs the quantum of the Higgs field.** It is not the particle that is fundamental but the field.

**The vacuum is not empty**: the zero-point energy $\hbar\omega/2$ accumulates in every mode. Virtual particle pairs are ceaselessly created and annihilated. Evidence:
- **Casimir effect**: the vacuum modes between two conducting plates are restricted → attraction (predicted 1948, measured 1958)
- **Lamb shift**: the separation of hydrogen $2s_{1/2}$ and $2p_{1/2}$ (Dirac predicted them identical). The result of vacuum fluctuations jiggling the electron's position (measured 1947 → birth of QED)

**Feynman diagrams — scattering as a picture**: two particles meet, exchange a virtual particle, and scatter apart again. Each diagram is one term in the scattering amplitude. Time runs upward, space sideways. A vertex = the interaction coupling constant. **The most powerful union of mathematics and the visual** — the diagrams survived overwhelmingly over Schwinger's operator formalism.

**Renormalization — taming infinity**: the first-order correction integral diverges in momentum. At first this was regarded as a catastrophe, but Tomonaga-Schwinger-Feynman discovered a procedure that absorbs the divergence into measurable quantities. **Wilson's deep reinterpretation of 1971**: the divergence is not a defect but a signal of "short-distance physics we do not know." QFT is not a fundamental theory but an **effective field theory (EFT)** — valid up to some energy scale.

**The Standard Model = the pinnacle of QFT**: gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1). 12 fermions (6 quarks + 6 leptons) + 12 gauge bosons (8 gluons + W·Z·γ, 4) + 1 Higgs. Formulated in the 1960s–70s, completed with the discovery of W·Z in 1983 and the Higgs in 2012. **The most accurate and most tested physical theory humanity has ever made.**

**The gauge principle = the origin of interactions**: demanding local symmetry $\psi \to e^{i\alpha(x)}\psi$ forces the introduction of the gauge field $A_\mu$. Its coupling form is also automatically determined. **Interactions are a consequence of symmetry.** The strong, weak, and electromagnetic forces are all explained by this principle.

**The Higgs mechanism**: gauge bosons should originally be massless (gauge symmetry). Yet W·Z are heavy (~80, 91 GeV). Resolution: when the vacuum spontaneously breaks the symmetry (Higgs $\langle\phi\rangle\neq 0$), the gauge bosons acquire mass as they pass through that symmetry-broken vacuum. **Verified by the 2012 LHC discovery of the Higgs.**

**Unsolved**:
- **Gravity** — non-renormalizable when quantized directly as a QFT. String theory and LQG are candidates, with no settled answer
- **Hierarchy problem** — why is the Higgs mass not at the Planck scale
- **Cosmological constant** — a 120-order-of-magnitude discrepancy between QFT prediction and observation
- **Strong CP** — why does QCD conserve CP
- **Neutrino masses** (beyond the Standard Model)
- **Dark matter and dark energy** — new particles or new fields beyond QFT?

## Mathematical structure

**Lagrangian density** — every QFT is defined by this single symbol:
$$S = \int d^4x\, \mathcal L$$

**Free scalar field** (spin 0, Klein-Gordon):
$$\mathcal L_{\text{KG}} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\partial_\mu\phi)(\partial^\mu\phi) - \tfrac{1}{2}m^2\phi^2 \;\Rightarrow\; (\Box + m^2)\phi = 0,\quad \Box = \partial_\mu\partial^\mu$$

**Dirac field** (spin 1/2):
$$\mathcal L_{\text{D}} = \bar\psi(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu - m)\psi,\quad \{\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu\} = 2\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathbb{I}$$

The Clifford algebra of the gamma matrices determines the spin structure. 4 components → 2 particles + 2 antiparticles.

**QED Lagrangian** — quantum electrodynamics:
$$\mathcal L_{\text{QED}} = \bar\psi(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu - m)\psi - \tfrac{1}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$$
- covariant derivative $D_\mu = \partial_\mu + ieA_\mu$ — coupling to the gauge field
- field strength $F_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu A_\nu - \partial_\nu A_\mu$ — Maxwell as is

**Gauge symmetry** ($U(1)$, QED):
$$\psi \to e^{i\alpha(x)}\psi,\quad A_\mu \to A_\mu - \tfrac{1}{e}\partial_\mu\alpha$$

Demanding local symmetry → the gauge field and its coupling form are automatically determined.

**Yang-Mills** (non-abelian $SU(N)$):
$$\mathcal L_{\text{YM}} = -\tfrac{1}{4}F^a_{\mu\nu}F^{a\,\mu\nu},\quad F^a_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu A^a_\nu - \partial_\nu A^a_\mu + g f^{abc}A^b_\mu A^c_\nu$$
- $f^{abc}$: structure constants, $[T^a, T^b] = if^{abc}T^c$
- non-abelianness → gauge bosons interact with themselves (self-coupling)

**Standard Model gauge group**:
$$G_{\text{SM}} = SU(3)_C \times SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$$
- $SU(3)_C$: strong interaction (QCD), 8 gluons
- $SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$: electroweak unification, $W^\pm, Z, \gamma$ (separated after the Higgs)

**Higgs mechanism** (spontaneous symmetry breaking):
$$V(\phi) = -\mu^2\phi^\dagger\phi + \lambda(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2,\quad \langle\phi\rangle = v/\sqrt{2}$$
If $\mu^2 > 0$ the vacuum is stable at $|\phi|=v$. Gauge bosons couple to $v$ and acquire masses $m_W = gv/2$, $m_Z = \sqrt{g^2+g'^2}\,v/2$.

**Canonical quantization** — field $\hat\phi$ and canonical momentum $\hat\pi = \partial\mathcal L/\partial\dot\phi$:
$$[\hat\phi(\vec x, t), \hat\pi(\vec y, t)] = i\hbar\delta^3(\vec x - \vec y)$$
The infinite-degrees-of-freedom extension of Hamiltonian Mechanics' canonical quantization.

**Mode expansion** (construction of Fock space):
$$\hat\phi(x) = \int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3 \sqrt{2\omega_k}}\left[\hat a_k e^{-ik\cdot x} + \hat a_k^\dagger e^{ik\cdot x}\right]$$
$\hat a^\dagger$ = particle creation, $\hat a$ = annihilation. Vacuum $|0\rangle$, $n$-particle state = $(\hat a^\dagger)^n|0\rangle/\sqrt{n!}$.

**Path integral** (the QFT generalization of Lagrangian Mechanics):
$$Z = \int\mathcal D\phi\, e^{iS[\phi]/\hbar}$$
The action phase weights every possible field configuration. In the classical limit $\hbar\to 0$ the variational principle is recovered.

**Scattering matrix — LSZ reduction**:
$$\hat S = \mathcal T\exp\left(-\tfrac{i}{\hbar}\int d^4x\, \hat H_{\text{int}}\right)$$
Initial free particles → final free particles. The bridge between time-ordered Green functions and cross sections.

**Feynman rules** (e.g. QED with coupling constant $e$):
- external fermion: $u(p)$ or $\bar u(p)$
- fermion propagator: $\frac{i(\not p + m)}{p^2 - m^2 + i\epsilon}$
- photon propagator: $\frac{-i\eta_{\mu\nu}}{k^2 + i\epsilon}$
- vertex: $-ie\gamma^\mu$
Multiplying all internal lines, vertices, and momentum conservation of a diagram gives the amplitude.

**Renormalization** — a divergence cutoff $\Lambda$, absorbed into measurable quantities:
$$g_R = g_B + \delta g(\Lambda),\quad m_R = m_B + \delta m(\Lambda)$$

**Beta function** — the energy-scale dependence of the coupling constant:
$$\beta(g) = \mu\frac{dg}{d\mu}$$
- QED: $\beta > 0$ → grows stronger at high energy (Landau pole)
- QCD: $\beta < 0$ → **asymptotic freedom** (Gross-Wilczek-Politzer 1973). Free quarks at high energy. Confinement at low energy

**Wilson renormalization group** (1971): the transformation of the momentum cutoff $\Lambda \to b\Lambda$ ($b<1$) → the flow of the coupling constants. UV/IR fixed points determine universality classes. **The unified theory of critical phenomena** and **the foundation of EFT.**

## Why it matters

**The formalism of all particle physics**:
- the Standard Model = written in QFT
- 12 fermions (6 quarks and 6 leptons each) + 12 gauge bosons + Higgs
- the skeleton of accelerator-experiment interpretation (LHC scattering cross sections are QFT calculations)

**Resolution of the SR + QM incompatibility**:
- negative energy → antiparticles
- single-particle non-locality → many-body field formalism
- non-conservation of particle number → into a dynamical variable

**QED precision** — humanity's most accurate theory:
- electron $g - 2$: theory vs experiment agree to 12 digits
- Lamb shift: agreement at 1047 MHz
- fine-structure constant $\alpha^{-1} \approx 137.035999...$ accurate to 10 digits

**Effective description of condensed matter**:
- Fermi liquid EFT
- BCS superconductivity = gauge symmetry breaking
- magnetism and quantum critical phenomena = QFT
- quantum Hall and topological matter = topological QFT (TQFT)

**Renormalization group = a universal tool**:
- universality classes of critical phenomena (Wilson)
- the natural hierarchy of all EFTs (Polchinski, Weinberg)
- cosmology (RG evolution of quantum fluctuations during inflation)

**Bridges to the next stage**:
- QFT on curved spacetime → **Hawking radiation** (the home ground of the connection to General Relativity)
- AdS/CFT holography → a non-perturbative definition of quantum gravity
- string theory and M-theory → candidates for quantum gravity
- quantum information ↔ QFT (entanglement entropy, quantum error correction ↔ spacetime geometry)

**Unsolved**:
- quantum gravity (the largest problem)
- hierarchy problem, cosmological constant, strong CP
- the origin of neutrino masses
- the identity of dark matter and dark energy

## Historical context

**Act I — the beginning (1925–1934)**:
- 1925 Born-Heisenberg-Jordan — the first attempt at field quantization (the electromagnetic field)
- 1927 Dirac — quantum theory of photon emission and absorption. **The start of QED**
- **1928 Dirac** — the Dirac equation. Unification of SR+QM. Nobel 1933
- 1932 Anderson — discovery of the positron. Verification of Dirac's prediction
- 1934 Fermi — the four-fermion theory of beta decay

**Act II — the golden age of renormalized QED (1947–1965)**:
- 1947 Lamb-Retherford — measurement of the Lamb shift. A challenge to theory at the Shelter Island conference
- **1947–49 Tomonaga / Schwinger / Feynman** — renormalized QED. Taming the divergences
  - Schwinger: operator formalism, precise but difficult
  - Feynman: diagrams — a cognitive triumph
- 1949 Dyson — the equivalence of the two formalisms, general rules for diagrams
- 1965 Nobel — Tomonaga, Schwinger, Feynman shared

**Act III — gauge theory and the Standard Model (1954–2012)**:
- **1954 Yang-Mills** — non-abelian gauge theory. At first ignored due to the problem of massless gauge bosons
- 1957 Lee-Yang — parity violation in the weak interaction (Nobel 1957)
- 1961–64 **Higgs / Brout-Englert / Guralnik-Hagen-Kibble** — spontaneous symmetry breaking and gauge-boson mass
- 1967–71 **Glashow-Weinberg-Salam** — electromagnetic-weak unification. Nobel 1979
- **1971 't Hooft-Veltman** — proof of the renormalizability of non-abelian gauge theory. Nobel 1999
- 1973 **Gross-Wilczek-Politzer** — QCD asymptotic freedom. Nobel 2004
- 1973 Kobayashi-Maskawa — CP violation + 6 quarks. Nobel 2008
- 1983 CERN — discovery of the W·Z bosons (Rubbia). Nobel 1984
- **2012 LHC** — discovery of the Higgs boson. Completion of the Standard Model. Nobel 2013 (Higgs, Englert)
- 2015 Kajita-McDonald — neutrino oscillations → mass. **The first crack in the Standard Model**

**Act IV — deep reinterpretation (1971–)**:
- **1971–74 Wilson** — the meaning of the renormalization group. QFT is not a fundamental theory but an **EFT**. Nobel 1982. **The deepest change in modern QFT**
- 1974 't Hooft — the large-N limit, a bridge to string theory
- 1974 Hawking — QFT on curved spacetime → Hawking radiation
- 1985– the string theory renaissance (first 1985, second 1995 Witten)
- 1997 Maldacena — AdS/CFT holography. As of 2024 the most active candidate for quantum gravity

**Key insight**: QFT was not made in one go. **It was formulated gradually over 50 years, from 1925 to 1975.** At every stage, divergences, paradoxes, and experimental challenges forced the theory. **That Wilson's 1971 renormalization-group reinterpretation turned QFT from a "fundamental theory" into an "effective theory"** was the deepest change. By this, the short-distance physics we do not know (quantum gravity) and the long-distance physics we know well (the Standard Model) are consistently separated.

## Connections

**Prior connections** (already studied):
- Quantum Mechanics — the infinite-degrees-of-freedom extension of canonical quantization. Measurement and entanglement become vacuum structure in QFT
- Quantum Mechanics II — scattering, perturbation, and Fermi's golden rule as the non-relativistic limit of QFT scattering. LSZ is their generalization
- Special Relativity — 4-momentum, metric, Lorentz covariance. SR is the backbone of the QFT formalism
- Lagrangian Mechanics — Lagrangian density $\mathcal L$, action $S = \int d^4x\,\mathcal L$. The QFT extension of the variational principle
- Hamiltonian Mechanics — canonical quantization $[,]=i\hbar$ generalized to fields
- Statistical Mechanics — partition function ↔ Wick rotation ↔ 4D statistical mechanics (already recognized). Critical phenomena = RG fixed points
- Optics — the photon = the quantum of the electromagnetic field. QED is the quantum formalism of optics
- Maxwell Electromagnetism — QED = quantized Maxwell + the Dirac electron
- General Relativity — QFT on curved spacetime → Hawking radiation. The unification of the two theories is the unsolved quantum gravity
