# Symmetry and Group Theory ↔ Music

## One-line essence
Just as *physics derives interactions from representations of symmetry groups*, this bridge holds that *the inversion·retrograde·transposition of Bach's counterpoint are group actions on the pitch space ℤ/12ℤ* — and that equal temperament (the 1722 *Well-Tempered Clavier*) is the *physical realization* that made that group structure actually playable.

## Physics side

Core tools of Lagrangian Mechanics:
- **Noether's theorem**: if $L$ is symmetric under a transformation ⟹ a conserved quantity exists — the fundamental grammar of physics, *symmetry = invariant*
- **The principle of least action**: a global view over the whole structure (path space) — isomorphic to viewing the totality of *transformation relations* rather than individual notes

Core tools of Quantum Field Theory:
- **The gauge principle**: demanding local symmetry $\psi \to e^{i\alpha(x)}\psi$ *forcibly derives* interactions — interaction is a consequence of symmetry
- **Group representation theory**: the Standard Model = representations of SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1). Particle classification = classifying irreducible representations of symmetry groups
- **Harmonic oscillators·mode expansion**: a field = a collection of infinitely many oscillators, excited states = particles — decomposition into vibrational modes as the foundation of form

## Music side

Core methodology of Johann Sebastian Bach:
- **Counterpoint**: a single subject proceeds polyphonically while being simultaneously transformed by *retrograde·inversion·augmentation·diminution·transposition* — *The Art of Fugue* (1750, unfinished) is its abstract summit
- **Equal temperament**: the *Well-Tempered Clavier* (1722·1742), 24×2 — dividing the octave into 12 equal parts so that *all keys are equal* (a 200-year precursor of Schoenberg's twelve-tone method)
- **Mathematical proportion**: numerology of measure counts·intervals (3 = Trinity, 14 = B-A-C-H) — though a hypothesis held by only some scholars
- **The crab canon of the *Musical Offering* (1747)**: the subject and its retrograde proceed simultaneously — an audible demonstration of time-reversal symmetry

Connections to aesthetics·music-foundations:
- **The objectivist aesthetics of Plato·Aristotle**: beauty = proportion·unity·form — Bach's criterion of beauty (Q2: mathematical proportion + contrapuntal precision) is the musical summit of this lineage
- **Kant's purposiveness without purpose**: the formal logic of the fugue is self-contained without external purpose — resonating with the closure of group structure

## Exact equivalences·dictionary

| Physics·mathematics | ⟺ | Music |
|---|---|---|
| the cyclic group $\mathbb{Z}/12\mathbb{Z}$ | ⟺ | the 12 pitch-class space of equal temperament — octave identification |
| the group generator $+1 \pmod{12}$ | ⟺ | a semitone shift |
| translation $T_n: x \mapsto x + n$ | ⟺ | **transposition** — relations preserved when changing key |
| reflection $I_n: x \mapsto -x + n$ | ⟺ | **inversion** — flipping the direction of intervals |
| the dihedral group $D_{12}$ (order 24) generated by $T_n$·$I_n$ | ⟺ | the standard transformation group of tonal music (the T/I group) |
| time reversal $t \mapsto -t$ ($\mathbb{Z}/2$) | ⟺ | **retrograde** — the crab canon·*The Art of Fugue* |
| scale transformation $t \mapsto \lambda t$ | ⟺ | **augmentation·diminution** — rescaling the subject's rhythm |
| the orbit of a group action | ⟺ | the set of all variants of one subject — an entire fugue as the exploration of a single orbit |
| classification by orbits ($D_{12}$-equivalence classes of sets) | ⟺ | **Forte pitch-class set theory** (1973 *The Structure of Atonal Music*) — classifying set classes by T/I equivalence |
| subgroups such as $\{0,3,6,9\} \cong \mathbb{Z}/4$ | ⟺ | diminished-seventh chords·whole-tone scales, i.e. **Messiaen's modes of limited transposition** — corresponding to proper subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}/12$, so the number of transpositions is finite |
| frieze symmetry (translation + glide reflection) | ⟺ | the repetition·imitation structure of the canon (translation = staggered imitation, glide reflection = retrograde canon) — a structural analogy |
| the Fourier series $f(t) = \sum_n c_n e^{in\omega t}$ | ⟺ | **timbre** = the overtone spectrum $\{c_n\}$ — Helmholtz 1863 *On the Sensations of Tone* |
| the fundamental frequency $\omega$ | ⟺ | pitch |
| the distribution of mode amplitudes | ⟺ | an instrument's characteristic timbre (the same note differs in spectrum) |
| frequency ratios $2:1$, $3:2$ | ⟺ | octave·perfect fifth (Pythagorean consonance — physical overtone alignment) |
| the geometric division $2^{1/12}$ (irrational ratios) | ⟺ | equal temperament — a compromise that gives up just ratios to gain group structure (the full cycle of $\mathbb{Z}/12$) |
| symmetry breaking (choosing a reference point) | ⟺ | establishing tonality — choosing a tonic among the 12 notes breaks the $\mathbb{Z}/12$ symmetry; Schoenberg's twelve-tone technique is the restoration of that symmetry |

**A caution on exactness — the boundary without exaggeration**:
- **Noether's theorem does not directly hold in music.** Music has neither a Lagrangian nor a conserved-quantity theorem. What holds is the stage before it — the shared grammar that *transformation invariants define the identity of a structure* (a subject remains "the same subject" under transposition·inversion). The correspondence is **exact up to the level of group actions**; at the level of dynamics it remains an analogy.
- The frieze/crystallographic-group correspondence is likewise a *structural analogy*, not a theorem of isomorphism.
- Correspondences firm at the theorem level: the $\mathbb{Z}/12$ pitch-class space, T/I = the $D_{12}$ action, Forte classification = orbit counting, Messiaen modes = subgroups, Fourier = timbre.

## Unresolved·boundary matrix (of the bridge domain itself)

1. **Emotion is not explicable by group theory** — why particular transformations (minor-mode inversion etc.) evoke particular affects lies outside group theory (the domain of neuroaesthetics: axis ③ cognition of aesthetics and the neuroaesthetics note)
2. **The incomplete correspondence between timbre perception ↔ spectrum** — even with identical Fourier coefficients, different attacks·temporal envelopes give different timbres (the limits of static spectral reduction)
3. **The equal-temperament vs just-intonation tension** — the group structure ($\mathbb{Z}/12$) and physical consonance (integer ratios) are mathematically incompatible ($2^{7/12} \neq 3/2$) — Bach's choice was *structure first*, and the aesthetic status of this choice remains an open problem
4. **The Bach numerology hypothesis** — 14 = B-A-C-H etc. is a hypothesis of only some scholars, not a theorem (the reservation of the original note is maintained)
5. **Whether a "gauge principle" of music exists** — does demanding local symmetry derive the rules of harmonic progression (neo-Riemannian theory is a candidate formalism, but without the compulsory force of physics)
