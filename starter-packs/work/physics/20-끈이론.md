# String Theory

## One-line essence
String theory is a quantum-gravity candidate that treats particles not as points but as the vibrational modes of one-dimensional strings — the 5 string theories are unified into 11-dimensional M-theory, **Strominger-Vafa 1996 exactly reproduced the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy by D-brane microstate counting (the first microscopic derivation in quantum gravity)**, and the string-theory landscape of $\sim 10^{500}$ vacua creates the multiverse and anthropic-principle debates — providing the microscopic definition for full quantum gravity.

## Physical picture

**The core hypothesis — strings, not particles**:
Every particle (electron, photon, graviton, quark) is a vibrational mode of a one-dimensional string. String length $l_s \sim 10^{-34}$ m (near the Planck scale). Different vibrational modes = different particles. **The theory has almost no parameters** (only $g_s$, the string coupling, and $l_s$) — in contrast to the Standard Model's 18 parameters.

**Open strings vs closed strings**:
- **Closed string** (loop): both ends closed. Automatically contains the **graviton** (the massless spin-2 vibrational mode) — the reason string theory is quantum gravity
- **Open string** (segment): both ends sit on D-branes. Gauge bosons appear from the vibration of the endpoints on the D-branes
- **All particles are different vibrations of the same object (the string)** — a powerful candidate for a unified theory

**The 5 string theories → M-theory unification** (Witten 1995 — the *Second String Revolution*):
- Type I (open and closed, $SO(32)$)
- Type IIA (closed, non-chiral)
- Type IIB (closed, chiral)
- $E_8 \times E_8$ heterotic
- $SO(32)$ heterotic

→ all 5 are different limits of 11-dimensional **M-theory** (connected by the S, T, U dualities). M-theory itself is based not on strings but on **2-branes (M2) and 5-branes (M5).**

**D-branes** (Polchinski 1995, **the watershed**):
The endpoints of open strings attach to a $p$-dimensional hyperplane (a Dp-brane). The non-perturbative degrees of freedom of string theory. **Candidates for BH microstates**:
- the gauge theory on the D-branes corresponds to the quantum gravity outside the BH (the starting point of AdS/CFT)
- the number of quantum states of a D-brane bundle = the BH entropy

**Strominger-Vafa 1996** — **the first microscopic derivation in quantum gravity**:
Counting the microstates of an extremal BH ($T_H = 0$, BPS states) as the quantum states of a D-brane bundle:
$$N_{\rm states}(Q_1, Q_5, n) = \exp\left[2\pi\sqrt{n Q_1 Q_5}\right]$$
- $Q_1$: the number of D1-branes, $Q_5$: the number of D5-branes, $n$: momentum modes
- $\ln N$ = $2\pi\sqrt{nQ_1 Q_5}$ = **exactly reproducing the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy $A/(4G\hbar)$**

**Meaning**: the Bekenstein 1972 hypothesis is **exactly derived by microstate counting** in string theory. The strongest evidence that quantum gravity is not conjecture but a computable discipline. The peak of the 30-year flow from General Relativity BH thermodynamics → Hawking radiation → string-theory microscopic counting.

**The string-theory landscape (Susskind 2003)** — $\sim 10^{500}$ vacua:
The 6 extra dimensions of string theory are curled up small (compactification). Depending on the shape (a Calabi-Yau manifold) and the choice of flux, **different 4D physical laws**:
- different particle masses
- different coupling constants
- different cosmological constants

The possible vacua are estimated at $\sim 10^{500}$ (Douglas-Susskind). **Each vacuum is an independent universe** → the multiverse scenario (eternal inflation).

**The anthropic-principle debate**:
Among the $10^{500}$ vacua, the fine-tuning of our universe (the small cosmological constant, the hierarchy problem) is not chance but "**only vacua in which observers can exist are selected**" — the weak anthropic principle. Strong criticism ('t Hooft et al.): "this is not physics but post-hoc rationalization." An unresolved debate.

**M-theory + the factorization paradox** (the connection to 19):
The string-theory partition function generalizes the wormhole summation of Replica wormholes (the genus expansion). In a single string-theory vacuum the wormhole acts like an ensemble — the string-theory-side interpretation of the factorization paradox is unsolved. An active research area in 2024.

## Mathematical structure

**Polyakov action** (the closed bosonic string):
$$S_P = -\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d^2\sigma\,\sqrt{-h}\,h^{ab}\partial_a X^\mu \partial_b X_\mu \eta_{\mu\nu}$$
- $X^\mu(\sigma, \tau)$: the spacetime coordinates of the string ($\mu = 0, \cdots, D-1$)
- $h_{ab}$: the world-sheet metric ($a, b = 0, 1$)
- $\alpha' = l_s^2$: the inverse string tension
- gauge symmetry: reparametrization + Weyl scaling

**Critical dimension** (anomaly cancellation):
- bosonic string: $D = 26$
- superstring (RNS·GS formalisms): $D = 10$
- M-theory: $D = 11$
- our 4D spacetime + a **6D (or 7D) compactification**

**Compactification — Calabi-Yau** (Candelas-Horowitz-Strominger-Witten 1985):
- the 6D extra dimensions form a Ricci-flat complex manifold (a Calabi-Yau 3-fold)
- $SU(3)$ holonomy → preserving 4D $\mathcal N = 1$ supersymmetry
- the Hodge numbers determine the 4D particle content and number of generations
- **flux compactification**: stabilized by NS-NS and R-R fluxes → the landscape

**D-brane action** (DBI):
$$S_{Dp} = -T_p\int d^{p+1}\sigma\,e^{-\Phi}\sqrt{-\det(g_{ab} + B_{ab} + 2\pi\alpha' F_{ab})}$$
- $T_p$: the D-brane tension
- $\Phi$: the dilaton
- $g, B, F$: the pulled-back metric, B-field, and gauge field strength

**S/T/U dualities**:
- **S duality**: strong coupling ↔ weak coupling ($g_s \to 1/g_s$). Type IIB is self-dual
- **T duality**: large radius ↔ small radius ($R \to \alpha'/R$). IIA ↔ IIB
- **U duality**: a combination of S and T. The $E_n$ exceptional-group structure

**Strominger-Vafa 1996** — the D1-D5-P system:
- an AdS$_3$ × S$^3$ × T$^4$ background
- $Q_1$ D1-branes + $Q_5$ D5-branes + $n$ momentum
- the BH entropy:
$$S_{BH} = \frac{A}{4G_5} = 2\pi\sqrt{n Q_1 Q_5}$$
- the number of microstates (the Cardy formula + the CFT$_2$ central charge $c = 6 Q_1 Q_5$):
$$N_{\rm states} = \exp\left[2\pi\sqrt{\frac{c\, n}{6}}\right] = \exp\left[2\pi\sqrt{n Q_1 Q_5}\right]$$
- **the two results agree exactly** — the first microscopic derivation in quantum gravity

**Cardy formula** (a universal result of CFT$_2$):
$$S(E) = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{c\, E}{6}}$$
- $c$: the central charge
- $E$: the excitation energy
- the central charge of the D-brane CFT directly determines the BH entropy

**String-theory partition function — the genus expansion** (Polyakov):
$$Z_{\rm string}(g_s) = \sum_{g=0}^{\infty} g_s^{2g-2}\,F_g$$
- $g$: the genus of the world-sheet (number of handles)
- $g_s$: the string coupling constant
- exactly parallel to the JT genus expansion of 19

**$\mathcal N = 4$ SYM ↔ AdS$_5 \times$ S$^5$** (the exact derivation of AdS/CFT):
- $N$ D3-branes → the AdS$_5 \times$ S$^5$ background
- 't Hooft coupling $\lambda = g_{YM}^2 N = g_s N (4\pi)$
- strongly coupled gauge ↔ weakly coupled gravity

**The number of landscape vacua**:
- topological classification of Calabi-Yau 3-folds: $\sim 10^4$ known classes
- flux choices on each CY: $\sim 10^{100} \sim 10^{500}$
- Bousso-Polchinski: $\sim 10^{500}$ (the current standard estimate)
- estimate of the cosmological-constant distribution: vacua with small $\Lambda$ are sufficiently many → the anthropic principle

## Why it matters

**The most developed candidate for quantum gravity**:
- non-perturbative definitions (M-theory, AdS/CFT)
- the 6 great dualities (S, T, U + further discoveries)
- the microscopic BH derivation (Strominger-Vafa)
- applications to non-Fermi liquids and strong correlation (holographic condensed matter)

**The microscopic definition of AdS/CFT, Hawking radiation, Page curve, and Replica wormholes**:
- AdS/CFT appears naturally from string theory
- the microscopic mechanism of Hawking radiation is refined by D-brane counting
- the wormhole genus expansion of the Page curve is a generalization of the string-theory world-sheet genus
- **20 is the microscopic foundation of the full quantum-gravity area (P2)**

**The shock of Strominger-Vafa**:
The 1972 Bekenstein hypothesis → the 1974 Hawking verification → **the 1996 Strominger-Vafa microscopic counting.** After 24 years, an exact derivation of the microscopic origin of BH entropy. The watershed of the quantum-gravity field.

**Technological applications** (indirect):
- AdS/CFT applications (holographic condensed matter, QGP viscosity, strong correlation)
- mathematical applications (mirror symmetry, enumerative geometry, geometric Langlands)
- quantum-information applications (the HaPPY code, topological quantum computing)

**Unsolved**:
- **is the landscape unverifiable?** — experimental verification identifying our universe among the $10^{500}$ vacua is not possible
- **the anthropic-principle debate** — is the strong anthropic principle physics or metaphysics
- **the stability of dS vacua** — the Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture (TCC), the Swampland
- **the factorization paradox** — the origin of the wormhole in a single string vacuum
- **is string theory really the theory of our universe?** — absence of direct evidence
- **generalization of the black-hole information paradox to flat and dS** — unsolved even in string theory

**Critical perspectives**:
- string-theory skepticism of Smolin, Woit, and others
- a lack of verifiable predictions (no string effects visible at LHC energies)
- the criticism "the theory is so flexible it explains anything" → the two-sidedness of the landscape

## Historical context

**Act I — the birth of string theory (1968–1984)**:
- 1968 Veneziano — the Veneziano amplitude (the dual resonance model of the strong interaction)
- 1970 Nambu, Susskind, Nielsen — the Veneziano amplitude appears from the vibration of a string. **The start of string theory**
- 1971 Ramond, Neveu-Schwarz — the superstring (adding fermions)
- 1974 Scherk-Schwarz, Yoneya — a massless spin-2 mode in string theory → the **graviton.** String theory = a quantum-gravity candidate
- early 1980s — right up to the Green-Schwarz anomaly cancellation (1984), string theory was outside the mainstream
- **1984 Green-Schwarz** — Type I anomaly cancellation. The **First Superstring Revolution** begins
- 1985 — the heterotic string (Gross-Harvey-Martinec-Rohm), Calabi-Yau compactification (CHSW)
- **The decisive turn**: 1984 — with anomaly cancellation string theory enters the academic mainstream. The 5 superstrings take hold, a candidate for a unified theory

**Act II — the duality revolution and M-theory (1995–1998)**:
- 1989 Sen — the first evidence of S duality
- **1995 Witten** — *String theory dynamics in various dimensions*. Presenting the 5 string theories as different limits of 11-dimensional M-theory. The **Second Superstring Revolution**
- **1995 Polchinski** — the formalization of D-branes. Non-perturbative degrees of freedom, BPS states, exact analysis of string theory possible
- **1996 Strominger-Vafa** — exactly reproducing the BH entropy via D-brane microscopic counting. **The first microscopic derivation in quantum gravity**
- 1996– Maldacena, precise Bekenstein-Hawking analysis
- **1997 Maldacena AdS/CFT** — the holographic duality that appeared naturally from string theory
- 1998 — the Banks-Fischler-Shenker-Susskind (BFSS) M-theory matrix model
- **The decisive turn**: 1995 Witten + Polchinski — string theory goes from an abstract theory to a **computable discipline.** D-branes appear as candidates for BH microstates

**Act III — the landscape and the multiverse (1999–2010s)**:
- 1999 Maldacena-Strominger et al. — microscopic analysis of black branes
- 2000 Bousso-Polchinski — the cosmological-constant landscape proposed via flux compactification
- 2003 Kachru-Kallosh-Linde-Trivedi (KKLT) — the first construction of a stabilized dS vacuum
- **2003 Susskind — *The Anthropic Landscape of String Theory*** (arXiv:hep-th/0302219). The formalization of the string landscape + the anthropic principle
- 2003– Douglas, Denef et al. — landscape statistics. The $10^{500}$ estimate
- 2005– the Vafa Swampland program — landscape vs swamp (EFTs that can vs cannot appear in real quantum gravity)
- 2010s — KKLT criticism (is there really a stable dS vacuum in the landscape? unsolved)
- **The decisive turn**: 2003 Susskind — the landscape settles as an essential feature of string theory. The verifiability and anthropic-principle debates begin in earnest

**Act IV — quantum information, Swampland, and factorization (2010s–)**:
- 2007– the explosion of AdS/CFT applications (condensed matter, QGP)
- 2014– bulk reconstruction (holography and quantum error correction)
- 2015 the HaPPY code — a deep meeting of string theory and quantum information
- 2018– the Swampland conjectures in earnest (the de Sitter conjecture, the distance conjecture, weak gravity)
- 2019– replica wormholes (JT + SYK), the wormhole contributions to the string-theory partition function
- 2022– the **factorization paradox** — the origin of the wormhole in a single string vacuum unsolved
- 2024 — string theory settled as the standard quantum-gravity candidate; dS vacua, factorization, and verifiability are the most active unsolved problems
- **The decisive turn**: 2010s quantum information and Swampland — string theory goes from an abstract theory to an **applied discipline** connected to quantum information, cosmology, and experiment. But verifiability remains unsolved

**Key insight**: string theory **transformed twice.** First (1984·1995) entering the academic mainstream via anomaly + duality, second (1996 Strominger-Vafa + 1997 Maldacena AdS/CFT) providing the microscopic definition for full quantum gravity. **The 24-year flow of the Bekenstein 1972 hypothesis → the 1974 Hawking verification → the 1996 string-theory microscopic derivation**, together with the 34-year accumulation of Replica wormholes and the 27-year accumulation of the Page curve, verifies the characteristic accumulation-confluence pattern of the full quantum-gravity area (the U-05 7-stage appendix refinement). Yet the verifiability and anthropic-principle debates created by the $10^{500}$ landscape remain the deepest unsolved problem — **the theory's success created a crisis of verifiability.**
