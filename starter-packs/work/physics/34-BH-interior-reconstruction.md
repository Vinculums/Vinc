## Mathematical structure

**Quantifying the AMPS paradox**:
- Late Hawking radiation $R$: $|R|$ large ($t > t_{\rm Page}$)
- BH interior $B$: free-fall degrees of freedom
- Monotonicity: $S_R + S_B = S_{\rm total}$ constant
- AMPS: entanglement of $R$ + $B$ → the new Hawking particle $H'$ is also *over-entangled* → firewall

**ER=EPR response**:
- $|R\rangle \otimes |B\rangle$ = thermofield double state
- ER wormhole: $|TFD\rangle = \sum_i e^{-\beta E_i/2}|i\rangle_R \otimes |i\rangle_B$
- Infalling observer: entanglement of $|R\rangle, |B\rangle$ = ER bridge

**Island formula** (Penington 2019, AHMS 2019):
$$S(R) = \min_I \,\text{ext}\left[\frac{A(\partial I)}{4G_N} + S_{\rm bulk}(R \cup I)\right]$$
- Empty island ($I = \emptyset$): $S(R) \approx S_{\rm Hawking}$
- Non-trivial island: $S(R) \approx S_{BH} - S_{\rm Hawking}$
- Page time: the crossover point of the two saddles

**QES formula**:
$$\frac{\delta}{\delta \gamma}\left[\frac{A(\gamma)}{4G_N} + S_{\rm bulk}(\Sigma_\gamma)\right] = 0$$
- $\gamma = \partial I$: quantum extremal surface

**Replica wormholes** (Penington-Shenker-Stanford-Yang 2020):
$$Z_n = \sum_{\rm topology} Z_{\rm disconnected} + Z_{\rm wormhole}$$
- Disconnected: simple Hawking (monotonic increase)
- Wormhole: information recovery (Page curve)

**Petz map** (Akers-Penington 2021):
$$\mathcal R[\sigma] = \rho^{1/2}\mathcal N^*[\mathcal N(\rho)^{-1/2}\sigma\mathcal N(\rho)^{-1/2}]\rho^{1/2}$$
- $\mathcal N$ = encoding (interior → radiation)
- $\mathcal R$ = decoding (interior reconstruction)
