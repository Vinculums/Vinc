# Condensed Matter

## One-line essence
Condensed matter physics treats phenomena in which new order, new excitations, and new phases emerge when an enormous number of quantum particles come together, and its entire toolkit is the EFT formalism of Quantum Field Theory — the stage on which the most diverse quantum phenomena in the universe unfold.

## Physical picture
**Emergence is the essence**: the quantum mechanics of a single water molecule cannot see the liquid-gas phase transition. The new laws and new order that appear only when $10^{23}$ of them gather is the theme of condensed matter. Anderson 1972 *More is Different* — a declaration that reductionism cannot solve everything. **The autonomous laws made by the many-body system create a new EFT.**

**The 5 key stages**:

1. **Free electrons + Fermi surface**: the momentum-space sphere that fermions fill at $T = 0$. The starting point of all metallic properties. Sommerfeld 1928. Specific heat, electrical conductivity, and magnetic response are all determined by excitations near the Fermi surface.

2. **Band theory**: the periodic potential of a crystal lattice → Bloch's theorem → energy bands. If the Fermi energy lies within a band it is a **metal**; if it lies within a band gap, an **insulator**. If the gap is small, a **semiconductor** (Si·Ge, the foundation of transistors and CPUs).

3. **Magnetism — spontaneous symmetry breaking**: in the Heisenberg/Ising models the rotational/flip symmetry breaks at low temperature and magnetization emerges. **Continuous symmetry breaking → Goldstone bosons (spin waves, magnons)**. The spontaneous symmetry breaking of QFT (12) is directly observed in condensed matter.

4. **Superconductivity — the Higgs mechanism of condensed matter**: two electrons attract via phonon exchange → **Cooper pair** → fermion pairs behave like bosons → boson condensation → zero resistance + the Meissner effect (expulsion of the magnetic field). BCS 1957. **Anderson 1962 proposed the gauge-symmetry-breaking + gauge-boson-mass mechanism before Higgs (1964).** The photon acquires mass inside the superconductor so the magnetic field cannot penetrate.

5. **Topological matter**: electrons in a strong magnetic field → the **quantum Hall effect**. The transverse conductivity $\sigma_{xy} = \nu e^2/h$ is exactly quantized ($\nu$ integer or fraction). The reason is **topological** — a topological invariant of the whole system (the Chern number) determines it. A robustness insensitive to impurities. After its explosion in the 1980s, on to topological insulators, Weyl/Dirac semimetals, and Majorana fermions.

**Analogy — the crystal lattice = the microstructure of spacetime**: if QFT is a formalism that places a field at each point of spacetime, condensed matter is **QFT on a crystal lattice**. The lattice makes a new "approximate spacetime," and on it quasiparticles such as the phonon, magnon, and polaron appear as new "approximate particles." The stage where the insight of QFT (12), that **every particle is merely an excitation of an EFT**, is most directly verified.

**Fermi liquid — a powerful EFT in one line**: even when a weak interaction is turned on in the free-electron model, the qualitative picture is preserved. Except that the electron is dressed as a "quasiparticle" and its mass changes. **Landau 1956.** The regime where the Fermi liquid breaks down (high-temperature superconductivity, quantum critical phenomena) is the current frontier of condensed matter.

## Mathematical structure

**Bloch's theorem** (periodic potential):
$$\psi_{n,\vec k}(\vec r) = u_{n,\vec k}(\vec r)\, e^{i\vec k\cdot\vec r}$$
$u$ has the periodicity of the lattice. Band index $n$, crystal momentum $\vec k$.

**Fermi surface** ($T=0$):
$$E_F = E_n(\vec k_F),\qquad n_e = 2\int_{|\vec k|<k_F}\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}$$

**Heisenberg magnetism model**:
$$\hat H = -J\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\hat{\vec S}_i\cdot\hat{\vec S}_j$$
$J > 0$ ferromagnetic, $J < 0$ antiferromagnetic. **Continuous symmetry SO(3) breaking → magnons (Goldstone bosons)**.

**Ising model**:
$$\hat H = -J\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\sigma_i\sigma_j - h\sum_i\sigma_i$$
2D Ising = Onsager's 1944 exact solution. The critical exponents determine the universality class.

**Landau-Ginzburg free energy** (order parameter $\psi$):
$$F[\psi] = \int d^d x\left[a|\psi|^2 + b|\psi|^4 + c|\nabla\psi|^2\right]$$
- $a > 0$: disordered (symmetric) / $a < 0$: ordered (broken symmetry), $|\psi|^2 = -a/(2b)$
- the universal form of every second-order phase transition. The same structure as the QFT (12) Higgs potential

**BCS gap equation**:
$$\Delta = V\sum_{\vec k}\frac{\Delta}{2E_{\vec k}}\tanh\frac{\beta E_{\vec k}}{2},\quad E_{\vec k} = \sqrt{\xi_{\vec k}^2 + \Delta^2}$$
- $V$: the pairing attraction (phonon-mediated)
- critical temperature: $k_B T_c \approx 1.13\,\hbar\omega_D\, e^{-1/N(0)V}$ — non-perturbative, diverges as $V \to 0$. **An exact non-perturbative result unsolvable by perturbation theory**

**London equation + Meissner**:
$$\nabla^2\vec B = \lambda_L^{-2}\vec B$$
The magnetic field decays exponentially inside the superconductor (London penetration depth $\lambda_L$). **Photon mass = $\hbar/\lambda_L c$** — the gauge boson acquires mass in the condensed-matter vacuum.

**Quantum Hall effect**:
$$\sigma_{xy} = \nu\,\frac{e^2}{h}$$
$\nu$ integer (IQHE) or fraction (FQHE, Laughlin 1983).

**TKNN formula** — the topological origin of the quantization:
$$\sigma_{xy} = \frac{e^2}{h}\sum_n\int_{\text{BZ}}\frac{d^2k}{2\pi}\,\Omega_n(\vec k),\quad C_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int\Omega_n d^2k \in \mathbb{Z}$$
$\Omega_n$: Berry curvature, $C_n$: Chern number (topological invariant).

**Topological insulator — $\mathbb Z_2$ classification** (Kane-Mele 2005, protected by time reversal):
- the interior is an insulator, **the surface is a topologically enforced conducting state**
- as long as time reversal is not broken, the surface states do not disappear

**Hubbard model** (the strong-correlation standard):
$$\hat H = -t\sum_{\langle ij\rangle\sigma}(\hat c_{i\sigma}^\dagger\hat c_{j\sigma} + \text{h.c.}) + U\sum_i \hat n_{i\uparrow}\hat n_{i\downarrow}$$
If $U/t$ is small, a Fermi liquid; if large, a Mott insulator. A candidate model for high-temperature superconductivity.

**RG critical phenomena** (Wilson, Quantum Field Theory):
$$\xi \sim |T - T_c|^{-\nu},\quad C \sim |T - T_c|^{-\alpha},\quad m \sim (T_c - T)^\beta$$
The critical exponents $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \nu, \eta$ are determined by dimension + symmetry alone — **universality**. Liquid-gas and magnetism are in the same class.

## Why it matters

**The foundation of modern technology — the industrial revolution made by condensed matter**:
- semiconductors (CPUs, memory, LEDs, solar cells) — band theory
- magnetic storage, MRI, NMR — magnetism, nuclear spin
- superconducting magnets (LHC, MRI, maglev trains) — BCS
- optical fiber, lasers, displays — photon-matter interaction
- **quantum computing** (topological qubits, Majorana) — topological matter
- graphene, 2D materials (Geim-Novoselov Nobel 2010)

**The richest stage of EFT** — direct verification of Quantum Field Theory:
- Fermi liquid = the condensed-matter version of the free Dirac EFT
- BCS = the prototype of spontaneous symmetry breaking + the Higgs mechanism
- LG free energy = the same structure as the Higgs potential
- RG universality classes = QFT IR fixed points

**The prototype of spontaneous symmetry breaking**: Anderson 1962 proposed the gauge symmetry breaking and photon mass of BCS before Higgs (1964). **The Standard Model Higgs mechanism is a particle-physics transplant inspired by condensed matter.**

**The deepest meeting of topology and physics**:
- TKNN: quantum Hall = Chern number
- topological insulator: $\mathbb Z_2$ invariant
- Majorana and non-abelian statistics: candidates for topological qubits
- TQFT (topological quantum field theory) — Chern-Simons, K-theory

**The emergence paradigm** — the limits of reductionism:
- high-energy physics (QFT) ↔ low-energy physics (condensed matter) is not reduction but **complementary**
- the autonomous laws made by the many-body system are essential
- Anderson *More is Different* (1972) — the philosophical declaration of condensed matter

**Bridge to quantum information and quantum computing**:
- topological qubit = a pair of Majorana fermions
- surface code (quantum error correction) ↔ toric code (topological order)
- AdS/CFT applications — quantum-gravity tools for strongly correlated systems (holographic condensed matter)

**Unsolved areas**:
- **high-temperature superconductivity** (discovered 1986, unsolved for 40 years) — not solvable by Fermi liquid
- **quantum critical phenomena** — the regime where the Fermi liquid breaks down
- absence of a **general theory of strong correlation** (no exact solution of Hubbard found)
- **clear detection of the Majorana fermion** (many candidates, decisive evidence undetermined)
- the mechanism of **twisted-graphene superconductivity** (2018–)

## Historical context

**Act I — free electrons and bands (1900–1940)**:
- 1900 Drude — the free-electron model (classical). Succeeded for resistivity and thermal conduction
- 1928 Sommerfeld — Fermi statistics + free electrons → the quantum correction of specific heat
- **1928 Bloch** — the electron wavefunction in a crystal lattice. The start of band theory
- 1931 Wilson — classification of insulators, metals, semiconductors
- 1939 Wigner-Seitz, Bardeen — work function, establishment of condensed-matter quantum mechanics
- **The decisive turn**: 1947 Bell Labs — Bardeen·Brattain·Shockley invent the transistor. Condensed matter becomes an industrial revolution (Nobel 1956)

**Act II — the formalization of magnetism and superconductivity (1925–1972)**:
- 1925 Ising model / 1928 Heisenberg magnetism
- **1937 Landau** — the order-parameter theory of phase transitions
- **1911 Onnes** — discovery of superconductivity (mercury, 4.2 K). Nobel 1913
- 1933 Meissner-Ochsenfeld — the Meissner effect
- 1950 Ginzburg-Landau — phenomenological superconductivity
- **1957 BCS** (Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer) — the microscopic theory. Fermion pairs → boson condensation. Nobel 1972
- **1962 Anderson** — gauge symmetry breaking + photon mass. **The Higgs-mechanism prototype** (2 years ahead of Higgs's 1964 proposal)
- 1962 Josephson — the Josephson effect (Nobel 1973)
- **The decisive turn**: 1972 Anderson *More is Different* — a declaration that condensed matter is not an application of particle physics but an autonomous discipline. The philosophy of emergentism is established

**Act III — the age of topological matter (1980–)**:
- **1980 von Klitzing** — the integer quantum Hall effect (quantization accuracy of $\sigma_{xy}$ to $10^{-9}$). Nobel 1985
- 1982 Tsui-Stormer-Laughlin — the fractional quantum Hall (Nobel 1998)
- **1982 TKNN** (Thouless-Kohmoto-Nightingale-den Nijs) — quantum Hall = Chern number. The direct union of topology and physics
- 1988 Haldane — a quantum Hall model without a magnetic field (Chern insulator)
- **2005–07 Kane-Mele, Bernevig-Hughes-Zhang** — topological insulators ($\mathbb Z_2$ classification, protected by time reversal)
- 2008 Fu-Kane-Mele — 3D topological insulators
- 2008– discovery of actual topological matter such as Bi₂Se₃, HgTe quantum Hall films
- **2016 Nobel** — Thouless-Haldane-Kosterlitz (topological phase transitions and matter)
- 2010– Majorana fermion candidates (InSb nanowires), candidates for topological qubits

**Act IV — strong correlation and the unsolved (1986–)**:
- **1986 Bednorz-Müller** — discovery of high-temperature superconductivity (LBCO, $T_c \approx 30$ K). Nobel 1987
- 1987– an explosion of high-temperature superconductors such as YBCO ($T_c \approx 92$ K, above liquid nitrogen), BSCCO
- **the mechanism of high-temperature superconductivity is unsolved** — BCS phonons will not do; magnetically mediated, gauge-fluctuation, and holographic candidates
- 1990s quantum critical phenomena — discovery of the regime where the Fermi liquid breaks down
- **2004 graphene** (Geim-Novoselov) — 2D Dirac fermions, direct observation of Special Relativity effects in condensed matter. Nobel 2010
- 2005– Dirac/Weyl semimetals, high-energy QFT particles appear in condensed matter
- **2018 twisted graphene** — magic-angle superconductivity (Cao-Jarillo-Herrero). A new stage of strong correlation
- **AdS/CFT applications** (2007–) — holographic condensed matter, using General Relativity tools for strong correlation

**Key insight**: condensed matter is not an application of particle physics but, conversely, **an autonomous discipline that inspired particle physics.** **BCS → Higgs, RG → Wilson, Goldstone → Nambu** were all discovered first in condensed matter and imported by particle physics. Every core tool of QFT (12) was verified and refined in condensed matter and flowed into particle physics. **The relationship of the two fields is not reduction but mutual interpenetration** — using the same EFT language at different scales.
