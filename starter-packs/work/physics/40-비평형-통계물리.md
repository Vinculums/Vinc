# Nonequilibrium Statistical Physics

## One-line essence
If Statistical Mechanics is the theory of equilibrium (the partition function), nonequilibrium statistical physics is the field that discovered **exact equalities holding even for processes far from equilibrium** — the Jarzynski (1997)·Crooks (1999) fluctuation theorems — and, through stochastic thermodynamics (Sekimoto·Seifert), which defines heat·work·entropy production **at the level of a single stochastic trajectory**, elevated the second law of Thermodynamics to "an inequality about averages = the shadow of an equality" — binding molecular motors·active matter·Maxwell's demon (information thermodynamics) into a single language.

## Physical picture

**Why nonequilibrium**: the weapons of equilibrium statistical mechanics (partition function·ensembles) are tools for a world where time stands still. Yet living cells·driven colloids·the expanding universe are all out of equilibrium. The 20th century had only two approaches — linear response near equilibrium (Onsager 1931, Kubo 1957), and phenomenology of individual systems. **In the 1990s the fluctuation theorems opened a third path: exact identities that hold no matter how far from equilibrium.**

**The Langevin picture — setting the stage**:
- A Brownian particle: one heavy particle + a heat bath ($10^{23}$ solvent molecules) reduced to **friction (systematic) + white noise (fluctuating)**
- Friction and noise are two faces of the same bath — their ratio is fixed by temperature (the Einstein relation 1905, the prototype of the fluctuation-dissipation theorem)
- Individual trajectories are stochastic (Langevin), while the time evolution of the probability distribution is deterministic (Fokker-Planck) — two expressions of the same physics

**The fluctuation-dissipation theorem (FDT)**: the response (dissipation) when an equilibrium system is gently perturbed is exactly proportional to the spontaneous fluctuations when it is not (Callen-Welton 1951, Kubo). Johnson-Nyquist noise in a resistor is the classic example. **A universal law near equilibrium** — beyond it lies the territory of the fluctuation theorems.

**The Jarzynski equality (1997) — mining equilibrium free energy from nonequilibrium work**:
- Manipulating a system at finite speed (irreversibly), the work $W$ is a random variable differing from trajectory to trajectory
- Yet $\langle e^{-\beta W}\rangle = e^{-\beta\Delta F}$ — **no matter how violently you pull**, the exponential average gives the equilibrium free-energy difference exactly
- Taking Jensen's inequality yields $\langle W\rangle \geq \Delta F$ — the second law is recovered as a corollary. The second law is the shadow of an equality
- Implication: "second-law-violating" trajectories with $W < \Delta F$ must exist for the equality to hold — violations are not forbidden, merely **exponentially rare**

**The Crooks fluctuation theorem (1999)**: the work distributions of the forward and time-reversed protocols are tied by $P_F(W)/P_R(-W) = e^{\beta(W - \Delta F)}$. The point where the two distributions cross is exactly $W = \Delta F$ — **the ruler by which experiments read free energy**. The Jarzynski equality is the integral corollary of Crooks.

**Experimental verification — single-molecule biophysics becomes the laboratory**:
- Pulling RNA hairpins with optical tweezers (Liphardt et al. 2002, the Bustamante group) — recovering folding free energies via the Jarzynski equality
- Collin et al. 2005 — direct verification of the Crooks theorem. Single-molecule experiments, where $k_BT$-scale fluctuations are visible, are the natural stage of the fluctuation theorems

**Stochastic thermodynamics (Seifert 2005, Sekimoto 1998)**:
- Thermodynamics defined not on the ensemble but on **individual stochastic trajectories**: heat $q$, work $w$, and entropy production $\Delta s_{\rm tot}$ along a trajectory
- Trajectory entropy $s(t) = -k_B\ln p(x(t), t)$ — the probability distribution itself evaluated along the trajectory
- The integral fluctuation theorem $\langle e^{-\Delta s_{\rm tot}/k_B}\rangle = 1$ — the mother form of all fluctuation theorems
- The thermodynamic uncertainty relation (TUR, Barato-Seifert 2015): the precision of a current carries an entropy cost — the precision-cost trade-off of biological clocks·molecular motors

**Maxwell's demon and information thermodynamics — information becomes fuel**:
- The demon (1867): a thought experiment violating the second law by observing·sorting molecules
- The Szilard engine (1929): one bit of observation → $k_BT\ln 2$ of work extracted. Landauer (1961): **erasing information** costs at least $k_BT\ln 2$ of heat — the demon's ledger is settled at memory reset (Bennett 1982)
- **Sagawa-Ueda (2008~2010)**: a generalized Jarzynski equality including the measurement mutual information $I$ — the exact identity of thermodynamics with feedback control
- Experiments: Toyabe et al. 2010 (demonstration of information→work conversion), Bérut et al. 2012 (measurement of the Landauer limit). The point where the "It from Bit" of Quantum Information is demonstrated in thermodynamics

**Active matter — matter whose every grain is an engine**:
- Bacteria·the cytoskeleton·bird flocks·self-propelled colloids: the constituents **individually consume energy and propel themselves** — matter in which detailed balance is broken at the microscopic scale
- The Vicsek model (1995): a flocking phase transition from alignment rules + noise alone. Toner-Tu (1995): its hydrodynamic theory — long-range order possible even in 2D (circumventing the equilibrium Mermin-Wagner theorem)
- MIPS (motility-induced phase separation, Cates-Tailleur): even with no attraction whatsoever, the feedback of "accumulating where you slow down" alone produces gas-liquid-type phase separation — a counterexample to equilibrium intuition
- Biophysical touchpoints: the Brownian-ratchet dynamics of molecular motors (kinesin), Hopfield kinetic proofreading (1974) — life purchases accuracy with entropy production

## Mathematical structure

**The Langevin equation** (overdamped, 1D):
$$\gamma\dot x = -\partial_x V(x,\lambda(t)) + \xi(t),\qquad \langle\xi(t)\xi(t')\rangle = 2\gamma k_BT\,\delta(t-t')$$
- $\gamma$: friction coefficient, $\xi$: Gaussian white noise, $\lambda(t)$: external driving protocol
- Noise strength $2\gamma k_BT$ — the binding of friction and fluctuation (the fluctuation-dissipation theorem of the second kind)

**The Einstein relation** (1905):
$$D = \frac{k_BT}{\gamma}$$
The ratio of diffusion (fluctuation) to mobility (response) is temperature — the first instance of the FDT.

**The Fokker-Planck equation** (the distribution representation of Langevin):
$$\partial_t p(x,t) = \partial_x\left[\frac{\partial_x V}{\gamma}\,p + D\,\partial_x p\right] = -\partial_x J(x,t)$$
In steady state, $J = 0$ means equilibrium (the Boltzmann distribution); $J \neq 0$ means a **nonequilibrium steady state (NESS)** — circulating probability currents are the marker of broken detailed balance.

**The fluctuation-dissipation theorem (Kubo form)**:
$$\chi''(\omega) = \frac{\omega}{2k_BT}\,S(\omega)$$
- $\chi''$: the imaginary part of the response function (dissipation), $S(\omega)$: the spectrum of equilibrium correlation functions
- The universal relation of linear response — one family with the Green-Kubo formulas (transport coefficient = integral of a correlation function)

**The Jarzynski equality** (1997):
$$\left\langle e^{-\beta W}\right\rangle = e^{-\beta\Delta F}$$
- The average is over all nonequilibrium trajectories starting from the initial equilibrium distribution
- $\Rightarrow \langle W\rangle \geq \Delta F$ (Jensen) — recovering the second law

**The Crooks fluctuation theorem** (1999):
$$\frac{P_F(W)}{P_R(-W)} = e^{\beta(W - \Delta F)}$$
$P_F$·$P_R$: the work distributions of the forward·time-reversed protocols. Integrating over $W$ yields the Jarzynski equality.

**Trajectory entropy and the integral fluctuation theorem** (Seifert 2005):
$$\Delta s_{\rm tot} = \Delta s_{\rm sys} + \Delta s_{\rm medium},\qquad s_{\rm sys}(t) = -k_B\ln p(x(t),t),\qquad \Delta s_{\rm medium} = \frac{q_{\rm diss}}{T}$$
$$\left\langle e^{-\Delta s_{\rm tot}/k_B}\right\rangle = 1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \langle\Delta s_{\rm tot}\rangle \geq 0$$
The detailed fluctuation theorem $P(-\Delta s_{\rm tot})/P(\Delta s_{\rm tot}) = e^{-\Delta s_{\rm tot}/k_B}$ — the probability of entropy-decreasing trajectories is exponentially suppressed, exactly (the general form of the Evans-Cohen-Morriss 1993·Gallavotti-Cohen 1995 lineage).

**The Sagawa-Ueda generalized Jarzynski** (with feedback, 2010):
$$\left\langle e^{-\beta(W - \Delta F) - I}\right\rangle = 1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \langle W\rangle \geq \Delta F - k_BT\,\langle I\rangle$$
- $I$: the mutual information between measurement outcome and system state (in nats)
- The second-law bound is relaxed by the information $\langle I\rangle$ — the Szilard engine ($\langle I\rangle = \ln 2$ → $k_BT\ln 2$ extracted) is the special case

**The Landauer principle** (1961):
$$Q_{\rm erase} \geq k_BT\ln 2 \quad (\text{per bit erased})$$

**The thermodynamic uncertainty relation (TUR, Barato-Seifert 2015)**:
$$\frac{\mathrm{Var}(J)}{\langle J\rangle^2} \geq \frac{2k_B}{\Sigma}$$
- $J$: an integrated current (transported quantity·revolutions, etc.), $\Sigma$: total entropy production
- A precise current (small relative variance) requires large dissipation — the fundamental efficiency limit of molecular machines

**The active Brownian particle (ABP)** — the minimal active-matter model:
$$\dot{\vec r} = v_0\,\hat n(\theta) + \sqrt{2D_t}\,\vec\xi,\qquad \dot\theta = \sqrt{2D_r}\,\eta$$
- $v_0$: self-propulsion speed — this term breaks detailed balance (the equilibrium limit is $v_0 \to 0$)
- Persistence length $\ell_p = v_0/D_r$. With a density-dependent slowdown feedback $v(\rho)$, MIPS follows

**Vicsek/Toner-Tu**: an XY-like model of alignment interactions + noise, but with **motion coupled in**, evading the equilibrium Mermin-Wagner theorem — 2D long-range polar order. A universality class unique to nonequilibrium.

**The KPZ equation** (Kardar-Parisi-Zhang 1986) — the flagship of nonequilibrium universality:
$$\partial_t h = \nu\,\nabla^2 h + \frac{\lambda}{2}(\nabla h)^2 + \eta(x,t)$$
- The height $h(x,t)$ of a growing interface: the nonlinear term $(\nabla h)^2$ takes it out of the equilibrium (Edwards-Wilkinson) class
- Exact 1D exponents: roughness $\alpha = 1/2$, growth $\beta = 1/3$, dynamic exponent $z = 3/2$ — the fluctuation distribution converges to the Tracy-Widom distribution of random matrix theory (confirmed by liquid-crystal experiments·exact solutions in 2010)
- The "hydrogen atom of nonequilibrium critical phenomena," corresponding to the equilibrium Ising class

## Why it matters

**Elevating the status of the second law — from inequality to equality**:
- The second law $\Delta S \geq 0$ of Thermodynamics is the **averaged shadow** of the fluctuation theorems
- "Violations" are not forbidden but exponentially rare events — the microscopic (violations observable) and the macroscopic (effectively an absolute law) become continuous through a single equality
- The modern resolution of the Loschmidt reversibility paradox (the Boltzmann H-theorem controversy of Statistical Mechanics): time-reversal symmetry lives on at the microscale, and irreversibility is quantified as the exponential asymmetry of trajectory probabilities

**Measurable thermodynamics — tools at the single-molecule scale**:
- Jarzynski·Crooks are the **measurement protocols** for protein·RNA folding free energies (irreversible pulling is allowed)
- The TUR lets performance limits of molecular motors·biological clocks be estimated directly from data (inferring entropy production)

**The fusion of information and physics — information thermodynamics**:
- With Landauer·Sagawa-Ueda, "information is physical" is established at the level of theorems
- Extended to Quantum Information entanglement entropy·quantum fluctuation theorems (two-point measurement) — the theoretical basis of quantum heat engines·quantum batteries
- The fundamental energy limits of computing (the physical basis of reversible-computing discussions)

**The theoretical language of biological physics**:
- Life = a dissipative structure maintaining a nonequilibrium steady state — stochastic thermodynamics is its ledger
- The energy costs of kinetic proofreading·adaptation·sensing are all quantified as entropy production
- Active matter is the unified hydrodynamics of the cytoskeleton·tissues·swarms — "the materials physics of living matter"

**Nonequilibrium universality — rebuilding statistical mechanics beyond equilibrium**:
- Nonequilibrium classes corresponding to the equilibrium universality classes (Wilson RG): KPZ interface growth, directed percolation, the Vicsek class
- There is still no general variational principle for NESS — "the partition function of nonequilibrium" is the holy grail of the field

**Unresolved problems**:
- A general theory of nonequilibrium steady states (an object corresponding to the equilibrium $Z$)
- A thermodynamically consistent formulation of fluctuation theorems for active matter (the limits of the effective-temperature concept)
- Quantifying FDT violations in glassy·aging systems
- Testing the hypotheses of the origin of life and dissipative adaptation
- Quantum nonequilibrium — integration with thermalization·ETH·many-body localization (the nonequilibrium quantum dynamics of Condensed Matter)

## Historical context

**Act 1 — Brownian motion and linear response (1905~1957)**:
- 1905 Einstein — Brownian motion·the diffusion-mobility relation (proving molecular reality)
- 1908 Langevin — introducing the stochastic differential equation (an "infinitely simpler" derivation)
- 1917 Smoluchowski·1931 Kramers lineage — Fokker-Planck·escape-rate theory
- **1931 Onsager** — the reciprocal relations (Nobel 1968): the first universal law of linear nonequilibrium
- 1928 Johnson-Nyquist — thermal noise; 1951 Callen-Welton — formalizing the FDT
- 1957 Kubo — completing linear-response theory (the Green-Kubo formulas)
- **Decisive turning point**: Onsager-Kubo — establishing that a universal theory exists up to "near equilibrium." Beyond that, a void

**Act 2 — the discovery of the fluctuation theorems (1993~2005)**:
- 1993 Evans-Cohen-Morriss — discovering a fluctuation relation in sheared-fluid simulations
- 1995 Gallavotti-Cohen — formalized as a theorem under the chaotic hypothesis
- **1997 Jarzynski** — the nonequilibrium work equality (PRL 78, 2690)
- **1999 Crooks** — the detailed fluctuation theorem (PRE 60, 2721)
- 1998 Sekimoto — stochastic energetics (defining heat·work along trajectories)
- 2002 Liphardt et al. — verifying Jarzynski with RNA hairpins; 2005 Collin et al. — verifying Crooks
- **2005 Seifert** — trajectory entropy·the integral fluctuation theorem (PRL 95, 040602): the unifying skeleton of stochastic thermodynamics
- **Decisive turning point**: Jarzynski 1997 — opening the new genre of "exact equalities far from equilibrium"

**Act 3 — the demonstration of information thermodynamics (1867~2012)**:
- 1867 Maxwell's demon → 1929 the Szilard engine → **1961 the Landauer principle** → 1982 Bennett (complete resolution of the demon)
- **2008~10 Sagawa-Ueda** — fluctuation theorems of measurement·feedback (introducing mutual information)
- 2010 Toyabe et al. — the information→work conversion experiment; 2012 Bérut et al. — measuring the Landauer limit
- **Decisive turning point**: Sagawa-Ueda — a 145-year-old thought experiment absorbed into quantitative thermodynamics

**Act 4 — active matter·precision limits·quantum extensions (1995~)**:
- 1995 the Vicsek model·Toner-Tu hydrodynamics — the flocking phase transition
- 2000s an explosion of self-propelled colloid·bacterial-turbulence experiments
- 2015 Cates-Tailleur — formalizing the MIPS review; active matter enters the soft-matter mainstream
- **2015 Barato-Seifert** — the thermodynamic uncertainty relation (TUR)
- 2010s~ quantum fluctuation theorems·quantum heat-engine experiments (ion traps·NV centers), converging with ETH·many-body localization
- **Core insight**: if 20th-century statistical mechanics was "the completion of equilibrium," nonequilibrium statistical physics after the fluctuation theorems is a reconstruction of thermodynamics encompassing **individual trajectories·information·life**. In that it still has no central object corresponding to the partition function, this field, together with the strong-correlation problem of Topological Matter and Strongly Correlated Systems, forms "the frontier without a theory."
