# Hawking radiation

## One-line essence
Hawking radiation is a direct consequence of the fact that the definition of the vacuum of a quantum field on curved spacetime differs from coordinate system to coordinate system — the Bogoliubov transformation makes one vacuum look like blackbody particles in another coordinate system, the surface gravity of the event horizon defines the temperature $T_H = \hbar c^3/(8\pi GM k_B)$, and the 50-year-unsolved information paradox reaches a partial solution via the RT and island formulas of AdS/CFT.

## Physical picture

**The vacuum of flat spacetime is not absolute — Unruh showed it first (1976)**:
Even in the same flat spacetime, an **accelerated observer** sees the Minkowski vacuum as blackbody radiation. Acceleration $a$ → temperature $T_U = \hbar a/(2\pi c k_B)$. This is Hawking's flat-space cousin. That the identity of the vacuum depends on the coordinate system is the key insight. **Hawking radiation is the curved-spacetime generalization of this fact.**

**Hawking's derivation (1974)** — the quantum-field evolution of a forming BH:
When a star collapses into a BH, define the quantum vacuum of the flat spacetime before collapse and track the field within it as it evolves through the spacetime out to distant infinity. Seen from infinity, the vacuum is not empty but **filled with particles of a blackbody spectrum.** This is Hawking radiation.

**The real mechanism — the Bogoliubov transformation**:
Hawking's own heuristic that "a virtual particle pair splits at the horizon" is **not a wrong picture but it is imprecise.** The exact derivation is:
1. positive-energy modes of the pre-collapse coordinate system = the definition of the "in-vacuum"
2. positive-energy modes of the far-infinity coordinate system after collapse = the definition of the "out-vacuum"
3. compute the Bogoliubov transformation between the modes of the two coordinate systems: $u_k^{\rm out} = \sum_l(\alpha_{kl}u_l^{\rm in} + \beta_{kl}(u_l^{\rm in})^*)$
4. **$\beta_{kl} \neq 0$ is the key** — meaning that the old vacuum is filled with particles of the new coordinate system
5. particle number = $\sum |\beta|^2$ → a blackbody distribution

**Analogy — a quantum-field version of the acoustic Doppler**: the same sound wave has different frequencies for stationary and moving observers. In Hawking radiation, the same quantum field has different vacuum definitions in different spacetime regions (flat, BH exterior) → connected by Bogoliubov → one vacuum looks like particles from the other side.

**Completion of the 4 laws of black hole thermodynamics** (Bardeen-Carter-Hawking 1972 + Hawking 1974):
| Law | Ordinary thermodynamics | BH |
|---|---|---|
| 0th law | T constant at equilibrium | surface gravity $\kappa$ constant |
| 1st law | $dU = TdS + \cdots$ | $dM = T_H dS_{BH} + \Omega dJ + \Phi dQ$ |
| 2nd law | $dS \geq 0$ | **generalized 2nd law**: $dS_{\rm matter} + dS_{BH}/4 \geq 0$ |
| 3rd law | $T = 0$ unreachable | $T_H = 0$ ($\kappa = 0$, extremal BH) unreachable |

**The combination of 4 fundamental constants — the deepest consistency of the universe**:
$$T_H = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B},\quad S_{BH} = \frac{k_B A}{4 l_P^2},\quad l_P = \sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}$$
**$\hbar$ (quantum) + $c$ (relativity) + $G$ (gravity) + $k_B$ (heat)** all appear. The BH is the only system where the 4 fields meet simultaneously.

**The information paradox (Hawking 1976)**:
Hawking radiation is **purely blackbody** = the particles are mutually uncorrelated = the information sucked into the BH is nowhere. Contradicts the unitarity of quantum mechanics ($|\psi\rangle \to U|\psi\rangle$). **50 years unsolved.**

**Page curve (Page 1993)** — quantifying the moment of information recovery:
- early: the von Neumann entropy of the Hawking radiation ↑ (entanglement with the BH accumulates)
- **Page time** $t_P \approx t_{\rm evap}/2$: the entropy maximum
- afterward: entropy ↓ (pressed by the limit on the BH side)
- final BH disappearance: entropy 0 (a pure state is recovered)

The naive Hawking calculation gives only monotonic entropy increase — contradicting the Page curve. **The mechanism by which quantum gravity recovers the information is missing.**

**The island formula (2019) — a partial solution 50 years later**:
The RT formula of AdS/CFT + quantum corrections (FLM) + the Quantum Extremal Surface (Engelhardt-Wall) → the **island formula**:
$$S(R) = \min\,\text{ext}\left[\frac{\text{Area}(\partial I)}{4G_N} + S_{\rm bulk}(R \cup I)\right]$$
- $R$: the region where Hawking radiation is collected
- $I$: the island — a region inside the BH but holding the information of $R$ (like a quantum error-correcting code)
- result: **exact reproduction of the Page curve.** It is quantitatively shown that Hawking radiation comes out carrying information

## Mathematical structure

**A free scalar field on curved spacetime**:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{-g}}\partial_\mu(\sqrt{-g}\, g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\nu \phi) - m^2\phi = 0$$

**Quantization** (mode expansion):
$$\hat\phi = \sum_k \left[\hat a_k u_k(x) + \hat a_k^\dagger u_k^*(x)\right]$$
The positive-energy definition of the mode functions $u_k$ is coordinate-system dependent.

**Bogoliubov transformation**:
$$u_k^{\rm new} = \sum_l(\alpha_{kl}u_l^{\rm old} + \beta_{kl}(u_l^{\rm old})^*)$$
$$\hat a_k^{\rm new} = \sum_l(\alpha_{kl}^*\hat a_l^{\rm old} - \beta_{kl}^*(\hat a_l^{\rm old})^\dagger)$$

Orthogonality: $\sum_l(|\alpha_{kl}|^2 - |\beta_{kl}|^2) = 1$

**Expectation value of particle number** (the old vacuum in the new coordinate system):
$$\langle 0_{\rm old}|\hat N_k^{\rm new}|0_{\rm old}\rangle = \sum_l |\beta_{kl}|^2$$

**Unruh effect** (Rindler accelerated coordinates):
$$\langle N_\omega\rangle_{\rm Rindler} = \frac{1}{e^{2\pi c\omega/a} - 1}$$
Acceleration $a$ → temperature $T_U = \hbar a/(2\pi c k_B)$. An exact **blackbody distribution.**

**Hawking's derivation** (Schwarzschild exterior, a forming BH):
The Bogoliubov calculation of modes passed through the redshifting spacetime. The key result:
$$|\beta_\omega|^2 = \frac{\Gamma_\omega}{e^{8\pi GM\omega/\hbar c^3} - 1}$$
- $\Gamma_\omega$: the greybody factor (the probability of passing the horizon)
- the denominator = the blackbody Bose distribution

**Hawking temperature**:
$$\boxed{T_H = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B}}$$

**Generalization by surface gravity**:
$$T_H = \frac{\hbar \kappa}{2\pi c k_B}$$
- $\kappa$: the surface gravity of the event horizon
- Schwarzschild: $\kappa = c^4/(4GM)$

**BH entropy** (Bekenstein-Hawking):
$$S_{BH} = \frac{k_B A}{4 l_P^2} = \frac{k_B c^3 A}{4\hbar G}$$
- $A$: the event-horizon area
- $l_P^2 = \hbar G/c^3$: the Planck area
- Schwarzschild: $A = 16\pi G^2 M^2/c^4$

**Generalization of the 1st law**:
$$dM c^2 = T_H dS_{BH} + \Omega_H dJ + \Phi_H dQ$$
$\Omega_H$: the horizon angular velocity, $\Phi_H$: the horizon potential.

**Evaporation time** (applying Stefan-Boltzmann):
$$\frac{dM}{dt} = -\frac{\hbar c^4}{15360\pi G^2 M^2} \;\Rightarrow\; t_{\rm evap} \sim \frac{G^2 M^3}{\hbar c^4}$$
- a solar-mass BH: $t_{\rm evap} \sim 10^{67}$ years ($10^{57}$ times the age of the universe)
- a primordial BH ($M \sim 10^{12}$ kg): evaporating at the current age of the universe — an observational candidate

**Page curve — the quantitative form** (the von Neumann entropy of the Hawking radiation):
- naive Hawking: $S_R(t)$ increases monotonically (proportional to $t/t_{\rm evap}$)
- Page's prediction: monotonic increase for $t < t_P$, monotonic decrease for $t > t_P$, maximum $S_{BH}/2$
- $t_P$: the Page time, $\approx t_{\rm evap}/2$

**Island formula (QES)**:
$$S(R) = \min_I \text{ext}_I\left[\frac{\text{Area}(\partial I)}{4G_N} + S_{\rm bulk}^{\rm ent}(R \cup I)\right]$$
- early: island $I = \emptyset$ → $S(R) = S_{\rm bulk}(R)$ = the naive Hawking result
- after the Page time: the island appears, $S(R) = A(\partial I)/(4G) + \cdots$ becomes smaller
- result: **exact reproduction of the Page curve**

**Replica wormholes** (Penington-Shenker-Stanford-Yang 2020, AHMS 2020):
When computing $\text{Tr}\,\rho_R^n$, wormhole-topology contributions → derivation of the island formula. The exact microscopic mechanism of information recovery.

## Why it matters

**The most important result of QFT on curved spacetime**:
- before 1974: how quantum fields + curved spacetime meet was not clear
- after 1974: the vacuum is coordinate-system dependent + Bogoliubov quantifies particle emergence → a standard tool

**The most quantitative clue of quantum gravity**:
- any quantum-gravity candidate must quantitatively derive $T_H$, $S_{BH}$, and the Page curve
- string theory: Strominger-Vafa 1996 counted BH microstates via D-branes → exactly reproducing Bekenstein-Hawking
- LQG: similar counting attempts (dependent on the Immirzi parameter)
- AdS/CFT: as seen in 16, the most precise tool

**The combination of 4 fundamental constants**:
$\hbar, c, G, k_B$ all appear in the formulas for $T_H, S_{BH}$. **The deepest consistency of the universe** — a strong constraint that the quantum, relativity, gravity, and thermodynamics must operate simultaneously in one system.

**The information paradox — 50% of the 50-year unsolved problem**:
- 1976–2019: unsolved
- 2019 island formula: exact derivation of the Page curve for AdS BHs → **the information-recovery mechanism quantitatively established in the AdS domain**
- unsolved: generalization to flat-space and dS BHs (the BHs of our universe)

**The change of Hawking's own position**:
- 1976: claimed information loss
- 2004 bet: conceded defeat to Preskill (the possibility of information recovery)
- last paper just before his death in 2018: soft hair (Hawking-Perry-Strominger), information encoded on the BH surface

**Firewall paradox** (AMPS 2012) → the ER=EPR response (2013):
- AMPS: monogamy, the equivalence principle, and locality cannot all three be kept → a firewall at the horizon?
- Maldacena-Susskind: old Hawking radiation entangled with the BH interior = a wormhole → firewall avoided

**Unsolved**:
- **generalization to flat space and dS** — the BHs of our universe are asymptotically flat or dS, not AdS
- the exact role of **Hawking-Perry-Strominger soft hair**
- **the microscopic degrees of freedom of quantum gravity** — D-branes / spin networks / which is real?
- the exact structure of **the black-hole interior spacetime** (firewall? smooth? fuzzball?)

## Historical context

**Act I — the emergence of BH thermodynamics (1971–1975)**:
- 1971 Hawking — the BH area theorem ($dA \geq 0$)
- 1972 Bardeen-Carter-Hawking — the 4 laws of BHs (thermodynamics-like, but unclear whether genuine)
- **1972 Bekenstein** (Wheeler's student) — the hypothesis BH entropy ∝ area. Initially criticized by Hawking
- 1974 Unruh — the accelerated-observer blackbody effect ($T_U = \hbar a/2\pi c k_B$)
- **January 1974 Hawking** — *Black hole explosions?* Nature 248. **The derivation of Hawking radiation.** A result breaking his own 1971 area theorem — Hawking effectively proved Bekenstein's hypothesis
- 1975 Hawking — *Particle creation by black holes* Commun. Math. Phys. 43, 199 — the formal paper
- **The decisive turn**: 1974 — Hawking, having criticized Bekenstein, verified him himself. Proving that the BH is a system where quantum, relativity, heat, and statistics meet in one place

**Act II — the information paradox and the Page-curve prediction (1976–1996)**:
- **1976 Hawking** — *Breakdown of predictability in gravitational collapse* PRD 14. Formal statement of the **information paradox**
- 1976 Bekenstein — the generalized 2nd law ($dS_{\rm matter} + dS_{BH}/4 \geq 0$)
- 1981 Wald — a rigorous derivation of Hawking radiation (the Bogoliubov formulation)
- 1983 Hartle-Hawking — the Euclidean BH, the no-boundary quantum universe
- **1993 Page** — *Information in black hole radiation* PRL 71. The quantitative prediction of the **Page time + Page curve**
- 1996 Strominger-Vafa — BH microstate counting via string-theory D-branes → reproducing Bekenstein-Hawking. The first microscopic derivation
- **The decisive turn**: 1993 Page — a quantitative prediction of what curve there must be if information recovery occurs. Clarifying the target of the following 50 years

**Act III — Firewall and ER=EPR (1997–2018)**:
- **1997 Maldacena AdS/CFT** — unitarity guaranteed in quantum gravity (the CFT is unitary). Suggesting that information recovery is possible in principle
- 2003 't Hooft / Susskind complementarity — what the infalling observer and the external observer see differ, but no contradiction
- 2004 Hawking — conceded the 1997 bet to Preskill (agreeing to information recovery)
- 2009 Mathur — the fuzzball proposal (the BH interior is strings)
- **2012 AMPS Firewall** (Almheiri-Marolf-Polchinski-Sully) — *Black holes: complementarity or firewalls?* — monogamy, the equivalence principle, and locality cannot all three be kept
- **2013 Maldacena-Susskind ER=EPR** — the firewall response: old Hawking radiation entangled with the BH interior = a wormhole
- 2014– the information paradox in earnest via quantum-information tools — bulk reconstruction, code subspace, HaPPY
- 2016 Hawking-Perry-Strominger — soft hair (infinite degrees of freedom on the BH surface)
- **The decisive turn**: 2013 ER=EPR — the information paradox is reformulated as a problem of quantum information and spacetime geometry. The AMPS firewall is partially resolved by ER=EPR

**Act IV — the island formula and the partial solution (2019–)**:
- **2019 Penington** (arXiv:1905.08255), **AEMM** (arXiv:1905.08762) — derivation of the Page curve, the **island formula**
- 2020 Penington-Shenker-Stanford-Yang, AHMS — **replica wormholes**, the exact information-recovery mechanism
- 2020s — the 50-year information paradox partially solved in the AdS domain
- 2024 — it is quantitatively shown that Hawking radiation comes out carrying information (AdS). Generalization to flat and dS unsolved, the most active research
- **The decisive turn**: 2019 the island formula — Hawking's 1976 50-year unsolved problem gets a partial solution via quantum information + AdS/CFT. Quantum gravity goes from conjecture to a **computable discipline** (at least in the AdS domain)

**Key insight**: Hawking radiation **changed identity three times.** First (1974) the discovery of a quantum effect of BHs, second (1976) the epicenter of the 50-year-unsolved information paradox, third (2019) the stage of a partial solution via AdS/CFT + quantum-information tools. The last transformation is the deepest — Hawking himself claimed information loss from 1976 to 2004, then agreed to recovery upon seeing the development of quantum-information tools. A rare case in which the development of a theory changed even one person's conviction.
