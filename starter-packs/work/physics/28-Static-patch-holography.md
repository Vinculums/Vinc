# Static Patch Holography — Observer-Dependent Holography in dS

## One-line essence
The holography of the **static patch** that one *observer* in a dS universe sees inside the cosmological horizon — **AdS global holography ↔ dS patch holography**. The flow of the Banks-Fischler 1999 finite-Hilbert-space hypothesis + Susskind 2021~ full development of static patch holography + **CLPW 2022 dS algebra (type II$_1$, Operator Algebras in Quantum Gravity)** + Witten 2023 static patch boundary algebra + Maldacena / Anninos-Denef-Konstantinidis dS entropy integrates the Swampland dS conjecture, the Soft Hair on Black Holes BMS, and the Bulk reconstruction into a **direct application of quantum gravity to our universe (accelerating expansion, dS)**. **The first instance of an external extension of the full quantum-gravity domain** (the 10th of the domain after the meta-integration) — U-13 stage-5 meta-integration candidate N=1→2 verifiable (external extension of the domain = the second form of meta-integration). A candidate for deriving the dS Page curve (Penington-Witten 2024).

## Physical picture

**What is a static patch?**:
- de Sitter universe: accelerating expansion ($\Lambda > 0$, $H^2 = \Lambda/3$)
- one observer (a free-falling observer following a timelike geodesic)
- **Cosmological horizon** $r = H^{-1}$ — the maximum distance from which the observer can receive light signals
- **Static patch** $\mathcal P$ = the region $r < H^{-1}$ = the observer's causal diamond
- another observer is a *different patch* — **observer-dependent**

**Analogy — a person holding a lantern**:
- a dark universe in endless accelerating expansion
- each observer has their own flashlight (cosmological horizon)
- inside the flashlight beam = the static patch
- the two flashlights never meet — eternally separated

**Key differences AdS vs dS**:
| Item | AdS (16) | dS (28) |
|---|---|---|
| Curvature | negative ($\Lambda < 0$) | positive ($\Lambda > 0$) |
| Boundary | timelike $\mathcal I$ | spacelike $\mathcal I^\pm$ (temporal infinity) |
| Form of holography | global (whole boundary) | patch (each observer) |
| Hilbert space | infinite | **finite** (Banks-Fischler 1999) |
| Observer | independent | dependent |
| Information conservation | automatic (boundary return) | genuinely unsolved |

**Banks-Fischler 1999 — Finite Hilbert space**:
- $S_{\rm dS} = A_{\rm horizon}/4G = 3\pi/(\Lambda G) \approx 10^{122}$

- "the dS universe is a *finite* quantum system"

## Mathematical structure

**de Sitter metric (static patch)**:
$$ds^2 = -(1-H^2 r^2)dt^2 + (1-H^2 r^2)^{-1} dr^2 + r^2 d\Omega^2_{D-2}$$
- $H^2 = \Lambda/3$ ($D = 4$)
- Static patch: $0 \leq r \leq H^{-1}$
- Cosmological horizon $r_c = H^{-1}$

**Hubble entropy** (Gibbons-Hawking 1977):
$$S_{\rm dS} = \frac{A_{\rm horizon}}{4G_N} = \frac{\pi}{G H^2} = \frac{3\pi}{\Lambda G}$$
- our universe: $\Lambda \approx 10^{-122} M_{\rm pl}^4 \Rightarrow S_{\rm dS} \approx 10^{122}$

**Hubble temperature**:
$$T_{\rm dS} = \frac{H}{2\pi} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{3}}$$

**Banks-Fischler bound** (1999):
$$\dim \mathcal H_{\rm static\ patch} \leq e^{S_{\rm dS}} = \exp(3\pi/\Lambda G)$$
- finite dimension — N-bound

**dS observer algebra** (CLPW 2022):
- Bulk fields on the static patch + observer's energy $H_{\rm obs}$
- Crossed product: $\mathcal A \rtimes \mathbb R$
$$\mathcal A_{\mathcal P}^{\rm crossed} = \text{type II}_1 \text{ factor}$$
- finite trace $\tau$, $\tau(1) = 1$
- $\tau(\rho) = \langle \rho \rangle$ for a cyclic separating state

**Generalized entropy in dS**:
$$S_{\rm gen}^{\rm dS}(\rho) = -\tau(\rho \ln \rho)$$
- $= \frac{A(\partial \mathcal P)}{4G_N} + S_{\rm matter}(\rho_{\rm matter})$
- the two terms separate naturally, made precise by the type II$_1$ trace

**Modular Hamiltonian** (dS generalization of Bisognano-Wichmann):
- the cyclic separating state of the static patch = thermofield double-like
- Modular flow = boost along the observer worldline
- $\sigma_t(a) = e^{itH_{\rm boost}} a e^{-itH_{\rm boost}}$

**Static patch + Page curve** (Penington-Witten 2024):
- the static patch is divided into *parts* ($A \cup A^c$)
- $S(A) = S_{\rm gen}(\gamma_A)$ — QES generalization
- Island = the disconnected entanglement wedge inside $A^c$
- dS Page curve = information recovery of the thermal radiation

**Cosmological complementarity** (Susskind):
- the patches $\mathcal P_1, \mathcal P_2$ of two observers $O_1, O_2$
- information does not exist in *both* simultaneously (cloning violation)
- information is *reconstructible* in each patch (viewpoint-dependent)
- $\mathcal H_1 \cong \mathcal H_2$ but the relation is nontrivial

**Static patch entanglement** (Maldacena 2019):
- entanglement between the static patch and the *exterior (triangle region)*
- $S_{\rm patch} = S_{\rm dS}$ (all information entangled with the exterior)
- the dS cousin of the BBM relation

**dS BMS-like algebra**:
- asymptotic symmetry of the cosmological horizon
- generalization of supertranslation + super-rotation
- the dS cousin of the Donnay-Fiorucci-Herfray-Ruzziconi 2020~ BMSW

**Witten 2023 static patch boundary**:
- an algebra on the boundary (observer-independent?)
- distinction of *causal patch* vs *entanglement patch*
- sharpening of the reconstructible region

**Anninos-Denef-Konstantinidis 2014**:
- landscape microscopic counting of dS entropy
- $S_{\rm dS}$ ≈ $\log(\text{\# vacua at } \Lambda)$
- a candidate microscopic origin of the $\Lambda$ of our universe

**TCC bound** (Swampland):
$$\Delta N < \ln(M_{\rm pl}/H)$$

- the *time limit* of static patch holography
