# Probability

## One-line essence
When uncertainty is formalized as a *measure* it is transformed into a decidable quantity, and this dialectic unifies the foundations of gambling, physics, finance, and AI.

## Intuitive picture

Probability is *intuitive*, but its *formalism* came late. It began at the gambling table and answers surfaced in the 17th century, but the *axiomatic definition* arrived only in 1933.

Three scenes:

- **Pascal-Fermat 1654 — gambling becomes mathematics**: when the Chevalier de Méré asked Pascal the *problem of points in dice gambling*, the Pascal-Fermat correspondence followed — the first formalization of the concept of *expected value*. A case of gambling creating mathematics.
- **Kolmogorov 1933 — measure is probability**: an era when probability was not *axiomatic* (the Bertrand paradox, the clash of Borel-Lebesgue intuitions). Kolmogorov took the measure theory of Analysis *as is* and completed the axiomatization of probability. Probability = a measure = 1.
- **Hairer 2014 — solving infinite-dimensional stochastic PDEs**: stochastic PDEs (KPZ, Phi^4, Navier-Stokes, etc.) were *meaningless in the classical sense*, but with Hairer's regularity structures their solutions were consistently defined — verifying that *even uncertainty, once formalized, becomes decidable*.

The meta-insight of this field: **uncertainty measurized = decidable + quantifiable**. A direct application of Analysis §Act 1 Lebesgue measure and a direct bridge to the random matrices of PHYSICS Condensed Matter and Operator algebra in Quantum Gravity — Type III and modular flow.

## Formal structure

### Kolmogorov axiomatization (1933)

**Probability space** $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P)$:
- $\Omega$: the sample space
- $\mathcal{F}$: a $\sigma$-algebra (the collection of events)
- $P : \mathcal{F} \to [0,1]$: a measure, $P(\Omega) = 1$, $\sigma$-countably additive

**Random variable** $X : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ (a measurable function).

**Expected value** $\mathbb{E}[X] = \int X\, dP$ (the Lebesgue integral).

**Conditional expectation** $\mathbb{E}[X | \mathcal{G}]$: the *Radon-Nikodym derivative* over a sub-$\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$.

### Limit theorems

**Law of large numbers** (LLN, Kolmogorov 1930): i.i.d. $X_n$, $\mathbb{E}[X_1] < \infty$ → $\frac{1}{n}\sum X_i \to \mathbb{E}[X_1]$ (a.s.).

**Central limit theorem** (CLT, Lindeberg 1922, Lévy 1925):
$$\frac{S_n - n\mu}{\sigma\sqrt{n}} \xrightarrow{d} N(0,1)$$

→ The answer to *why the normal distribution is natural*.

**Cramér large deviations** (1938): the probability of deviating far from the mean *decays exponentially*. The rate function $I(x)$ is defined.

### Markov chains and stochastic processes

**Markov property**: $P(X_{n+1} | X_n, X_{n-1}, \ldots) = P(X_{n+1} | X_n)$.

**Transition matrix** $P_{ij} = P(X_{n+1} = j | X_n = i)$.

**Ergodic theorem** (Birkhoff 1931): time average = space average (for an ergodic chain).

**Stationary distribution**: $\pi P = \pi$.

### Brownian motion and Itô calculus

**Brownian motion** $B_t$ (Wiener 1923):
- $B_0 = 0$
- Independent increments, stationary
- $B_t - B_s \sim N(0, t-s)$
- Continuous yet differentiable *nowhere*

**Itô integral** (1944): $\int_0^t f(s)\, dB_s$ — a generalization of the Riemann integral (defined even for the non-differentiable Brownian).

**Itô formula**:
$$df(B_t) = f'(B_t)\, dB_t + \frac{1}{2} f''(B_t)\, dt$$

→ The extra $\frac{1}{2} f''$ term is the correction for the divergence of Brownian variation.

**SDE**: $dX_t = \mu(X_t)\, dt + \sigma(X_t)\, dB_t$ — a stochastic differential equation.

### Black-Scholes and mathematical finance (1973)

**Black-Scholes equation** (Black-Scholes 1973, Merton 1973):
$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2 \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S^2} + rS\frac{\partial V}{\partial S} - rV = 0$$

Call-option price: $C = S_0 N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2)$.

→ The *financial* application of Itô calculus. Scholes and Merton, Nobel in economics 1997 (Black died in 1995).

### MCMC and Bayesian methods

**Bayes' theorem**: $P(\theta | D) = \frac{P(D | \theta) P(\theta)}{P(D)}$.

**Metropolis-Hastings** (1953, 1970): a proposal $q(\theta'|\theta)$ + an acceptance ratio.

**Gibbs sampling** (Geman-Geman 1984): sequential conditional distributions.

**Hamiltonian MC** (Duane 1987, Neal 2010): a physics analogy.

→ Bayesian statistics, Stan, PyMC, deep-learning posteriors.

### Hairer regularity structures (2014)

The precise definition of solutions of *singular SPDEs* such as KPZ and Phi^4. Fields 2014.

For PDEs whose *very definition* was meaningless with existing tools (Itô), a consistent definition via *renormalization*.

→ A direct successor to Analysis §Act 4 + deeply motivated by QFT renormalization in Quantum Field Theory.

## Why it matters

- **PHYSICS direct**: Brownian motion (Einstein 1905, Smoluchowski 1906), random matrices (Operator algebra in Quantum Gravity — Type III and modular flow: random matrices vs. ζ zeros), statistical mechanics (a direct application in Statistical Mechanics), the parallel with QFT renormalization.
- **The foundation of mathematical finance**: Black-Scholes, option pricing, VaR, CVaR, Heath-Jarrow-Morton, affine models. The whole finance industry depends on this field.
- **The foundation of AI/ML**: Bayesian inference, variational inference, diffusion models, SGD, deep-learning generalization (PAC-Bayesian) all belong to this field.
- **The foundation of statistical inference**: frequentist (Fisher, Neyman-Pearson), Bayesian (Jeffreys, de Finetti), causal (Pearl, Rubin, Imbens-Angrist).
- **Probabilistic logic and cryptography**: zero-knowledge, secret sharing, and homomorphic encryption rest on probabilistic foundations.
- **Meta-position**: *the formalization of uncertainty* is the foundation of *every applied discipline* — this field is the most powerful instance of Analysis §Act 4 *abstraction = expansion of application*.

## Historical context — R-03 four acts (mathematics variant)

### Act 1 — Birth: Pascal, Bernoulli, Laplace, Borel (1654–1898)

- **1654**: the **Pascal-Fermat** correspondence — the problem of points in gambling (the Méré conjecture). The first formalization of *expected value*
- **1657**: **Huygens**, *De Ratiociniis in Ludo Aleae* — the first probability book
- **1713**: **Jakob Bernoulli**, *Ars Conjectandi* (published posthumously) — the first formalization of the *law of large numbers* (i.i.d.). Bernoulli trials
- **1733**: **de Moivre**, the *binomial → normal-distribution approximation* (a precursor of the CLT)
- **1763**: **Bayes**, *An Essay towards solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances* (published posthumously) — Bayes' theorem
- **1812**: **Laplace**, *Théorie analytique des probabilités* — the formal synthesis of probability theory
- **1837**: **Poisson**, the *Poisson distribution* (rare events) — the Bernoulli limit
- **1846**: **Chebyshev**, the *Chebyshev inequality* — the refinement of the LLN
- **1898**: **Borel**, $\sigma$-additive measure — the first seed of *measure theory* entering probability

→ It began in gambling, astronomy, and demography. Bernoulli's LLN + Laplace's formalization. Borel first recognized the bridge to measure.

**Decisive turn**: Pascal-Fermat 1654 → Borel 1898 — *intuitive probability* meets *measure theory* → the road toward the axiomatization of probability opened.

### Act 2 — Formalization: Kolmogorov, Wiener, Itô (1900–1956)

- **1900**: **Hilbert** 23 problems — No. 6 *the axiomatization of physics and probability* (later answered by Kolmogorov)
- **1900**: **Bachelier**, *Théorie de la spéculation* — a *stock* model via Brownian motion (5 years ahead of Einstein 1905, yet ignored by academia)
- **1905**: **Einstein**, the formalization of **Brownian motion** — direct evidence of molecular motion
- **1906**: the first introduction of the **Markov chain**
- **1909**: **Borel**, the *strong LLN*
- **1922**: **Lindeberg**, the general **CLT** (the Lindeberg condition)
- **1923**: **Wiener**, the **Wiener measure** — the measure-theoretic formalization of Brownian motion (the infinite-dimensional generalization of Lebesgue measure)
- **1925**: **Lévy**, the *Lévy process* — jumps + Brownian
- **1930**: **Kolmogorov**, the general *strong LLN* + Markov processes
- **1931**: **Birkhoff**, the **ergodic theorem** (consistent with Analysis §Act 2)
- **1933**: **Kolmogorov**, *Grundbegriffe der Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung* — **the completion of the axiomatization of probability**. Probability = measure. The answer to Hilbert's No. 6
- **1936**: **Cramér**, *large deviations*
- **1938**: **Khinchin**, *stationary processes*
- **1944**: **Itô**, the **Itô integral** + the **Itô formula** — the calculus of the Brownian
- **1953**: **Doob**, the book *Stochastic Processes* — the synthesis of martingales + stochastic processes
- **1953**: **Metropolis et al.**, the **MCMC algorithm** (the Metropolis algorithm)
- **1956**: **Doob**, *Doob's optional stopping theorem*

→ Hilbert No. 6 → Kolmogorov 1933 is the answer. Wiener and Itô give *continuous-time probability*. The formalization of Markov + martingales + stochastic processes.

**Decisive turn**: Kolmogorov 1933 — the axiomatization *probability = measure = 1* → probability was formally enrolled as a *mathematical discipline*. Itô 1944, with *the calculus of the Brownian*, formed the application bridge to SDEs, finance, and physics.

### Act 3 — Verification/extension: Itô calculus, Black-Scholes, MCMC, random matrices (1956–1990s)

- **1957**: the **Markov decision process** (Bellman, Howard) — the foundation of reinforcement learning
- **1965**: **Mandelbrot**, the *Mandelbrot distribution* (Pareto, heavy tail) — non-Gaussian finance
- **1968**: the **Lévy flight** — jumps + drift
- **1969**: **Strassen**, the *invariance principle* — the refinement of Donsker's theorem
- **1970**: **Hastings**, the *Metropolis-Hastings* generalization
- **1973**: **Black-Scholes-Merton**, the **Black-Scholes equation** — the formalization of option pricing. Nobel in economics 1997
- **1974**: **Wigner-Dyson-Mehta**, the refinement of **random matrix theory (RMT)** — the GOE, GUE, GSE classification
- **1980s–**: the **refinement of the Hilbert-Pólya conjecture** — RH ↔ random matrix spectrum (Montgomery 1973, Odlyzko 1987)
- **1984**: **Geman-Geman**, **Gibbs sampling** (started in image restoration, the standard of Bayesian statistics)
- **1987**: **Duane**, **Hamiltonian MC** — Bayesian methods using the physics analogy
- **1990**: **Pearl**, *causal inference* — Bayesian networks, do-calculus
- **1995**: the *subsequent* proof of **Doeblin** (discovered after remaining unpublished since 1940) — the refinement of LLN and CLT

→ Itô + Black-Scholes for financial application. RMT directly links PHYSICS and number theory. MCMC and Bayesian methods become the statistical standard. Pearl's causal inference generalizes statistics.

**Decisive turn**: Black-Scholes 1973 + RMT 1970s — the *Itô abstraction* is applied to *finance + physics* simultaneously → probability theory settles as the standard tool of the applied disciplines.

### Act 4 — Reinterpretation/application: rough paths, regularity structures, deep learning, causal (1998–present)

- **1998**: **Lyons**, **rough paths theory** — the *differentiable extension* of the Brownian
- **2000s**: **Voiculescu**, **free probability** — the noncommutative generalization of random matrices (cited in Analysis §Act 4)
- **2007**: **Hairer**, the *PDE with random forcing* series
- **2014**: **Hairer**, **regularity structures** — the general solution of singular SPDEs. Fields 2014
- **2014–**: the **Bismut-Lebeau** formalism + Witten deformation — probability + geometry (heat kernels)
- **2015–**: **diffusion models** (Sohl-Dickstein, Song, Ho) — stochastic generative models + AI directly
- **2018–**: the **industrialization of causal inference** (Pearl, Rubin, Imbens Nobel in economics 2021)
- **2020–**: **score-based models, flow matching, rectified flow** — the generalization of diffusion models
- **2022**: **Hairer-Friz**, the *general rough path*
- **Present**: **Stochastic Localization** (Eldan, Klartag, Lee-Vempala) — the basis of high-dimensional sampling

→ Hairer's regularity structures become the *general tool of singular SPDEs*. Diffusion models explode in AI applications. Causal inference industrializes. Free probability unifies quantum information and random matrices.

**Decisive turn**: Hairer 2014 + diffusion models 2015– — *abstract probability* (regularity structures) expands into the *AI industry* (diffusion models) → probability theory becomes the three-dimensional integrating tool of *mathematics + physics + AI*.

### Core insight

The four acts of probability-statistics are the verification that *formalizing uncertainty as a measure makes it quantifiable*. The flow: the Pascal-Fermat intuition → the Borel measure bridge → the Kolmogorov 1933 axiomatization → the application explosion of Itô 1944 + Black-Scholes 1973 + Hairer 2014.

The meta-mechanism of this field is the *direct successor* of Analysis §Acts 1–2 *Lebesgue measure → functional analysis* — the measure goes from functions → probability, the norm from distributions → estimation. But this field adds the intrinsic component of *uncertainty* — *the dialectic of undecidability and quantifiability* is unique to it. A direct bridge to PHYSICS statistical mechanics, random matrices, and QFT renormalization.
