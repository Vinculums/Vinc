## Mathematical structure

**Prospect Theory** (Kahneman-Tversky 1979·1992 cumulative):
$$V(x) = \begin{cases} x^{\alpha} & x \geq 0 \\ -\lambda \cdot (-x)^{\beta} & x < 0 \end{cases}$$
- $\alpha = \beta \approx 0.88$, $\lambda \approx 2.25$ (loss aversion)
- yet *cultural gaps*: Asia $\lambda \approx 1.5$·the West $\lambda \approx 2.25$ (Wang-Rieger-Hens 2017)

**Probability weighting function** (Tversky-Kahneman 1992):
$$\pi(p) = \frac{p^\gamma}{(p^\gamma + (1-p)^\gamma)^{1/\gamma}}, \quad \gamma \approx 0.65$$
- *overweighting small probabilities* (lotteries·terrorism)·*underweighting large probabilities* (safety)

**Hyperbolic Discount** (Laibson 1997):
$$U(c_t) = u(c_0) + \beta \sum_{t=1}^\infty \delta^t u(c_t), \quad \beta \approx 0.7, \delta \approx 0.99$$
- $\beta$ is the *present bias* — discounting future value *non-exponentially*

**Replication-crisis statistics** (Camerer et al. 2016):
- effect sizes on average *50% of the original studies* (attenuation)
- correcting for $p$-hacking + publication bias, *the true effects are estimated to be even smaller*

**WEIRD statistics** (Henrich 2010·Apicella-Norenzayan-Henrich 2020):
- 96% of behavioral-economics samples = WEIRD
- 12% of the world population
- Average Ultimatum-game offers: WEIRD 48% / non-WEIRD dispersed 26~58%

**LLM behavioral imitation** (Mei et al. 2024 *PNAS*):
- GPT-3.5 vs 25K humans compared
- domains of agreement: Ultimatum·dictator·trust (rationally similar to human behavior)
- disagreement: dilemma·long-term decision (LLMs excessively *rational*)

**RLHF formula** (Christiano 2017·Ouyang 2022):
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{RLHF}} = \mathbb{E}_{(x, y_w, y_l)} [\log \sigma(r(x, y_w) - r(x, y_l))]$$
- human preference pairs $(y_w, y_l)$ → reward model $r$ → policy learning
- *a quantitative tool for learning human values* — yet limited by *fatigue·bias·culture*

**Constitutional AI** (Anthropic 2022·Bai et al.):
$$\text{CAI} = \text{RLHF} + \text{AI feedback (self-critique by constitution)}$$
- human dependence ↓ + consistency ↑
